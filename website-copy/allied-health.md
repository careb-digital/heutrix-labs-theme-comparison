# Allied health

## Make one recurring admin workflow easier to see, manage and hand over.

Heutrix works with selected Australian allied health practices where referral, intake, reporting or document work crosses people and systems but still depends on inboxes, spreadsheets, memory or repeated status checks.

The aim is one bounded non-clinical workflow with clearer ownership, visible status and next action, agreed testing, practical staff guidance and a maintainable handover—not a replacement practice-management platform.

## Who this is for

Allied health is a secondary Heutrix launch segment. A suitable practice usually has:

- a recurring intake, referral, reporting, evidence or document workflow;
- multiple people, roles or systems touching the work;
- unclear ownership, inconsistent handover or poor management visibility;
- a workflow owner and decision-maker available;
- relevant staff able to participate in discovery, testing and training; and
- a problem important enough to fund as a fixed-scope improvement.

## Common allied health workflow problems

- Enquiries and intake follow-up handled differently by different staff
- Referral status and next actions spread across inboxes, spreadsheets and practice notes
- Reports, letters or administrative requests hard to track from request to review
- Documents chased through inbox searches and informal reminders
- Clinician and admin handovers varying by person or location
- Staff onboarding steps lacking clear ownership and completion visibility
- Managers unable to see work in progress, overdue items or bottlenecks
- AI tools being used without approved information boundaries or review rules

## Illustrative examples

**These are hypothetical workflow states, not client results or promised outcomes.**

### Referral follow-up

**Current-state pattern:** Referral status depends on inbox searches, staff memory and a shared tracker that is not consistently updated.

**Possible future-state pattern:** One agreed view shows owner, status, missing information, next action and due point for the bounded referral workflow.

### Reporting preparation

**Current-state pattern:** Requests, inputs, drafts and reviews are spread across different places, making completion difficult to see.

**Possible future-state pattern:** Agreed stages, owners and review points create a shared view of what is waiting, blocked or ready.

### Document collection

**Current-state pattern:** Required documents are chased manually and missing items are discovered late.

**Possible future-state pattern:** A scoped checklist shows what is required, what has arrived, who owns follow-up and when it is due.
