# See where Heutrix can help

## Bring one workflow that should not be this hard.

Tell us what is causing frustration. In a 20-minute, no-obligation workflow fit call, we will understand one operational problem, assess whether Heutrix is the right fit and agree the smallest useful next step.

You do not need a process map, technical brief, screenshots or proposed solution. A general, non-sensitive description is enough.

**Call details:** 20 minutes • No obligation • One problem at a time • No sensitive information

## What we will cover

1. What starts the workflow and which roles or teams are involved.
2. Where ownership, status, handover, follow-up or reporting becomes difficult.
3. What a useful operating result would look like.
4. Whether the smallest useful next step is Heutrix Diagnostics, Heutrix Workflow Transformation, Heutrix AI Guardrails, a later review or no project.

This is a fit assessment, not an unpaid workflow audit, consulting workshop or technical design session.

## Privacy note before submitting

Do not include patient, participant, client, clinical, Medicare, NDIS, credential, worker-sensitive or other personal or sensitive information.

Do not upload records, screenshots, forms, reports, passwords, credentials or confidential documents through the public request path. Heutrix will agree an appropriate project-specific process before any additional information is considered.

## What happens next

1. **Share the problem:** Submit a general, non-sensitive description through the tested lead path.
2. **Heutrix reviews it:** We respond within two business days with scheduling details, one concise high-level question or an honest no-fit response.
3. **Have the call:** In 20 minutes, we focus on the problem, fit and smallest useful next step.
4. **Leave with a direction:** The outcome may be a paid product, a later review, limited information needed or a no-fit/no-build recommendation.
