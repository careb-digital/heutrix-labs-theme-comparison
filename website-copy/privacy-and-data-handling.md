# Privacy and data handling

Heutrix Labs works with disability support providers and allied health practices where privacy, confidentiality and careful information handling are part of everyday operations.

Our project approach starts with data minimisation: use the least information needed, prefer de-identified or sample data where practical, define access and storage boundaries, and agree review and retention steps.

## Information we ask you not to send through public forms

Do not send the following through website forms, initial email enquiries or other unsecured channels. A general description of the workflow is enough to begin a conversation.

- Patient information
- Participant information
- Client records
- Clinical notes
- Medicare details
- NDIS numbers or sensitive NDIS-related records
- Diagnostic information
- Health information
- Staff-sensitive information
- Passwords or credentials
- Confidential business records not required for initial enquiry

## Information for the workflow fit call

The 20-minute, no-obligation call needs only a general description of the operational problem, the roles involved, the tools used at a high level and the outcome you want. Do not upload or send records, screenshots, credentials or examples containing personal or sensitive information.

Information submitted through the request form is used to assess whether Heutrix may be able to help, arrange the call and provide its direct follow-up. Any optional ongoing resource emails require separate permission.

The live collection notice must identify the actual receiving entity, contact method, lead destination, access, retention and any service providers before the form is published. This copy is an operational summary and does not replace that approved notice.

## Resource downloads

The complete public guides and workbooks are available without an email address. Downloading a resource does not give Heutrix permission to contact you.

If you separately request a workflow fit call from the resources page, the request may record that high-level source context so Heutrix can understand the journey. It must be handled as part of the approved lead record and not treated as proof that you completed, applied or approved the resource.

Do not upload or send a completed workbook. Use general, synthetic or appropriately de-identified information in any working copy. Download measurement must remain inactive until the analytics, consent and privacy approach is approved and tested.

## How we approach workflow projects

Before using live information, the proposed project process must clarify the purpose, minimum data required, approved tools, user access, review responsibilities and what happens to project data after handover.

- The purpose of the workflow and minimum information required
- Whether de-identified, synthetic or sample data can be used
- Who needs access and what each role can do
- Where information is stored and processed
- Which tools and vendors are approved
- Whether AI, automation or external integrations are involved
- What human review and approval steps are required
- What must be retained, archived, returned or deleted

## AI and sensitive information

Do not enter personal or sensitive information into publicly available AI tools. Any proposed AI use involving personal information needs organisation-level due diligence and an approved process.

The process should address purpose, necessity, tool suitability, access, storage, retention, permissions and accountable human review.

## Client responsibility

Each client remains responsible for determining its privacy obligations and approving collection, use, disclosure, access, storage, retention and tool-use decisions within its organisation.

## Heutrix project controls

Heutrix must use only approved collaboration, storage, credential, communication and delivery systems. Project access should be least-privilege and time-bounded. Storage, transfer, retention, deletion, incident handling and access removal must be stated in the project-specific scope or data schedule before real client information is accepted.

These controls remain subject to owner approval and legal review. This page does not claim that a particular tool, storage location, retention period, insurance position or cross-border arrangement has already been approved.
