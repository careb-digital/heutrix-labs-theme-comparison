## Copy conventions

- Use Australian English.
- Keep Heutrix Labs positioned as an operational workflow implementation partner.
- Do not imply clinical, legal, audit, registration, privacy-advisory or regulatory services.
- Do not promise compliance, efficiency, audit or registration outcomes.
- Keep Safe AI guidance tied to approved use cases, information boundaries and accountable human review.
- Initial enquiry copy must ask users not to include personal or sensitive information.
