# Resources

**Eyebrow:** Free, ungated starter resources

## Work through the problem before you buy anything.

These practical resources help Australian disability support providers—and selected allied health practices—prioritise one workflow, make enquiry-to-service-start work visible or put first AI-use boundaries in place.

You can download the complete guides and working files without submitting an email address. Each resource is useful on its own and explains when a template is no longer enough.

## Choose the resource that matches the decision.

Start with the operational problem. Do not choose a technology or Heutrix product before the workflow, information boundary and accountable owner are clear enough.

### 20-Minute Workflow Bottleneck Scorecard

**Audience:** Best first resource for disability-provider operations leaders.

Compare up to 12 recurring non-clinical workflows using frequency, staff effort, handoffs, delay, rework, visibility, feasibility and evidence confidence.

**Useful result:** One evidence-backed workflow to examine next, plus any control issue that must be resolved before change.

**Downloads:** PDF guide and formula-driven workbook.

**Primary CTA:** Download the PDF guide

**Secondary CTA:** Download the workbook

**Paid bridge:** If the priority or feasible option is still unclear, Heutrix Diagnostics may be useful. If one bounded workflow is already clear, a Workflow Transformation scope may be worth discussing.

### Enquiry-to-Service-Start Visibility Starter Kit

**Audience:** Disability-provider-first, adaptable for allied health referral workflows.

Create a minimum operational view of stage, status, owner, next action, due date, exception and readiness decision without turning the tracker into a duplicate client record.

**Useful result:** Synthetic testing of normal, waiting, overdue, exception and closure paths before choosing an approved system.

**Downloads:** PDF field guide and working tracker.

**Primary CTA:** Download the PDF field guide

**Secondary CTA:** Download the working tracker

**Paid bridge:** If the template exposes recurring integration, permission, handover or adoption gaps, Heutrix Workflow Transformation may be the relevant paid route.

### AI Guardrails Staff Starter Pack

**Audience:** Teams already considering or using AI for administrative work.

Screen proposed use cases, set information and decision boundaries, name accountable human review, and record conditions, escalation and review dates.

**Useful result:** A usable starting path while actual tool and use-case approval remains with the organisation.

**Downloads:** PDF staff guide and use-case register workbook.

**Primary CTA:** Download the PDF staff guide

**Secondary CTA:** Download the use-case register workbook

**Paid bridge:** If use is already widespread or the rules are difficult to apply in practice, Heutrix AI Guardrails may help turn the starter controls into an organisation-specific operating system.

## Download and information boundaries

- No email address is required for the downloads.
- Use general, synthetic or appropriately de-identified examples.
- Do not enter participant, patient, worker, clinical, credential or other personal or sensitive information into these files or an unapproved system.
- The resources are general operational starter templates, not legal, privacy, clinical, employment, audit, registration, regulatory or compliance advice or assurance.
- A score, status or screening result is not an organisational approval, project scope or guarantee.
