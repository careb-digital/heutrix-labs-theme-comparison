## Ten prompts

Run one prompt per outcome-focused chat. The heading provides a suggested chat title. Each prompt uses this Project's instructions and sources. Complete dependent owner decisions before treating the resulting drafts as approved or live.

### 06-01 | Reconcile current marketing facts

Compare current page copy, shared release copy, campaign rules and proof registers. Identify stale CTA, brand, founder and permission statements using dated decisions. Return precise proposed replacements and unresolved owner choices; do not reopen approvals already granted.

### 06-02 | Select the first three proof stories

Choose three existing approved anonymised cases most relevant to intake, reporting and document workflows. Explain the audience/problem fit, permitted claims, evidence limitations and product route. Preserve the four-provider context and do not imply separate client wins.

### 06-03 | Strengthen one case study

Using one supplied approved case and its evidence-map entry, refine the narrative into problem, delivered workflow, operating effect and limitations. Retain approximate wording and distinguish client feedback from measured outcomes. Do not add results or identifying details.

### 06-04 | Finish founder trust copy

Draft a compact founder section using only the September 5 approved names, contribution summaries and origin. Compare it with existing page placement. Produce separate optional questions for portraits or expanded biographies rather than blocking use of approved text.

### 06-05 | Refresh the first twelve posts

Review the first four activation-relative weeks against current offers, approved proof and request-mode CTA. Revise only outdated or weak posts. Preserve a useful standalone lesson, one relevant next step and an explicit list of activation dependencies.

### 06-06 | Plan campaign activation

Create a first-four-week publishing checklist covering publisher, activation date, approved copy, working lead route, response coverage and evidence limits. Use relative days until a date is confirmed. Treat weeks 5–18 as a content bank to adapt from results.

### 06-07 | Improve resource-to-conversation links

Map each of the three resources to its buyer problem, relevant page, optional discussion prompt and fit-call route. Keep resources ungated and result sharing optional. Do not ask for completed workbooks or infer contact permission from downloads.

### 06-08 | Specify resource quality review

Use the documented AI Guardrails blank-answer concern to plan workbook/interactive parity checks. Include incomplete answers, control overrides, heuristic labels and guide wording. Define how any verified fix would require versioning, review and replacement of distribution copies.

### 06-09 | Draft a small outreach test

Prepare three workflow-specific message drafts for approved disability-provider prospects, each using at most one relevant resource or case. Include a message-testing plan and no-fit learning fields. Do not send messages or begin scheduling before operational readiness.

### 06-10 | Define meaningful marketing measurement

Draft a privacy-aware measurement brief separating page views, resource use, volunteered resource context, qualified calls and paid work. Show denominators and attribution limits. Start with manual evidence where feasible; do not activate tracking or treat downloads as consent.
