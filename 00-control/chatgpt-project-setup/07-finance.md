## Ten prompts

Run one prompt per outcome-focused chat. The heading provides a suggested chat title. Each prompt uses this Project's instructions and sources. Complete dependent owner decisions before treating the resulting drafts as approved or live.

### 07-01 | Audit the current model's inputs

Inspect the supplied integrated workbook and identify source, date, unit, currency, tax treatment and approval status for each material assumption. Separate owner-supplied inputs from estimates. Produce an assumption register and prioritise the five inputs that most affect decisions.

### 07-02 | Verify workbook integrity

Check the current integrated workbook's formulas, links, cached results and scenario logic using an appropriate recalculation method. Identify material errors and untested areas. Do not use the old pricing-report audit as evidence for this different workbook.

### 07-03 | Stress-test delivery effort

Model base, 25% and 50% additional consultant/developer hours for each offer using the current workbook. Show direct margin, sensitivity and scope implications. Compare against proposed target/floor rules while making their unapproved status explicit.

### 07-04 | Draft private quoting rules

Prepare an owner-reviewable pricing policy covering loaded cost, non-labour cost, effort allowance, target/floor margin, pass-throughs, payment stages and exception authority. Reconcile the current model reference in PRICING-POLICY. Keep all prices internal.

### 07-05 | Replace budget allowances with evidence

Turn Operating Budget and Tax & Cash assumptions into a quote/actual collection checklist. Prioritise registrations, insurance, legal, software, security and offshore onboarding. Record dates and confidence without inventing vendor quotes or spend approvals.

### 07-06 | Build the cash downside case

Using the integrated model, test delayed first sale, lower win rate, slower collection and delivery overrun. Show minimum cash, timing of funding needs and management actions. Keep tax/GST assumptions explicitly subject to verification.

### 07-07 | Validate support economics

Review the provisional recurring support offer against its modelled hours and obligations. Separate defect correction, included stabilisation and paid maintenance. Produce a scope/effort/value decision memo and sensitivity table rather than treating recurring revenue as secured.

### 07-08 | Measure true acquisition contribution

Create a simple actuals template for sales effort, proposal time, delivery labour, contractors, licences, collection and outstanding defect costs. Define acquisition-adjusted contribution and contribution per founder hour without double-counting overhead or labour.

### 07-09 | Reconcile forecast and capacity

Connect forecast project volumes to Delivery's available hours, review bottlenecks and stabilisation commitments. Identify infeasible months and realistic volume scenarios. State what capacity evidence is missing before recommending commitments.

### 07-10 | Prepare the commercial approval pack

Summarise unresolved costs, rates, scope hours, margins, payment rules and exception decisions for owner review. Attach the relevant sensitivity evidence and acceptance conditions. State exactly what approval would permit for private quoting and what remains unverified.
