## Ten prompts

Run one prompt per outcome-focused chat. The heading provides a suggested chat title. Each prompt uses this Project's instructions and sources. Complete dependent owner decisions before treating the resulting drafts as approved or live.

### 05-01 | Prepare the legal review brief

Use the review register and three-product architecture to draft a concise adviser brief. List documents, unresolved business decisions, contradictory legacy wording and questions on liability, IP, acceptance, defects, data, payment and termination. Preserve do-not-issue status.

### 05-02 | Map contract reconciliation

Compare the supplied current legal masters with the offer specification and company evidence. Produce a clause-level issue table with source, inconsistency, proposed business intent and adviser question. Do not claim to inspect native documents that are not supplied.

### 05-03 | Map actual information flows

Document the proposed enquiry, scheduling, CRM, delivery, AI and closeout data flows. For each show purpose, minimum fields, system, access, transfer, retention and deletion. Keep proposed providers and unapproved offshore access visibly unresolved.

### 05-04 | Draft collection notices from operations

Using the selected real lead flow, draft plain-language consultation and referral collection notices. Address optional assessment summaries and separate permissions. Identify missing facts that prevent publication; do not claim the mock endpoint stores leads.

### 05-05 | Specify an engagement data schedule

Create a reusable data schedule with allowed/prohibited information, client authority, access approval, storage, processing locations, subcontractors, incident contact, retention and deletion evidence. Populate a synthetic example and list unresolved policy decisions.

### 05-06 | Assess offshore delivery readiness

Prepare a decision checklist for the offshore developer scenario in the finance model. Cover task necessity, information access, location, subcontractor terms, least privilege, supervision, transfer and exit. Separate financial assumptions from permission to access client data.

### 05-07 | Run an incident tabletop

Create a synthetic scenario involving sensitive material accidentally sent through an enquiry channel. Walk through containment, restricted handling, escalation, evidence and notification decision points. Flag where current policy or professional advice is needed instead of inventing legal deadlines.

### 05-08 | Reconcile acceptance and IP terms

Translate Delivery's proposed acceptance, stabilisation, defect and handover rules into a business-positions table for legal review. Include reusable methods, client-specific code, third-party limitations and final-payment dependencies. Highlight contradictions before drafting clauses.

### 05-09 | Design document release control

Create a lightweight release checklist linking each native master, rendered export, version, legal review, owner approval and effective date. Include how superseded packs and ZIP files are prevented from accidental issue. Do not reclassify any current draft as approved.

### 05-10 | Assemble the onboarding gate pack

Check whether the first paid engagement could be supported by verified particulars, approved terms, insurance, systems and data procedures. Return evidence present, missing and dependent on owner/adviser decisions, with the smallest action to close each gap.
