## Ten prompts

Run one prompt per outcome-focused chat. The heading provides a suggested chat title. Each prompt uses this Project's instructions and sources. Complete dependent owner decisions before treating the resulting drafts as approved or live.

### 01-01 | Complete the identity evidence plan

Turn IDENTITY-CHECKLIST into a founder action sheet covering legal entity, trading name, ABN, GST, address, signing authority and notices contact. Specify the evidence needed, where each verified fact will be used and which fields must remain unresolved.

### 01-02 | Settle brand and entity naming

Compare current uses of Heutrix, Heutrix Labs and Heutrix Pty Ltd. Draft one proposed naming convention for marketing, invoices, contracts and website footer. Separate branding preference from legal identity verification; identify every affected source.

### 01-03 | Clarify operational accountability

Review ROLE-REGISTER. Replace ambiguous Whole Team accountability with proposed responsibility roles for decision, consultation and backup, leaving people assignments for owner confirmation. Highlight missing coverage and the Sashane/Shashane spelling question without assuming the answer.

### 01-04 | Define enquiry coverage

Prepare an operating rota and absence procedure for enquiry monitoring, fit calls and follow-up. Translate the intended two-business-day commitment into measurable coverage questions, escalation triggers and synthetic checks. Do not mark existing Open roles approved.

### 01-05 | Specify the minimum systems register

Use APPROVED-SYSTEMS to define minimum requirements for email, scheduling, CRM, storage, credentials, contracts and AI tools. Identify what could use an existing system and what needs a decision. Produce approval criteria before recommending vendors.

### 01-06 | Prepare insurance questions

Use INSURANCE-REGISTER and the actual three-product scope to prepare a broker briefing and missing-evidence checklist. Include implementation, subcontractor and offshore scenarios. Do not assert that any policy exists or covers a particular activity.

### 01-07 | Bound founder public profiles

Compare approved founder summaries with the founder trust-layer drafts. Produce safe short bios using only approved facts, then a separate optional fact-and-portrait questionnaire. Distinguish publication permissions already granted from approvals still missing.

### 01-08 | Draft decision authority limits

Prepare a proposed authority matrix for private quotes, discounts, contracts, purchases, production access, incidents and public claims. Use role placeholders, link dependencies to Finance and Legal, and identify decisions that cannot remain assigned to Whole Team.

### 01-09 | Plan founder access onboarding

Create a founder joiner/mover/leaver checklist covering approved accounts, MFA, least privilege, recovery ownership, export access and removal. Use synthetic account labels. Identify systems that need approval before the checklist can be tested.

### 01-10 | Assemble company readiness evidence

Create a compact company-readiness pack from current registers. For each gap show the owner decision or evidence needed and which outreach/onboarding gate it affects. End with the minimum evidence set needed before the first paid engagement.
