## Ten prompts

Run one prompt per outcome-focused chat. The heading provides a suggested chat title. Each prompt uses this Project's instructions and sources. Complete dependent owner decisions before treating the resulting drafts as approved or live.

### 00-01 | Reconcile the current baseline

Compare DECISIONS, VERIFIED-FACTS, LAUNCH-BOARD and RISKS-AND-BLOCKERS with the September 6 LEAD-MAGNETS release record. Produce a correction table showing conflicting wording, controlling evidence, proposed replacement and unresolved questions. Preserve limited founder approvals and distinguish mock submissions from real leads.

### 00-02 | Build the launch critical path

Create a dependency-ordered plan from current open gates. Separate outreach, paid onboarding and website release. For every action specify prerequisite, deliverable, proposed accountable role, decision required and closure evidence. Identify the five actions that unlock the most downstream work.

### 00-03 | Prepare the founder decision meeting

Turn unresolved identity, systems, commercial and delivery questions into a 60-minute meeting pack. Group related decisions, show a recommended option and tradeoff, and provide a decision-record template. Leave unconfirmed names and choices open.

### 00-04 | Design a weekly operating review

Create a one-page weekly review using the launch board and sales/delivery evidence. Cover decisions overdue, blockers, leads awaiting action, capacity, cash and next commitments. Include definitions and an example populated only with clearly labelled synthetic data.

### 00-05 | Map change dependencies

Take a proposed change to the two-business-day response commitment. Identify every affected sales, website, staffing and policy source, questions to settle, required reviewers and evidence of consistency. Produce a reusable change-impact checklist without adopting the change.

### 00-06 | Plan founder rollout

Review COLLABORATION-SETUP and existing local-project paths. Prepare a staged founder rollout with baseline selection, access checks, independent review and two bounded pilot changes. Preserve nested histories and distinguish local preparation from published remote setup.

### 00-07 | Resolve competing project roots

Inventory supplied Heutrix project labels, paths and release sources. Recommend a primary operating root and a treatment for legacy projects and comparison deployments. Produce a keep/retarget/reference-only decision table; do not delete, move or archive anything.

### 00-08 | Establish decision and version hygiene

Design a minimal protocol for recording owner decisions, approved versions and replacement sources across ChatGPT Projects and the repository. Include a source-refresh checklist and a cross-project handoff format. Avoid creating duplicate policy registers.

### 00-09 | Review readiness independently

Challenge a proposed launch-ready declaration against the evidence. Return pass, fail or not evidenced for each critical gate, cite the exact source, and identify the smallest corrective action. Do not count a build, mock confirmation or draft document as business approval.

### 00-10 | Choose the next fortnight's work

Using the reconciled baseline and available founder hours I supply, select a realistic fortnight backlog. Rank by dependency and risk reduction, limit simultaneous work, and define stop conditions. If hours are missing, provide an explicitly provisional allocation.
