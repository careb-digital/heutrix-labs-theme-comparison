# Pricing and scope

## Fixed scope and price before paid work begins.

Heutrix does not publish standard prices for work that has not been scoped. The cost of an engagement depends on the workflow, the decision or operating result required, the people and systems involved, information-handling needs, testing and the handover required.

After the 20-minute workflow fit call, Heutrix will recommend **Heutrix Diagnostics**, **Heutrix Workflow Transformation**, **Heutrix AI Guardrails** or no project. If there is a suitable paid next step, its deliverables, assumptions, dependencies, exclusions, timing, third-party costs, change triggers and fixed price are stated in writing.

No paid work begins until those terms are agreed.

## How each product is priced

| Product | Commercial basis | Best used when |
|---|---|---|
| Heutrix Diagnostics | Paid, fixed-scope decision engagement | The priority, workflow boundary, baseline or feasible solution is not yet clear |
| Heutrix Workflow Transformation | Fixed-scope implementation for one bounded workflow | The workflow, owner, users and desired operating result are clear enough to scope and test |
| Heutrix AI Guardrails | Paid, fixed-scope governance engagement | Workplace AI use needs approved boundaries, accountable review and practical staff guidance |

Heutrix Diagnostics is not free discovery or a compulsory first step. Its findings remain useful if you stop, use them internally or choose another implementer. If you later proceed with Heutrix, approved Diagnostic artefacts can be reused so the same discovery is not repeated; the Diagnostic fee is not automatically credited.

## What affects scope and price?

- The number of workflows, teams, roles, locations or decision-makers involved
- How clearly the current workflow, owner and desired result are defined
- The number and condition of systems, data sources, forms and documents involved
- Access, permissions, licences, vendor constraints and technical feasibility
- Information sensitivity and the approved collection, storage, retention and deletion process
- Forms, trackers, registers, dashboards, automation or lightweight tooling included in scope
- User acceptance testing, review rounds, training and documentation
- Required integrations, migration, data repair or third-party support
- Handover formats, administrator guidance and stabilisation needs
- Remote or approved onsite delivery requirements

## What the written scope confirms

- The exact problem and workflow boundary
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Users, roles, systems and access requirements
- Client responsibilities and decision points
- Assumptions, dependencies and exclusions
- Testing, review, training and handover coverage
- Timing and the conditions that start the delivery window
- Third-party licences, connectors, vendor work, travel or other pass-through costs
- Change-control triggers and approval process
- Fixed price and approved payment terms

## Common scope boundaries

Unless expressly agreed in writing, Heutrix work does not include:

- Legal, privacy, clinical, audit, registration or regulatory advice or outcomes
- Replacement of a core practice, participant, client or case-management platform
- A broad enterprise or multi-workflow transformation
- A promised integration before feasibility, permissions and data handling are assessed
- Unapproved live sensitive-data processing
- Ongoing monitoring, support or enhancement after the agreed stabilisation period
- Promised compliance, revenue, adoption, clinical or fixed time-saving outcomes

## Need help defining a realistic first scope?

Bring a general, non-sensitive description of one recurring workflow, the people involved, the tools used at a high level and what is difficult to track, hand over or report. In 20 minutes, we will assess fit and agree the smallest useful next step.
