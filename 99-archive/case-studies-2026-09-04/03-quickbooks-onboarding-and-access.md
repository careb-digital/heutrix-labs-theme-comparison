# QuickBooks onboarding gained a finish line: access accepted and configured

> **Anonymised founder-delivery experience.** This story is drawn from operational work completed by Heutrix founders in a prior care-provider role, before Heutrix began formal operations. It is not a Heutrix client engagement. The organisation is withheld pending publication approval.

Creating a user in a time-tracking system is easy to mark as complete. The worker may still be unable to use it, assigned to the wrong group or missing the invoice setup that depends on their new identity.

| Starting point | Founder-built intervention | Operating effect |
|---|---|---|
| “User created” was treated as finished | A sequenced QuickBooks onboarding path ending in accepted access, correct grouping and verified invoice setup | The handover ended at usable configuration rather than an invitation email |

## The real constraint

The workflow crossed several small but dependent actions: capture contact details, create the account, send the invitation, allocate the appropriate groups, confirm acceptance and carry the worker identifier into the invoicing workbook.

If any step was assumed rather than checked, the failure appeared later—often when a worker needed to record time or submit an invoice.

## What the founders built

The onboarding process made each transition explicit:

1. capture the minimum worker details required for setup;
2. create the QuickBooks Time user;
3. send the invitation;
4. allocate the approved user groups;
5. verify that the invitation was accepted and onboarding completed; and
6. configure the corresponding invoice tabs, numbers and worker details.

The final checks covered invoice number, invoice date, pay cycle, payee information, business details and bank-deposit information. The workflow also required a check that no unintended pre-populated data remained before the template was protected and released.

## The operating result

The definition of done moved beyond “invite sent”. Administration could follow a consistent sequence from new starter to usable system access and correctly configured invoicing material. The process exposed unfinished setup before it reached a live pay cycle.

## Evidence and limits

The source board contains the complete QuickBooks onboarding and verification path. Supporting workbook artefacts show the linked invoice-tab and invoice-number structure. This case does not claim uninterrupted access, payroll accuracy, faster onboarding or compliance with any employment or screening requirement.

## How Heutrix would deliver this today

Heutrix would confirm the supported platform, licences, account ownership, permission groups and test users in writing. **Workflow Transformation** acceptance would cover invite delivery, accepted access, correct role allocation, failed invitation, reassignment and the downstream invoice handover using synthetic identities first.
