# Hours and kilometres became investigable by client, worker and fortnight

> **Anonymised founder-delivery experience.** This story is drawn from operational work completed by Heutrix founders in a prior care-provider role, before Heutrix began formal operations. It is not a Heutrix client engagement. The organisation is withheld pending publication approval.

A single total can tell accounts that a pay cycle is large. It cannot explain which client, worker or fortnight created the change—or whether hours and kilometres are moving for different reasons.

| Starting point | Founder-built intervention | Operating effect |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle totals hid the operational shape underneath | Separate client, worker and fortnight views for hours and kilometres | Reviewers could ask a specific question without rebuilding a new analysis each time |

## The real constraint

The same measure needed to answer several operating questions:

- What was recorded for each client?
- What did each worker submit?
- How did hours and kilometres change by fortnight?
- Where did the invoice and QuickBooks records diverge?

Trying to answer all of that in one dense table would have made the dashboard harder to use.

## What the founders built

The report separated the investigation paths. Dedicated pages showed hours and kilometres by client, by worker and across fortnightly periods. Additional variance fields compared submitted totals with QuickBooks time and kilometre values.

The model included common dimensions for date, worker, pay cycle, client code and activity. That structure allowed the same filters and definitions to carry across pages instead of creating disconnected calculations for each view.

## The operating result

Reviewers could begin with the question they actually had. A client-level concern, a worker-level discrepancy and a fortnightly trend no longer required three separate manual analyses. The dashboard organised the investigation without replacing the person accountable for interpreting it.

## Evidence and limits

The report package was inspected at page, visual, field and data-entity level. This supports the available views and comparison measures. It does not establish the reason for any real variance, the completeness of source data or a measured improvement in financial control.

## How Heutrix would deliver this today

Heutrix would start with the management decisions the views must support, then document each measure and filter. Acceptance would use synthetic data to verify worker, client and pay-cycle slicing; totals across pages; blank and duplicate records; and access to any sensitive drill-down detail.
