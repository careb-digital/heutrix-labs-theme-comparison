# Pay-cycle reminders arrived with the right link and the right next action

> **Anonymised founder-delivery experience.** This story is drawn from operational work completed by Heutrix founders in a prior care-provider role, before Heutrix began formal operations. It is not a Heutrix client engagement. The organisation is withheld pending publication approval.

A generic “invoices are due” email still leaves every worker to find the correct file, remember the process and decide which cells to change. The founders designed the reminder as part of the workflow rather than as a separate broadcast.

| Starting point | Founder-built intervention | Operating effect |
|---|---|---|
| Submission depended on memory, inbox history and file searching | An end-of-cycle reminder linked each worker to their own controlled invoice workbook | The prompt arrived with a specific action and a known place to complete it |

## The real constraint

The recurring problem was not simply that people forgot. The path from reminder to completed invoice contained unnecessary decisions: which template, which pay-cycle tab, which fields, which submission route and what counted as done.

Automating only the email would have preserved that ambiguity.

## What the founders built

The pay-cycle design connected four components:

1. a configured invoice workbook for each worker;
2. a dedicated cloud link to that workbook;
3. an automated reminder at the end of the pay cycle; and
4. concise guidance on editable and protected fields.

Workers could open the assigned file, complete the relevant pay-cycle sheet and submit it through the defined route. The reminder supported a preconfigured process rather than asking the recipient to reconstruct it.

## The operating result

The workflow reduced the number of decisions between “invoice due” and “invoice ready for accounts”. The link, file and expected action were aligned. Administration gained a repeatable cycle trigger instead of relying entirely on individual follow-up habits.

## Evidence and limits

The process board directly documents the worker-specific links and automated end-of-cycle reminder. The owner confirms that the founders built the solution, but the underlying automation export and delivery logs were not available in the reviewed material. This case therefore does not claim reminder volume, delivery reliability, submission uplift or time saved.

## How Heutrix would deliver this today

Heutrix would confirm the trigger, recipient source, link permissions, wording, failure alert, opt-out or exception handling and accountable owner. Synthetic users would test normal delivery, missing email, inaccessible link, changed worker status and duplicate-send prevention before activation.
