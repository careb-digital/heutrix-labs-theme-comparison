# Source evidence map for founder-delivery case studies

**Status:** Internal editorial working record — not for publication  
**Updated:** 4 September 2026  
**Owner:** Janith  
**Purpose:** Preserve the evidence and claim boundary behind the 15 anonymised case-study angles.

## Source statement

On 4 September 2026, Janith stated that the process documentation, invoicing solution, Power BI dashboards, incident-management forms and note-summarisation automation were built for CareBest by Janith and other Heutrix founders before Heutrix began formal operations.

This is direct owner testimony. It supports attribution as prior founder-delivery experience, but it does not by itself establish permission to name CareBest publicly, publish screenshots, disclose operating data or claim measured outcomes.

## Review method

- The Miro board was reviewed directly in view-only mode through its accessible board outline.
- The SharePoint website required authentication and did not accept the address attempted in the isolated browser session. The locally synchronised CareBest OneDrive/SharePoint library was therefore used to review the available workbooks, Power BI packages and recurring source folders.
- Spreadsheet structure, formulas, validations, named ranges and protection features were inspected without copying live worker or invoice values.
- Power BI report packages were inspected at report-page, visual, field, entity and connection level without extracting operational records.
- Incident PDFs were text-extracted and rendered for visual verification. Completed incident records were not reviewed.
- The digital incident database and shift-note response sheet were inspected only at metadata and header level. Completed rows were not opened or copied.
