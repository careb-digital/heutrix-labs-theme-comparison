# Refer an organisation

Route: `/refer`.

Know a team we could help? Introduce an organisation with a recurring operational problem.

Collect the referrer's name, email, organisation and role; the referred organisation; optional business contact name/email; and a high-level reason for the introduction. Require permission to make the introduction and share any included contact details, confirmation that no sensitive information is included, and permission to contact the referrer about this request.

This is a business introduction to Heutrix, not a participant or patient referral.

Action: **Review my referral**. The next screen shows a reviewable draft and an email handoff, or the configured hosted referral form. It never claims the referral has been received merely because the draft was prepared. An introduction is not confirmation of an engagement.
