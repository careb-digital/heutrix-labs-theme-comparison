DEFERRED is not a launch requirement. Yellow highlighting in Word/PDF and bold labels here make open items visible. [Placeholder] identifies an unanswered field, not a proposed answer.

No actual deadline or decision owner is assigned by this suggested order.

**Record handling:** Use non-sensitive evidence references only. Client particulars, signatures, bank details, policies and confidential legal advice belong in an approved restricted workspace outside Git. An answer is not closed until the authorised decision and affected documents are reconciled. Document 09 contains the legal release criteria; this register tracks the work and does not grant approval.
