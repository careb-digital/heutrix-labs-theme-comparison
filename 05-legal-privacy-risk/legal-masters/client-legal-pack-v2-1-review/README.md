# Heutrix legal launch pack

**Prepared for Monday 14 September 2026. Version 2.1 review draft, 13 September 2026.**

The public website privacy policy and terms are already owner approved and published. This pack reconciles the client documents and supplies document-use guidance and SOPs. **Client documents remain on hold for issue/signature until the specified particulars, commercial, insurance, data and legal approvals are recorded.** No approval or effective date is inferred from the launch deadline.

Start with the [launch readiness memo](Word/00_Heutrix_Launch_Readiness_Memo.docx), use the matrix below to select documents, and complete the [single approval worksheet](Word/09_Heutrix_Approval_Decisions.docx). The [legal review register](../../review-register/LEGAL-REVIEW-REGISTER.md) controls release. [SOPs in readable source form](source/08_Launch_SOPs.md) are available alongside the Word manual.
