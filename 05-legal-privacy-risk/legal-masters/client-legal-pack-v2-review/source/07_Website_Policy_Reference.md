## When to use these policies

Link to them when a person makes an initial enquiry, asks about website privacy, uses a general resource or requests information about the public website. Formspree holds submitted requests and sends notifications to hello@heutrix.com.au. Closing the page or clearing a draft does not delete provider/email copies. Resource downloads do not subscribe a person to marketing, and a form receipt does not book a meeting.

Shashane handles enquiries with Rochelle as backup. Privacy requests use hello@heutrix.com.au. Check the actual provider inbox and quota through SOP 01. Do not claim that a new CRM, calendar, AI tool or client-data system is approved.

## Version boundaries

The old v1 privacy policy, collection notices and website terms are historical drafts and must not be published. The v2 project privacy document is a separate review draft for project-specific arrangements; it does not replace these website policies. The website review waiver does not approve the CSA, NDA, order forms, SOWs or project controls.

If forms, providers, tracking, information uses, retention or contacts change, follow SOP 11, approve the changed notices and preserve a dated release record. Do not change approved public wording through a client-pack reissue.
