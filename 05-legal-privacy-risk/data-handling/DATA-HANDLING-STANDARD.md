**Status:** approval required before real client information is accepted.

**13 September preparation:** The [launch SOPs](../legal-masters/client-legal-pack-v2-2-review/source/08_Launch_SOPs.md) provide proposed procedures for unsolicited information, access, AI, incidents, privacy requests and secure closure. [G5 and G7](../legal-masters/client-legal-pack-v2-2-review/source/09_Approval_Decisions.md) identify system, retention, role and affected-user approvals still required. This addition does not approve a system, retention period or real client handling.

## Default rules

- Collect the minimum information needed for qualification and delivery.
- Do not request participant, patient or worker sensitive detail through the public form or fit call.
- Use synthetic or appropriately de-identified data for demonstrations and initial testing.
- Use only systems listed as approved in `01-company/approved-systems/APPROVED-SYSTEMS.md`.
- Give access on a least-privilege, time-bounded basis and record who approved it.
- Keep credentials in the approved credential manager and never in this repository.
- Record storage location, access, transfer, retention, deletion, backup and incident handling in each statement of work or data schedule.
- Remove Heutrix access and complete agreed retention/deletion actions at closeout.

## Approval questions

- What information may be collected at each stage?
- Where may it be stored and processed?
- Are subcontractor or offshore access scenarios permitted?
- Who handles an incident and how is the client notified?
- What is retained for legal/accounting reasons, for how long and by whom?
- How is deletion evidenced?

This operational standard does not replace legal or privacy advice.
