# Delivery and acceptance standards

**Status:** working standard; owner approval required before onboarding.

## Minimum engagement gates

1. Signed scope and approved terms.
2. Payment/start condition met.
3. Named client workflow owner, decision-maker and users.
4. Approved access and information-handling plan.
5. Written baseline, future state and acceptance criteria.
6. Test evidence using synthetic/de-identified material first.
7. Client user acceptance and recorded decisions.
8. Staff/admin guidance, limitations and handover.
9. Acceptance or documented exceptions.
10. Closeout, access removal, retention/deletion actions and stabilisation start.

## Rules to approve

- response when the decision-maker does not accept or reject by the due date;
- definition and remedy for an in-scope defect;
- number of included refinement rounds;
- change request approval and payment;
- treatment of client-caused delay, pause or restart;
- third-party platform failure and update exclusions;
- rollback or parallel-run approach; and
- exact start and end of any stabilisation period.
