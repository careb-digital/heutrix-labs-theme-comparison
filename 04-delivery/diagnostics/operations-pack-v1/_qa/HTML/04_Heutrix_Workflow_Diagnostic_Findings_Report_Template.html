Option

When it fits

Advantages

Trade-offs / gates

Process and ownership change

Problem is caused by unclear rules, roles or handovers.

Fastest; low technical dependency.

Requires adoption, management follow-through and monitoring.

Configure approved existing tools

Current platform can support fields, status, alerts or views.

Lower maintenance; familiar environment.

Platform limits, permissions and governance still apply.

Low-code workflow

Defined repeatable rules need orchestration or visibility.

Can reduce manual coordination.

Requires ownership, testing, support and change control.

Tailored internal system

Need cannot be met maintainably in approved tools.

Can fit the workflow closely.

Higher cost, delivery risk and ongoing support responsibility.

Controlled AI assistance

A bounded drafting, summarising or extraction use case has suitable data and review.

May assist repetitive knowledge work.
