4. Fit-call outcome messages

Proceed to Diagnostic

Subject: Proposed next step - Workflow Diagnostic for [workflow]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for today's conversation. The proposed scope is one operational workflow: [workflow], starting at [start] and ending at [end]. The main issue described was [problem], and the intended decision is [decision].

This appears suited to the Heutrix Workflow Diagnostic because [fit reasons]. The fixed fee is AUD 950 plus GST. The Diagnostic includes a kickoff and data-handling check, one remote workshop of up to 90 minutes with up to four client participants, limited document review, current- and future-state maps, a prioritised opportunity list, a findings presentation and a concise written report.

It does not include implementation, system configuration, legal or compliance assurance, clinical advice or production access. The default is redacted, de-identified or synthetic material only.

I will send the Client Services Agreement and Workflow Diagnostic Order Form together for review. No paid work begins until they are signed, payment has cleared and the required inputs are received.

Regards,
[Name]
