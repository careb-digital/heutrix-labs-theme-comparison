SALES QUALIFICATION

Fit Call Playbook and Scripts

A structured 30-minute decision call for one operational workflow

Owner

Commercial Lead | assignment to be ratified

Duration

30 minutes | remote

Decision

Proceed / clarify before offer / do not proceed

Data rule

No identifying patient, participant, worker or sensitive information

 

Call outcome: The fit call is successful when both parties can make a clear decision. It is not a free mini-diagnostic, solution design workshop or compliance review.

 

1. Purpose and boundaries

  • Understand the business problem and the one workflow most likely to be in scope.
  • Test whether the Diagnostic can produce a useful decision within its fixed scope and evidence limits.
  • Confirm there is an accountable client owner, suitable participants and usable non-identifying evidence.
  • Explain the AUD 950 plus GST offer, inclusions, exclusions, timing and next step without pressure.
  • Decline or redirect work that requires implementation, formal assurance, regulated advice or unsafe data handling.

Do not diagnose on the call: Ask enough to qualify the engagement. Do not map the full workflow, recommend software, estimate savings, design controls or give compliance conclusions during the fit call.

3. Thirty-minute agenda and core script

Call point

Suggested wording

0-3 min | Open

Thanks for making the time. I will ask about the workflow at a high level, check whether our one-workflow Diagnostic is the right next step, and leave you with a clear decision. Please keep examples general and do not share patient, participant, worker or other identifying information.

3-6 min | Context

Before we focus on the workflow, what prompted you to look at this now, and what would you like to be clearer or more reliable?

6-12 min | Define scope

If we could examine only one workflow, which one would matter most? Where does it start, and what event means it is complete?

12-18 min | Find friction

Where does work wait, get repeated, depend on memory or become hard to see? Who feels the problem, and what evidence do you already have?

18-22 min | People and evidence

Who owns the workflow, who should attend a workshop, and what redacted or de-identified documents could help us understand it?

22-25 min | Safety and boundaries

Would the work require production access, identifiable information, clinical judgement, legal or compliance assurance? If yes, we need to stop and assess a different scope and approval path.

25-28 min | Reflect and decide

What I have heard is [problem], across [start] to [end], owned by [owner], with the main friction at [points]. The Diagnostic appears [fit / potentially fit / not fit] because [reason].

28-30 min | Close

The next step is [send the Diagnostic documents / clarify the listed points / close or refer the enquiry]. I will send a written summary by [date].

 

4. Qualification questions

Problem. What is happening now? Why does it matter? What has changed? What would happen if nothing changed?

Workflow boundary. What triggers the workflow? What is the final output or completion event? Which adjacent process is explicitly outside scope?

Frequency and impact. How often does it run? How many items or cases? Where are delay, rework, missed follow-up or visibility problems observed?

People and ownership. Who owns the end-to-end outcome? Which roles perform or approve steps? Who can confirm facts and consolidate feedback?

Information and systems. What non-identifying information moves through the workflow? Which inboxes, forms, spreadsheets or systems are used?

Evidence. Can the client provide up to five relevant documents or 50 pages in total, redacted, de-identified or synthetic?

Outcome. Is the client seeking a current-state view, prioritised improvements and high-level options, or expecting a configured system?

Authority and timing. Who approves the AUD 950 plus GST spend? Can payment, inputs and workshop participants be available?

7. Common objections and safe responses

Question

Response

Can you just recommend software?

We may identify a system option, but only after understanding the workflow. The Diagnostic can also conclude that a process or ownership change is more useful than new technology.

Can you review whether we are compliant?

No. This offer is an operational workflow diagnostic, not an audit or compliance assurance service. We can identify an operational control or information gap without giving a compliance conclusion.

Can we send participant or patient files?

Not under the default Diagnostic setting. Use synthetic, de-identified or appropriately redacted material only. Any exception requires a separate signed data authorisation before transfer.

Does AUD 950 include the build?

No. It includes the defined workshop, limited document review, maps, opportunity list, presentation and concise report. Any implementation requires a separate scope and agreement.

Will this save us money?

The Diagnostic may identify opportunities, but Heutrix does not guarantee savings or productivity outcomes. Any estimate must be based on client evidence and clearly state its assumptions.

Can AI automate the workflow?

Possibly, but AI is only one option. We first test whether the workflow should be simplified, clarified or tracked. Any client-data AI use requires a defined use case, approved tool, data limit and accountable human review.

 

8. Note-taking standard

  • Record observable statements and attribute material claims to the speaker or source.
  • Use role names, not patient, participant or worker names.
  • Label unverified information as an assumption or point to confirm.
  • Record the workflow start, end, owner, volume/frequency, systems, pain points, evidence and requested outcome.
  • Do not copy unnecessary detail from the client's screens, files or examples.
  • End with the decision, reasons, conditions, owner and due date for the follow-up.
