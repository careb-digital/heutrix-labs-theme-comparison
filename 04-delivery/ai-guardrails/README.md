# Heutrix AI Guardrails

This product is for practical workplace AI governance: appropriate use, information boundaries, accountable human review, escalation and staff guidance.

It is not legal, privacy, clinical or regulatory advice and should not promise compliance. Before launch, define the standard deliverables, timeline, buyer effort, acceptance criteria, update responsibility and boundary between policy guidance and professional advice.

## Refined working definition — 8 September 2026

Use the AI Guardrails section of the [integrated architecture](../../02-market-and-offers/offers/offer-strategy-2026-09/06-integrated-offer-architecture.md) for the proposed core pack and completion test: scoped known-use inventory, assessment criteria, use boundaries, information/human-review rules, approval register/process, reporting and escalation, staff guidance/briefing, and an ongoing owner with review triggers.

Completion should demonstrate the agreed governance process through sponsor review and representative scenarios, with unresolved actions handed over. Technical enforcement, tenant configuration, continuous monitoring and AI-enabled software deployment are not implied. Assess and scope any technical work separately.

Validate coverage, effort, timing, acceptance and upkeep in the [owner validation register](../../02-market-and-offers/offers/offer-strategy-2026-09/05-owner-validation-register.md) before treating this definition as a standard contracted package. The approved name remains Heutrix AI Guardrails.
