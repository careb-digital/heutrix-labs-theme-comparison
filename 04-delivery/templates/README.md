# Delivery templates

Place reusable, approved delivery templates here when they apply across products. Product-specific masters stay in the relevant product folder.

Every template should show its status (`draft`, `approved master` or `superseded`), owner, version, effective date and required approvals. Do not place populated client documents in this repository.

## Brand use

Use the [current Heutrix brand assets](../../06-marketing/brand/README.md) for reusable documents, proposals, presentations and exports. Use the supplied logo artwork rather than a typed substitute; preserve legal names, review states and approved content.
