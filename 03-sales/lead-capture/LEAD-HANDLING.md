## Minimum flow

1. Receive the enquiry in the approved destination.
2. Create or match the contact and organisation in the approved pipeline.
3. Record source, consent, requested workflow, sector, urgency, any voluntarily stated resource context and any safety/data flags.
4. Acknowledge receipt without asking for participant, patient or other sensitive detail.
5. Route to book a 20-minute fit call, request safe missing information, decline or nurture.
6. Record the outcome and next action with an owner and date.

## Lead-magnet pathway

The public lead magnets are ungated. Downloading a guide or workbook does not create a lead, show consent or authorise Heutrix to contact the visitor.

When a visitor separately requests a fit call after using a resource:

- record the resource ID and source only when supplied through the approved attribution path or volunteered by the person;
- record any self-reported result only in high-level, non-sensitive terms;
- do not ask the person to upload or send a completed workbook, screenshot, prompt, tracker or register;
- do not request identifiable participant, patient, worker, clinical, credential, payment or confidential information; and
- route the discussion to Diagnostics, Workflow Transformation, AI Guardrails, more evidence gathering or no project based on fit—not the resource downloaded.

## Release test

- A valid test lead reaches the monitored destination and pipeline.
- Invalid submissions show a useful error and do not create false success.
- Duplicate and spam handling are defined.
- No sensitive information is required in the public form.
- The privacy notice and consent record match the actual handling.
- Owner and backup receive and action the alert within the approved service level.
- Resource attribution is recorded separately from contact permission, and the download path works without a form submission.
