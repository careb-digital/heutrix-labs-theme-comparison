# 8. Post-call path specification

| Path | Entry condition | Deliverable | Timing | CRM action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No fit / no build | One or more hard red gates; paid work not justified. | Brief reason and one useful next move. | Within approved two-business-day application. | Lead status `No Fit`; set one primary and optional secondary reason. |
| Nurture / revisit | Need fits, but timing, owner, budget cycle, access or another prerequisite is not ready. | Agreed trigger/date; at most one approved resource. | Confirm after call; next task on agreed date. | Lead status `Nurture`; set reason and `nurture_review_date`. |
| Information needed | Fit cannot be determined from high-level evidence. | One concise list of non-sensitive facts and why each matters. | Within approved interval. | Lead status `Information Needed`; task with due date. |
| Heutrix Diagnostics | Credible problem; priority, boundary, baseline or feasibility unclear. | Fixed-scope Diagnostics proposal. | Proposal date stated in follow-up; do not invent a turnaround. | Convert/create opportunity; route `Heutrix Diagnostics`. |
| Heutrix Workflow Transformation | Workflow, owner, users, outcome, systems, access and acceptance basis are sufficiently clear. | Limited scoping request, then fixed-scope proposal, Diagnostics or no-go. | Proposal date stated after inputs are confirmed. | Convert/create opportunity; route `Heutrix Workflow Transformation`. |
| Heutrix AI Guardrails | Workplace AI use needs approved boundaries, review and staff guidance. | Limited scoping request, then fixed-scope proposal or no-go. | Proposal date stated after inputs are confirmed. | Convert/create opportunity; route `Heutrix AI Guardrails`. |
| Proposal follow-up | Written commercial scope issued. | Review, question resolution, decision or respectful close. | Mutually agreed review date preferred. | Opportunity stages `Proposal Sent` → `Decision Pending` → `Won/Lost`. |
