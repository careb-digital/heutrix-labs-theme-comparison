# 5. Fit, no-fit and route criteria

## Six qualification gates

Record each as **Green**, **Yellow**, **Red** or **Unknown**, with a one-sentence evidence note.

| Gate | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem fit | Recurring, non-clinical operational workflow in the launch sectors. | Adjacent sector or mixed operational/advisory need that can be separated. | Legal, clinical, regulatory, audit, registration or accountable professional decision-making is central. |
| Boundary | One workflow can be named and bounded. | Priority or boundary needs paid clarification. | Whole-platform replacement, broad transformation or undefined “automate everything” request. |
| Operational value | Repeated delay, chasing, rework, weak ownership or poor visibility creates a credible reason to act. | Effect is plausible but unmeasured. | One-off or minor inconvenience that does not justify paid work. |
| People and decision | Workflow owner, relevant staff and decision-maker are available or can be named. | One is missing but there is a credible plan to involve them. | No owner, no approval path or unwillingness to involve users. |
| Access and feasibility | Lawful access, permissions, representative non-sensitive materials and testing participation appear possible. | Vendor, security, data or integration facts need Heutrix Diagnostics or a specialist check. | Required access is unavailable or the buyer demands an unassessed integration/outcome guarantee. |
| Commercial and timing fit | Buyer can consider a four- or five-figure fixed-scope project and has a real decision window. | Valid need but later timing, budget cycle or prerequisite. | Expects a low-cost task, unpaid design, immediate emergency delivery or unsupported enterprise capability. |

## Route rules

### Heutrix Diagnostics

Recommend when the operational problem is credible but any of these remain unclear:

- which workflow should be addressed first;
- current-state boundary or ownership;
- baseline and benefit case;
- feasible options, dependencies or data path;
- acceptance criteria; or
- whether a build is justified at all.

The diagnostic must remain useful if the buyer does not proceed with Heutrix. Do not promise an automatic fee credit; reuse approved diagnostic artefacts in later scope so discovery is not duplicated.

### Heutrix Workflow Transformation

Recommend direct scoping only when all of these are sufficiently clear:

- one workflow and owner;
- users and roles;
- desired operating result;
- current systems and plausible access path;
- representative safe test inputs;
- acceptance basis;
- decision-maker and buyer participation; and
- fixed-scope commercial seriousness.

The 20-minute call may confirm this route, but detailed solution design and technical feasibility belong in paid or written scoping work. Dashboards, trackers, forms, handovers, lightweight internal tools and automation are deliverable types within this product, not separate public offers.

### Heutrix AI Guardrails

Use when the immediate problem is informal or planned workplace AI use and the organisation needs approved and prohibited uses, information boundaries, accountable human review, escalation and practical staff guidance. Route broader or unclear operational problems to Heutrix Diagnostics.

### Nurture / revisit

Use when the need fits but timing, budget cycle, internal ownership, system access or another prerequisite is not ready. Agree a specific review month or trigger. Add resources or marketing only with the relevant permission.

### No fit / no build

Use one respectful outcome:

- **DIY resource:** the issue is simple, low-risk and unlikely to justify paid work. Share only an approved resource, not custom design.
- **Referral category:** another discipline is needed, such as legal, privacy, clinical, cybersecurity, enterprise platform or change advice. Name a provider only when a real, approved referral exists; otherwise state the type of adviser to seek.
- **No-build recommendation:** the existing process or tool can be clarified without new software, the benefit case is weak, or the proposed change would add unnecessary complexity.
- **Capability no-fit:** the scope, platform, enterprise requirements, geography or support expectation is outside verified Heutrix capability.

## Hard-stop handling

If the prospect begins sharing sensitive care details, interrupt respectfully:

> I’m going to pause you there so we do not bring sensitive information into this call. A general description is enough—for example, “a document is missing” rather than who it relates to or what it contains.

If the call centres on legal, clinical, regulatory or compliance advice:

> That decision needs an appropriately qualified adviser and sits outside Heutrix's workflow-implementation role. We can assess a separate operational workflow only if it can be clearly separated from that advice.
