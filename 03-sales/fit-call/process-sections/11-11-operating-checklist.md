# 11. Operating checklist

## Before launch

- [ ] Assign [CALLER NAME], [BACKUP CALLER], lead-review owner and opportunity owner.
- [ ] Confirm video/phone format, platform, phone fallback and accessibility option.
- [ ] Configure real scheduler, timezone, available hours, notice window, future booking window and reschedule/cancel rules.
- [ ] Approve the exact trigger and coverage of the two-business-day commitment.
- [ ] Confirm scheduler, CRM, email, reminder and calendar integrations.
- [ ] Replace all square-bracketed placeholders.
- [ ] Make **Request** the CTA until the complete scheduler path passes QA.
- [ ] Test server-confirmed receipt, duplicate prevention, spam controls, rate limiting and monitored errors.
- [ ] Test booking, calendar event, timezone, caller assignment, reminders, reschedule, cancel and no-show paths.
- [ ] Publish a reviewed collection notice tied to the actual tools and retention process.
- [ ] Verify that no form value or sensitive content reaches analytics.
- [ ] Create approved DIY resource(s) and referral categories before promising them.

## For every request / booking

- [ ] Lead record exists once, with source and landing page.
- [ ] Caller is named in the confirmation and calendar event.
- [ ] Booking ID, time, timezone, channel and reminder status are present.
- [ ] Form contains no apparent sensitive information; if it does, follow the incident/remediation path.
- [ ] High-level qualification is reviewed before the call.

## Before every call

- [ ] Confirm the one workflow and likely qualification gaps.
- [ ] Open the 20-minute guide and CRM record.
- [ ] Check meeting link/phone instructions.
- [ ] Do not prepare a free design or tool recommendation.

## During every call

- [ ] State time, purpose and sensitive-information boundary.
- [ ] Confirm role, workflow, operational effect, owner, decision-maker, access, timing and commercial range fit.
- [ ] Summarise evidence and unknowns.
- [ ] Recommend one route and state who does what next.
- [ ] End on time.

## After every call

- [ ] Mark attendance and outcome.
- [ ] Complete all six qualification gates with evidence notes.
- [ ] Set one route and reason code.
- [ ] Create follow-up deadline and owner.
- [ ] Send written follow-up within the approved interval.
- [ ] Create an opportunity only for a genuine paid path.
- [ ] Record no-fit, no-show, nurture, proposal, won or lost reason.

## Weekly owner review

- [ ] Requests awaiting response beyond SLA.
- [ ] Bookings without confirmations or reminders.
- [ ] Attended calls without outcomes.
- [ ] No-shows without closure.
- [ ] Nurture records without consent basis or review date.
- [ ] Proposals without an agreed decision date.
- [ ] Reason-code distribution and leading no-fit/lost patterns.
- [ ] Qualified leads, booking completion, show rate, qualified-call conversion and gross profit by source.
- [ ] Any sensitive-data incident, delivery failure, duplicate record or attribution gap.
