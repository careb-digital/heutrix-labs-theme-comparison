# 10. Follow-up sequence and service levels

## Transactional sequence

| Trigger | Message / task | Timing | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Request submitted | Verified receipt | Immediate after backend acknowledgement | System |
| Request submitted | Human fit review / reply | Within the approved application of two business days | [LEAD REVIEW OWNER] |
| Booking confirmed | Confirmation email and calendar event | Immediate | System |
| Booking upcoming | 24-hour reminder | 24 hours before | System |
| Booking upcoming | One-hour reminder | 1 hour before | System |
| Booking changed | Updated confirmation and calendar event | Immediate | System |
| No-show | Easy rebooking message | Shortly after caller records no-show | [CALLER / SYSTEM] |
| No-show still unresolved | Close-the-loop message | Proposed: two business days later | [LEAD REVIEW OWNER] |
| Call attended | Outcome recorded | Same day where practical | [CALLER] |
| Call attended | Written recommendation / next step | Within the approved application of two business days | [CALLER] |
| Proposal sent | Review follow-up | Mutually agreed review date | [OPPORTUNITY OWNER] |
| Proposal silent | Pause/close option | Proposed cadence in section 7.14 | [OPPORTUNITY OWNER] |

## Nurture rules

- Use the agreed review date or operational trigger instead of a generic weekly drip.
- Keep direct follow-up about the enquiry separate from ongoing marketing consent.
- Stop outreach when the prospect declines, unsubscribes or asks for closure.
- Do not use healthcare problem details for advertising audiences or personalisation.
- A useful resource can support the relationship; it must not disguise repeated sales pressure.
