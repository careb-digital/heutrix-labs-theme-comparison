# 13. Release acceptance test

The journey is ready only when a production test lead can:

1. click a CTA whose wording matches the action;
2. submit the minimal form without sensitive information;
3. create exactly one CRM lead with source and landing page;
4. see real availability and confirm a time when **Book** is used;
5. receive a calendar event and email naming the caller, length, format, agenda, privacy boundary and reschedule link;
6. receive accurate reminders;
7. reschedule and cancel without manual confusion;
8. be marked attended or no-show;
9. receive one documented fit outcome and written next step within the approved interval; and
10. produce a complete, non-sensitive CRM record with route and reason codes for the analytics workstream.

Until all ten pass, keep the public CTA in **Request** mode and do not send meaningful traffic to the flow.
