# 3. Website and booking copy pack

## 3.1 Site-wide CTA labels

### Public CTA in both modes

- **Primary CTA:** See where Heutrix can help
- **Short CTA where space is tight:** See if we can help
- **Link with selected context:** See where Heutrix can help with [WORKFLOW OR PROBLEM]

The public CTA is intentionally neutral about the mechanism. The next action must still be explicit:

- **Request-mode form button:** Request my 20-minute call
- **Booking-mode form button after scheduler QA:** Continue to available times
- **Scheduler button:** Choose this time

### Avoid

- Any superseded call label or any use of **book** without a working scheduler—wording that creates the wrong expectation.
- Get a free strategy session — promises unpaid consulting.
- Claim your spot / Limited spots / Apply now — pressure language without verified scarcity.
- Submit / Send enquiry — does not describe the next step.

## 3.8 Fit / not-fit microcopy beside the form

**Usually a good fit**

- One recurring non-clinical workflow can be named.
- Several people, roles or systems touch the work.
- A workflow owner and a decision-maker can participate.
- The problem matters enough to consider a properly scoped paid improvement.
- The team can provide lawful, approved access and participate in testing.

**Usually not a fit**

- Legal, clinical, audit, registration, compliance or regulatory advice or guarantees.
- Replacement of a whole practice, participant, case-management or enterprise platform.
- Automation that removes accountable human decisions.
- A one-off inconvenience with no credible benefit case.
- A project with no owner, no approval path or no safe access to the people and systems involved.
