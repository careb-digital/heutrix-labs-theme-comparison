## 7.14 Proposal follow-up sequence

Use mutually agreed dates where possible. The following is an internal default for owner approval, not a public promise:

1. **Proposal sent:** record recipient, amount, offer, scope version and agreed review date.
2. **On the agreed review date:** ask whether the proposal is accurate, what remains unresolved and who owns the decision.
3. **Five business days later if silent:** send one concise check-in with the option to pause or close.
4. **Ten business days after that if still silent:** close the loop and mark `Closed Lost — No decision / no response`, unless the buyer supplied another date.

Do not send daily nudges, countdowns, invented capacity warnings or automatic discounts.
