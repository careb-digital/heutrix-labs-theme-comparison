# 6. Twenty-minute call guide

## Before the call

- Review the form and source page.
- Check for a hard no-fit or sensitive-data issue.
- Confirm caller, format and link/phone instructions.
- Open the CRM record and note the one workflow to test.
- Do not research a solution or prepare unpaid architecture.

## 0:00–2:00 — Opening and boundary

> Thanks for making the time. We have 20 minutes and there is no obligation to proceed. My aim is to understand one recurring operational workflow, check whether Heutrix is a sensible fit and agree the smallest useful next step. This is not a design workshop, so we will stay at a high level and will not need patient, participant or other sensitive information. I may pause us to keep to time or protect that boundary. Does that work for you?

Then confirm:

- caller and attendee roles;
- whether the person can approve a next step or who else is involved; and
- the one workflow to discuss.

## 2:00–6:00 — Current workflow

Ask no more than three questions:

1. What starts this workflow, and what should be true when it is complete?
2. Which roles touch it, and where is ownership or handover least clear?
3. At a high level, which tools hold the work today?

Do not map every step or troubleshoot live.

## 6:00–10:00 — Operational effect and desired result

1. What happens when the workflow does not run well—delay, chasing, missing items, rework or weak visibility?
2. How often does that happen, using their own observation rather than an industry assumption?
3. What would a useful, observable improvement look like for staff and managers?
4. Why is this being considered now?

Do not calculate a promised ROI on the call. If evidence is weak, record a baseline plan as a Heutrix Diagnostics need.

## 10:00–14:00 — Qualification

1. Who owns the workflow and who approves a change?
2. Can the relevant staff participate in a workshop, two focused reviews, testing and training?
3. Are the current systems, permissions and representative non-sensitive materials likely to be available?
4. Are there vendor, security, privacy, integration or procurement questions that must be checked?
5. If there is a sensible case, is a four- or five-figure fixed-scope engagement within the range the organisation can consider?
6. What decision window is real, and what must happen before a project could start?

## 14:00–17:00 — Summary and fit statement

> Let me check that I have this right. The workflow is [BOUNDARY]. The main operational issue is [EFFECT]. A useful result would be [OBSERVABLE RESULT]. [OWNER/DECISION-MAKER] would need to be involved, and the main unknowns are [UNKNOWNS].

Ask: **What have I missed or misunderstood?**

Then state one of:

- **Likely fit:** “This appears consistent with the kind of bounded operational workflow Heutrix assesses.”
- **Possible fit, evidence needed:** “The need may fit, but I do not have enough evidence to recommend implementation yet.”
- **No fit:** “This sits outside Heutrix's role because [PLAIN REASON].”

## 17:00–19:00 — Recommendation

Choose one route only.

**Heutrix Diagnostics**

> The smallest useful next step is Heutrix Diagnostics. The value is deciding what to fix, what not to build and what a decision-ready first scope should contain. I will send the proposed deliverables, assumptions and price in writing.

**Heutrix Workflow Transformation**

> The workflow appears clear enough to consider Heutrix Workflow Transformation, subject to written feasibility, access and acceptance checks. I will send the limited information needed to prepare a fixed-scope proposal. We will not start design or build until that scope is agreed.

**Heutrix AI Guardrails**

> The immediate need is a practical set of workplace AI boundaries. I will confirm the high-level use cases, tools, information types and accountable roles needed before proposing Heutrix AI Guardrails.

**Nurture**

> The need appears credible, but [PREREQUISITE/TIMING] is not ready. The smallest useful next step is to revisit this in [AGREED MONTH/TRIGGER].

**No fit / no build**

> I do not recommend paid Heutrix work for this at present because [REASON]. The most useful next move is [APPROVED DIY RESOURCE / TYPE OF SPECIALIST / SIMPLE NO-BUILD ACTION].

## 19:00–20:00 — Commitments and close

- Repeat the outcome and owner.
- Confirm exactly what Heutrix will send and the approved deadline.
- Confirm what, if anything, the prospect will provide—and repeat that it must be non-sensitive unless a suitable process is agreed.
- Ask whether the summary can be sent to any other decision-maker; obtain their details directly rather than guessing.
- End on time.

## Call discipline: what is out of scope

- no live workflow map;
- no vendor or integration recommendation without feasibility checks;
- no screen sharing of live records;
- no reviewing documents or screenshots containing personal or sensitive data;
- no technical architecture, implementation plan or custom checklist;
- no legal, privacy, clinical, regulatory, audit or registration advice;
- no promised savings, compliance result or delivery date; and
- no discount negotiation before scope and value are understood.
