# Pricing policy

**Status:** approval required before quoting.

- Do not publish a pricing page, public price, starting range, pricing navigation item or pricing CTA. The 3 September 2026 website decision supersedes the earlier temporary rule that only withheld exact prices.
- Every engagement must be fixed in scope and price before paid work begins.
- Use the current pricing and margin analysis under `07-finance/pricing-and-margins/`.
- Do not quote below the approved margin floor. Narrow scope or obtain a documented exception instead of silently discounting.
- Reuse approved Diagnostic artefacts in later implementation, but do not automatically credit the Diagnostic fee unless the owner approves that policy.
- State third-party licences, connectors, vendor work, travel and other pass-through costs separately.

Approval must record loaded hourly cost, expected delivery/non-delivery hours, non-labour cost, target and floor margin, payment stages, cancellation/pause rules and exception authority.
