# Positioning

## Category

Diagnose the right operational problem, transform one bounded workflow, or put practical AI guardrails in place.

## Operating principle

Workflow first. Technology second. Improve approved existing systems where feasible and confirm every tool, integration, licence, permission and data path in writing.

## Public outcome boundary

Heutrix can promise a defined process for mapping, designing, building, proving and handing over an agreed operational workflow. It must not promise compliance, clinical or legal outcomes, revenue, fixed time savings, staff adoption or third-party service availability.

## Voice

Credible, direct, practical, calm and privacy-aware. Use Australian English and avoid inflated transformation language.
