# Ideal client profile

## Primary launch segment

Australian disability support providers with a recurring non-clinical workflow that crosses people or systems and causes visible chasing, delay, weak ownership or poor management visibility.

Fit is based on workflow maturity rather than a public employee-count threshold. A suitable prospect can name the workflow, its accountable owner, the operational consequence, relevant users, available access and a serious implementation budget.

## Secondary segment

Allied health practices with comparable intake/referral, reporting, evidence or document workflow problems.

## Not a launch segment

GP clinics remain a future segment until a non-clinical workflow thesis, supported-system boundary, proof plan and dedicated offer are approved.

## Good-fit conditions

- one important workflow can be bounded;
- an owner and decision-maker are available;
- staff can participate in discovery and testing;
- systems/materials can be accessed lawfully and safely;
- the buyer accepts a no-build recommendation where appropriate; and
- the work can be fixed in scope with written acceptance criteria.
