## Buyer-facing selector copy

### Not sure what to fix first?

Choose **Heutrix Diagnostics**. Map what is happening now, identify the most important constraint and leave with a prioritised implementation brief—even when the right recommendation is not to build.

### Know which workflow needs to work better?

Choose **Heutrix Workflow Transformation**. Heutrix maps, redesigns, builds or configures, tests, trains and hands over one agreed improvement. Dashboards, trackers, forms, handovers, lightweight internal systems and automation sit within this product rather than competing as separate offers.

### Need responsible workplace AI rules?

Choose **Heutrix AI Guardrails**. Define appropriate uses, information boundaries, accountable human review, escalation and staff guidance before informal AI use becomes embedded.

## Product architecture rules

The offers are separate entry points, not a mandatory sequence. Direct Transformation requires sufficient scope and feasibility clarity. AI-enabled implementation still requires appropriate controls; adequate existing client controls can suffice without a separate AI Guardrails engagement. The approved name is **Heutrix AI Guardrails**.

| Current product | What sits inside it | Treatment in navigation and forms |
|---|---|---|
| Heutrix Diagnostics | Workflow review, bottleneck analysis, prioritisation and a decision-ready action plan. | Present as the paid starting point when the right change is not yet clear. |
| Heutrix Workflow Transformation | Workflow redesign, automation, management visibility, trackers, lightweight internal systems, testing, training and handover. | Present as one product with scope variants, not a menu of separate build types. |
| Heutrix AI Guardrails | Appropriate-use boundaries, information handling, human review, escalation and staff guidance. | Present as the governance path for organisations adopting workplace AI. |

Regulated-provider and disability-provider language is sector positioning and a source of relevant use cases, not an additional product.
