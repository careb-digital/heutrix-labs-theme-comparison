### Tools and technical capability

- Which Microsoft 365 products Heutrix can configure today: Excel, Forms, Lists, SharePoint, Teams, Power Automate and/or Power BI.
- Which Google Workspace products Heutrix can configure today: Sheets, Forms and/or Drive.
- Whether Heutrix can currently deliver Airtable or Smartsheet work.
- Which care-management platforms Heutrix has hands-on capability with. ShiftCare, SupportAbility and Lumary are relevant examples, not verified Heutrix capabilities.
- Whether care-platform work is limited to exports/imports and adjacent workflows or includes APIs/native integrations.
- Who pays for licences, connectors, API access, sandboxes, vendor support and additional environments.
- The maximum number of systems, roles, integrations, permission groups, views, measures, forms and automation steps included in Heutrix Workflow Transformation.
- Security review, credential handling, least-privilege access, production deployment and rollback procedures.

**Do not publish the optional named-tools paragraph until these are answered.** The safer launch wording is: “Heutrix assesses approved existing systems and confirms every tool, integration, licence, permission and data path in writing.”

### Delivery scope and capacity

- Maximum number of concurrent Workflow Transformation engagements Heutrix can deliver without weakening the published timeline.
- Exact length and format of the two review checkpoints and training session.
- Standard number of user-test participants and refinement rounds.
- Whether training may be recorded, how consent is captured and how recordings are stored or deleted.
- The condition that starts the 2–4 week window: signed scope, deposit/payment, access ready, workshop booked, or all of these.
- Heutrix Diagnostics and Heutrix AI Guardrails timelines. These are intentionally not stated as fixed public windows.
- Internal communication channel, cadence, named delivery owner and response expectations during a project.

### Acceptance, defects and change control

- Who signs acceptance and what happens if the decision-maker does not respond by the agreed date.
- The exact definition of a qualifying in-scope defect.
- When the 30-day stabilisation period starts and Heutrix's response/correction target during it.
- Whether the stabilisation promise applies to third-party platform updates or only to Heutrix-controlled configuration; recommended answer: only the latter.
- The approval and payment process for change requests.
- Whether a paused project incurs rescheduling or restart fees when client access or approvals are delayed.
- Whether rollback, parallel running or staged launch is standard or project-specific.

### Ownership and intellectual property

- Which deliverable formats are handed over by default.
- Whether custom code/source is always transferred, licensed or transferred only after final payment.
- Whether Heutrix retains reusable templates, generic methods or non-client-specific components.
- Export and portability limits for every proposed third-party platform.
- Responsibility for post-handover administration, credentials, backups, monitoring and vendor renewals.

### Data, privacy and legal boundaries
