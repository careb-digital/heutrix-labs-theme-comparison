## Shared introduction

Heutrix helps Australian disability support providers understand operational friction, improve one important workflow and put practical AI guardrails in place. We also work with allied health practices facing similar non-clinical workflow problems.

Start with the question you need to answer. Each offer can stand alone; the appropriate next step depends on your problem, readiness and required controls.

## Heutrix Diagnostics

**Identify what is worth fixing — and define the sensible next step.**

When an intake, referral, reporting or document process involves repeated chasing, duplicated work or unclear handovers, it can be difficult to decide what should change first.

Heutrix Diagnostics is a paid, scoped review of the agreed workflow or operational problem. It examines how the work happens, identifies friction and dependencies, compares practical options and gives you a decision-ready action brief. That can include changing the process, using existing tools differently, proceeding to implementation or taking no further action.

You receive a current-state view, baseline or measurement plan, prioritised findings, improvement options and recommended next steps with the important assumptions and limitations explained. Implementation is separate. The findings remain useful if you act internally or choose another provider.

**Offer link label:** Explore Heutrix Diagnostics

**Enquiry context:** Bring a general description of the workflow causing concern. The introductory conversation checks fit; the paid Diagnostic provides the investigation and recommendations.

## Heutrix Workflow Transformation

**Improve one important workflow — and make the improvement work in practice.**

When one recurring non-clinical workflow is clear enough to scope responsibly, Heutrix redesigns and implements the agreed improvement around approved systems where feasible.

The result is a documented, tested working process with clear ownership, status and next action, staff guidance, management visibility and a handover your team can maintain. The solution may involve process changes alone or supporting forms, trackers, dashboards, automation, integrations or lightweight tools, subject to feasibility and written scope.

The typical delivery window is 2–4 weeks once written scope, access, decision-makers and prerequisites are ready. The scope confirms testing, review rounds, training, handover and the 30-day path for qualifying in-scope defects. Ongoing support and new requirements are agreed separately.

You can start here directly when the workflow, intended result and dependencies are sufficiently clear. Diagnostics is useful when they are not.

**Offer link label:** Discuss my workflow

**Enquiry context:** Tell us which workflow needs to improve and who owns it. We will assess whether it is ready for implementation or needs further investigation.

## Heutrix AI Guardrails

**Give workplace AI clear boundaries, practical controls and accountable human oversight.**

When staff are using AI, or your organisation is considering it, leadership needs a practical way to decide what is appropriate, what needs additional approval and what should not be allowed.

Heutrix AI Guardrails helps establish the agreed use boundaries, information-handling rules, human-review requirements, approval process and staff guidance. The scope identifies the teams and use cases covered, how the framework will be introduced and who will maintain it.

This is operational governance support. It does not provide continuous technical monitoring, eliminate AI risk or replace legal, privacy, clinical or regulatory advice. Implementing an approved AI-enabled workflow is separately scoped where needed.

**Offer link label:** Put AI guardrails in place

**Enquiry context:** Describe the AI use or governance uncertainty in general terms. We will discuss the scope needed to establish workable boundaries and responsibilities.

## Selected FAQs

**Do we need Diagnostics first?** No. Start directly with Workflow Transformation when one workflow, owner, users, desired result, systems and access path are clear enough to define scope and tests. AI Guardrails can also be purchased directly.

**Do all three offers need to be bought together?** No. Each addresses a different need. AI-enabled implementation still needs appropriate controls, which may already exist in your organisation.

**Are dashboards and automations separate services?** They are possible components of a Workflow Transformation, selected when they support the agreed result and can be delivered within scope.

**Will we need new software?** Heutrix assesses approved existing tools first. New components and integrations require feasibility, permissions, licensing and data-path checks before they are promised.

**How do we know the work is complete?** Diagnostics delivers a reviewed decision and action brief. Workflow Transformation is tested against written acceptance criteria and handed over. AI Guardrails establishes the scoped governance framework with practical guidance and ongoing ownership. Exact completion requirements are agreed before paid work.

**What happens afterwards?** Your team owns the agreed operating responsibilities. Further implementation, support or governance reviews are separately scoped. Buying one offer does not commit you to another.
