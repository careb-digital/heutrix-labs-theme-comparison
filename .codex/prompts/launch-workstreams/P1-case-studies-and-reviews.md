# P1 — Build the case-study and review collection system

**Recommended environment:** ChatGPT Work  
**Priority:** Post-launch optimisation  
**Start when:** Begin with the first real Heutrix Diagnostics or Workflow Transformation engagement; publish only with approval.

## Copy this prompt into a new chat

```text
Create a repeatable proof-collection system for Heutrix Labs. Work inside the current Heutrix repository. Read the current control registers and approved offer first; use the proof findings in HEUTRIX-LABS-LAUNCH-REVIEW.md as historical context. The current business must not manufacture proof; the goal is to collect consented, defensible evidence from real delivery.

Goal: turn each suitable project into measurable, privacy-safe proof while protecting client confidentiality and avoiding claims about compliance, clinical outcomes or sensitive individuals.

Design the system across the project lifecycle:
1. Pre-project baseline: workflow definition, owner, current steps/handoffs, observable failure modes and agreed measures that can be collected without patient/participant information.
2. Delivery evidence: approved screenshots/artifacts, acceptance results, implementation dates, training/adoption evidence and scope changes.
3. 30-day review: before/after measures, qualitative staff/manager feedback, limitations and confounding changes.
4. Consent: named, anonymised and private/internal proof options; exact approval for wording, logos, quotes, screenshots and duration of use; withdrawal/update process.
5. Interview guides for buyer, workflow owner, manager and user.
6. Case-study template: context, problem, constraints, scope, approach, evidence, result, limitations, client quote and next step.
7. Review request sequence timed to a genuine successful moment, with neutral language and no incentive that could distort disclosure.
8. Evidence register recording source, date, approver, consent scope and verification status for every public claim.

Create sector-specific templates for allied health and disability providers, but never include identifying care data. Avoid cherry-picked percentages without denominator/timeframe and distinguish correlation from causation.

Deliverables:
- Baseline and 30-day measurement templates.
- Consent/approval workflow for legal review.
- Interview scripts, review-request messages and case-study template.
- Public-claim evidence register.
- Publication checklist for website, proposals and LinkedIn.

Definition of done: future public proof can be traced to real source evidence and explicit consent, client confidentiality is protected, limitations are visible, and Heutrix has a consistent process for collecting proof rather than asking for vague testimonials after the fact.
```
