# H3 — Make the fit call and sales process concrete

**Recommended environment:** ChatGPT Work  
**Priority:** High-impact improvement  
**Dependency:** H1 offer and B3 delivery-person identity.

```text
Act as the conversion and sales-process designer for Heutrix Labs. Work inside the Heutrix repository. Follow AGENTS.md task-scoped reading; read the relevant decision/fact entries, CTA/contact copy and approved offer first; treat HEUTRIX-LABS-LAUNCH-REVIEW.md as historical evidence. The local site now uses request-mode language, while a real lead destination and direct-booking mechanism remain unimplemented.

Goal: define a low-friction, trustworthy fit-call journey that qualifies buyers, sets expectations and produces a clear next step without turning the call into unpaid consulting.

Design the full journey:
1. CTA wording: use “Book” only with a real scheduler; otherwise “Request.”
2. Verify and state call duration, named caller, response/availability commitment and whether the call is video or phone.
3. Explain the agenda: identify one workflow, assess fit, and agree the smallest useful next step. State that no patient/participant information is needed.
4. Define minimal pre-call questions for sector, role, team context, workflow problem, current tools at a high level, urgency and decision context. Avoid sensitive care data.
5. Define fit/no-fit criteria and respectful no-fit outcomes, including DIY resource, referral or no-build recommendation where appropriate.
6. Create confirmation, calendar, reminder, reschedule, no-show and post-call messages.
7. Create a 20-minute call guide with opening, discovery, qualification, summary, recommendation and next-step sections.
8. Define post-call paths: no fit, nurture, Heutrix Diagnostics, direct Workflow Transformation or AI Guardrails when appropriate, proposal and follow-up.
9. Define CRM fields/stages and reason codes for the analytics workstream.
10. Write website-ready CTA sections and “what happens next” microcopy.

Ask for missing operational facts rather than inventing them. Do not promise same-day or one-business-day response unless the owner can honour it. Do not use false urgency, pressure tactics or fake scarcity.

Deliverables:
- End-to-end fit-call journey map.
- Website/booking/confirmation/reminder copy pack.
- Qualification form and call script.
- CRM stage and follow-up sequence specification.
- Operating checklist and owner decisions still required.

Definition of done: a visitor knows who the call is with, how long it takes, what information to bring, what will happen, what outcomes are possible and when follow-up occurs; the owner can run the call consistently and record qualification outcomes.
```
