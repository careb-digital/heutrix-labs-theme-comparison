# H1 — Package the three Heutrix products

**Recommended environment:** ChatGPT Work  
**Priority:** High-impact improvement  
**Dependency:** Owner input on delivery capability, ideal client and supported tools.

## Copy this prompt into a new chat

```text
Act as Heutrix Labs' offer strategist and service-design lead. Work inside the Heutrix repository. Read the current control registers and offer sources first; treat HEUTRIX-LABS-LAUNCH-REVIEW.md as historical evidence, then inspect the active service copy.

Goal: replace the retired service menu with three clear products: Heutrix Diagnostics, Heutrix Workflow Transformation and Heutrix AI Guardrails. Keep Workflow Transformation as the main implementation path while giving Diagnostics and AI Guardrails distinct jobs-to-be-done.

Use the Value Equation as an analytical framework: increase the importance and specificity of the dream outcome and perceived likelihood; reduce perceived delay and buyer effort. Do not imitate a public figure's writing style.

Work through:
1. Define the primary ideal client by sector, role, team size/maturity, problem severity, current systems and buying trigger. Preserve the confirmed audience order: Australian disability support providers first, allied health practices second and GP clinics as a future segment.
2. Define good-fit and not-a-fit criteria.
3. State the controllable outcome without promising compliance, revenue, clinical outcomes or fabricated time savings.
4. Specify scope: baseline, current-state map, future state, build/configuration, testing, acceptance, training, documentation, ownership, handover and 30-day stabilisation.
5. Define customer responsibilities, required access, meeting load, decision-maker involvement, exclusions, dependencies and change control.
6. Use the current typical 2–4 week example timeline, clearly subject to the written scope, access and dependencies.
7. Decide how paid Heutrix Diagnostics leads into Workflow Transformation without making discovery feel like disguised sales work.
8. Simplify the public choice architecture to three clear starting points and map each existing service into the new structure.
9. Produce website-ready copy: offer name, one-line promise, who it is for, deliverables, process, timeline, engagement-agreement wording, FAQs and CTA. Do not create a public price, starting range, pricing page or pricing CTA; H4 supports private quoting.

Ask a concise decision questionnaire before finalising facts that the project files cannot establish. Do not invent tools, capacity, experience, outcomes, guarantees or delivery timelines.

Deliverables:
- Offer decision document.
- One-page flagship offer specification.
- Three-offer comparison/decision table.
- Website-ready offer and homepage copy.
- List of facts requiring owner validation.

Definition of done: a qualified buyer can understand the problem solved, result, full scope, buyer effort, timing, boundaries, ownership and next step without comparing six vague services.
```
