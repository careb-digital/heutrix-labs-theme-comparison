# H2 — Create an honest proof demonstration and sample deliverables

**Recommended environment:** Codex  
**Priority:** High-impact improvement  
**Dependency:** H1 flagship deliverables and positioning are final enough to demonstrate.

## Copy this prompt into a new chat

```text
Create Heutrix Labs' first honest capability-demonstration experience. Work in the current Heutrix repository. Read the control registers, current website and approved Heutrix Workflow Transformation specification first; use HEUTRIX-LABS-LAUNCH-REVIEW.md as historical context. The business may not yet have publishable customer proof, so this work must demonstrate deliverables without pretending to show client results.

Goal: give a cautious allied health or disability-provider buyer enough concrete evidence to understand what Heutrix can produce and how it reduces delivery risk.

Build one coherent synthetic-data example around a realistic but fictional workflow, such as referral intake or incident follow-up. Include:
1. Clearly labelled “Demonstration using synthetic data — not a client result.”
2. A current-state workflow map showing bottlenecks and unclear handoffs.
3. A future-state workflow showing trigger, owner, status, next action, overdue/exception path and handover.
4. A synthetic manager view or tracker/dashboard.
5. A staff quick-start guide.
6. An administrator runbook covering ownership, maintenance and recovery.
7. Acceptance criteria/test checklist.
8. A concise implementation timeline and 30-day stabilisation example.
9. Privacy/human-review boundaries appropriate to the fictional scenario.

Integrate the demonstration into the website with useful captions, accessible structure, mobile behaviour and a CTA leading naturally to the flagship offer or fit call. Prefer a focused interactive or annotated walkthrough over decorative dashboard imagery. Optimise assets and add social/SEO metadata where appropriate.

Do not use real patient/participant data, invented customers, fake logos, fabricated before/after metrics, fake testimonials or unverified compliance claims. Avoid making the fictional organisation resemble a real client.

Deliverables:
- The demonstration page/section and reusable sample artefacts.
- Source note documenting all fictional assumptions.
- Accessibility and responsive checks.
- Clear replacement plan for adding real approved case proof later.
- Summary of files changed and tests run.

Definition of done: a buyer can inspect representative outputs from map through handover, clearly understands that the scenario is synthetic, and sees evidence of Heutrix's method without being misled about customer results.
```
