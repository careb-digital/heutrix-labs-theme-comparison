# P5 — Verify Melbourne local presence and Google Business Profile eligibility

**Recommended environment:** ChatGPT Work  
**Priority:** Post-launch optimisation  
**Start when:** Business location/service-area facts and Google eligibility are verified.

## Copy this prompt into a new chat

```text
Own local-presence strategy for Heutrix Labs. Work inside the current Heutrix repository. Read the control registers and current website first; use the local SEO and trust findings in HEUTRIX-LABS-LAUNCH-REVIEW.md as historical context, and consult current official Google Business Profile guidance before recommending account actions.

Goal: establish an accurate, policy-compliant Melbourne/local presence only if Heutrix is genuinely eligible, while avoiding fake offices, keyword-stuffed locations or unsupported service-area claims.

Tasks:
1. Gather verified facts: real operating location, whether clients are served face to face, whether the address can/should be public, service areas, opening/contact hours, legal/trading name, monitored phone/email, website domain and category/service reality.
2. Determine Google Business Profile eligibility using current official guidance. Cite the relevant rule and clearly state any uncertainty.
3. If eligible, prepare an owner checklist for profile creation/claim, verification, accurate name/category, service area/address handling, services, description, photos, website/UTM links, messaging/call handling and ongoing review responses. Do not take external account actions without authority.
4. If not eligible, recommend compliant alternatives: Melbourne wording supported by facts, local partnerships/directories where appropriate, relevant local pages/content and strong branded search presence.
5. Audit website consistency for business name, contact details, service area and structured data. Recommend or implement only verified facts.
6. Create a review-handling policy: ask neutrally after genuine success, respond professionally, protect confidentiality and never incentivise or gate reviews improperly.
7. Define measurement for calls, direction requests if relevant, website visits and qualified leads via tagged links.

Do not invent an address, virtual office, service radius, opening hours, reviews or local ranking claims. Do not recommend creating multiple location pages without distinct real value.

Deliverables:
- Eligibility decision with official-source citations.
- Verified local fact sheet.
- GBP or non-GBP action checklist.
- Website/local schema recommendations.
- Review-response and measurement plan.

Definition of done: Heutrix's local claims match operational reality, any Google profile follows current policy, website identity is consistent, and local activity can be connected to qualified enquiries.
```
