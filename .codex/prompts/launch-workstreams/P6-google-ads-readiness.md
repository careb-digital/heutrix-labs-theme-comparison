# P6 — Decide whether Heutrix is ready for Google Ads

**Recommended environment:** ChatGPT Work  
**Priority:** Post-launch optimisation  
**Start when:** Forms/bookings, proof, attribution, follow-up and unit economics have real evidence.

## Copy this prompt into a new chat

```text
Act as a conservative paid-acquisition strategist for Heutrix Labs. Work inside the current Heutrix repository. Read the control registers, approved offer and pricing policy, analytics definitions and available post-launch data first; use HEUTRIX-LABS-LAUNCH-REVIEW.md as historical context. Consult current official Google Ads documentation where technical or policy details matter.

Goal: make an evidence-based go/no-go decision for a small Google Search Ads pilot. Do not recommend spend merely because the website is live.

First audit readiness:
1. Real form/booking path works and failures are monitored.
2. Confirmed lead/booking conversions are tracked without double counting.
3. Source/UTM and CRM stages connect through qualified lead, show, proposal, customer and gross profit.
4. Fit-call follow-up and response time are operationally reliable.
5. Offer, pricing, risk reducers and landing pages are coherent.
6. Honest proof/demonstration is present.
7. Privacy/consent and advertising-tag implications are reviewed.
8. Delivery capacity and gross-margin economics can support acquisition.

Use real data to calculate break-even economics:
- Gross profit per new customer.
- Qualified-call-to-customer rate.
- Lead-to-qualified-call and click-to-lead rates where available.
- Maximum sustainable CAC, qualified CPL and CPC under conservative/base scenarios.

If readiness fails, produce a stop list and the minimum evidence needed before reconsideration. If ready, design a tightly controlled pilot:
- One priority segment and one high-intent problem cluster.
- Search-only structure, negative-keyword approach and geographic targeting based on verified delivery area.
- Message-matched landing page and truthful ads.
- Budget cap, duration, conversion verification, lead-quality review cadence and explicit stop/scale rules.
- Exclusions for vague AI curiosity, employment/training, software-platform searches and regulated/compliance promises Heutrix does not provide.

Do not invent keyword volume, CPCs, conversion rates or revenue. Use live planning data only when accessible and label estimates. Do not launch campaigns or spend money without explicit approval.

Deliverables:
- Readiness scorecard and go/no-go verdict.
- Break-even acquisition model.
- Stop list or pilot brief.
- Keyword/negative-keyword research plan and landing-page requirements.
- Daily/weekly monitoring and decision rules based on qualified leads and gross profit.

Definition of done: the owner knows whether paid search can be tested responsibly, the maximum affordable economics are transparent, and any pilot has hard budget, quality and stop controls before money is spent.
```
