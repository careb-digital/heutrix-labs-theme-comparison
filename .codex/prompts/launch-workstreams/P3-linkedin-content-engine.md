# P3 — Build a LinkedIn content engine

**Recommended environment:** ChatGPT Work  
**Priority:** Post-launch optimisation  
**Start when:** Positioning, founder identity and demonstration assets are approved.

## Copy this prompt into a new chat

```text
Create a sustainable founder-led LinkedIn content system for Heutrix Labs. Work inside the current Heutrix repository. Read the control registers, approved offer, current LinkedIn system and verified founder facts first; use the positioning, customer-problem, proof and lead-generation sections of HEUTRIX-LABS-LAUNCH-REVIEW.md as historical context.

Goal: attract Australian allied health and disability-provider owners/operations leaders by teaching specific workflow failure modes, showing useful demonstrations and creating informed demand for one-workflow improvement—not by posting generic automation or AI commentary.

Develop:
1. A clear audience and point of view anchored in “one workflow, proven and handed over” plus management visibility and human-controlled automation.
2. Four content pillars: problem diagnosis, workflow design principles, synthetic demonstrations, and owner/operator decision guidance.
3. A hook library based on recognisable situations such as ownerless referrals, missing documents discovered late, open incident actions, spreadsheet handoffs and reporting-by-chasing.
4. A 6-week schedule with a realistic founder cadence and a mix of text posts, annotated images/documents, short captioned demonstration clips and occasional lead-magnet promotion.
5. Twelve complete draft posts, including at least three demonstration-led posts and three objection-handling posts.
6. Comment/DM response guidance that moves genuine interest to the correct resource or fit call without spammy outreach.
7. A content-to-landing-page map and UTM naming convention.
8. A repurposing workflow from one demonstration or case study into multiple useful formats.
9. Metrics: target-role engagement, qualified profile visits, landing visits, lead-magnet completions, qualified leads and booked calls—not vanity impressions alone.

Use verified founder/business facts only. Clearly label synthetic examples. Do not invent customers, results, industry data, regulatory claims, testimonials or urgency. Do not imitate any public figure's writing style.

Deliverables:
- Content strategy and editorial rules.
- Six-week calendar.
- Twelve publication-ready drafts and visual briefs.
- UTM/content attribution plan.
- Weekly review process based on lead quality.

Definition of done: the owner can publish consistently for six weeks, every post maps to a buyer problem and approved next step, proof is honest, and performance can be tied to qualified website activity rather than impressions alone.
```
