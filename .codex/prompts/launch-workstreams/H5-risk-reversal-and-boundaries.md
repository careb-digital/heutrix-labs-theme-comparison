# H5 — Define ethical risk reversal and commercial boundaries

**Recommended environment:** ChatGPT Work with contract/legal review  
**Priority:** High-impact improvement  
**Dependency:** H1 offer scope and H4 pricing/milestones.

## Copy this prompt into a new chat

```text
Act as a service-offer risk strategist for Heutrix Labs. Work in the current Heutrix repository. Read the control registers and current offer/legal sources first; use the risk-reversal, objections, FAQ, terms and offer sections of HEUTRIX-LABS-LAUNCH-REVIEW.md as historical context. This task is commercial/service design, not legal advice; identify language that must be reviewed by an Australian lawyer and reflected in the client agreement.

Goal: reduce the buyer's financial, performance, time, quality, trust and switching risk without promising outcomes Heutrix cannot control.

Design a coherent risk-reduction system covering:
1. Written scope and success measures established before build.
2. Objective acceptance criteria and an acceptance-test process.
3. Stage gates: Diagnostics/design approval before build; explicit decision points for proceed, revise or stop.
4. Fixed-scope/fixed-price boundaries and change-control rules.
5. A 30-day correction period for qualifying in-scope defects, with clear exclusions and reporting process.
6. Client ownership: accounts, access, exports, source/configuration where applicable, documentation and maintenance responsibilities.
7. Synthetic/de-identified testing first and accountable human review for sensitive/exception decisions.
8. A no-build recommendation policy when the proposed work is not justified.
9. Dependencies and client responsibilities that prevent Heutrix from guaranteeing adoption, compliance, revenue or operational outcomes.
10. Support, incident, rollback and exit/handover expectations.

Create a traceability table connecting each major buyer fear to the proposed control, website explanation, proposal/scope clause and client-agreement topic. Recommend website copy that is reassuring but concise; keep the detailed legal mechanics out of marketing pages where appropriate.

Do not default to a money-back guarantee. Do not claim compliance, zero risk, guaranteed savings, guaranteed timelines or unlimited revisions. Do not create legal terms as if approved.

Deliverables:
- Risk register and control matrix.
- Page-ready risk-reversal/FAQ copy.
- Proposal/scope checklist.
- Contract topics for lawyer review.
- Operating process for acceptance, defect correction, change control and handover.

Definition of done: buyers can see how scope, acceptance, ownership, defects and stage decisions protect them; Heutrix's uncontrollable risks remain explicit; and website promises can be operationally honoured and contractually reviewed.
```
