# Heutrix Examples

## Short description

Heutrix helps Australian allied health practices and disability support providers improve operational workflows, reduce avoidable admin and introduce AI with practical safeguards.

## Headline

> Improve the workflow before adding more technology.

## Homepage introduction

Important work can become difficult to manage when it is spread across inboxes, spreadsheets, disconnected systems and staff memory. Heutrix reviews how the workflow operates now, identifies where follow-up or visibility is being lost, and recommends the simplest practical improvement.

## Allied health example

> Make referral, enquiry and follow-up work easier to see.

Heutrix can help map the current process, clarify ownership, improve tracking and create a practical operating view without replacing clinical judgement or introducing unnecessary software.

## Disability-provider example

> Improve intake, onboarding and routine administration without turning every problem into a compliance project.

Heutrix focuses on operational workflows such as participant intake, worker onboarding, document collection, internal requests and service-start coordination.

## Workflow Diagnostic offer

> One workflow. A clear current-state view. Prioritised improvements. A practical next step.

The AUD 950 plus GST Workflow Diagnostic is for organisations that know a process is slow, inconsistent or difficult to monitor but need evidence before committing to a build.

## Safe AI example

AI can assist with approved drafting, summarising, task extraction and internal knowledge work. It should not replace human judgement or receive identifiable patient, participant, staff or sensitive business information through unapproved public tools.

## Discovery question examples

- Where does this workflow start and finish?
- Who owns the next action at each stage?
- What information is copied, delayed or lost?
- Which steps depend on memory or one person's spreadsheet?
- What needs human review?
- What would a useful improvement look like after 30 days?

## Avoid this

> Heutrix uses cutting-edge AI to automate your entire practice and guarantee compliance.

Why it fails: it is unsupported, unsafe, outside scope and inconsistent with the workflow-first position.
