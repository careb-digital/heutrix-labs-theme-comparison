## Confirmed direction

Heutrix Pty Ltd helps Australian allied health practices and disability support providers improve operational workflows, visibility and safe use of AI.

The latest direct decision is to target both segments equally while using segment-specific language.

Heutrix should be positioned around:

- workflow diagnosis
- practical process improvement
- better tracking and operational visibility
- appropriate automation
- maintainable internal systems
- privacy-aware, human-reviewed AI use

## Operating principle

> Workflow first. Technology second.

Understand the people, information, decisions, handovers and failure points before recommending software, automation or AI.

## Mandatory boundaries

- Do not describe Heutrix as an NDIS compliance auditor, registration consultant, clinical governance consultancy or legal adviser.
- Do not claim official audit, assurance, clinical or medical capability.
- Do not promise registration, compliance, savings, productivity, revenue or regulatory outcomes.
- Do not recommend public generative AI tools for identifiable patient, participant, worker or sensitive business information.
- Keep AI in an assistive role with appropriate human review.
- Do not claim existing health-sector case studies, client results or ten years of company history.
- Use "combined founder experience" where evidence supports it.

## Current commercial rule

Use **AUD 950 plus GST** for the launch Workflow Diagnostic unless Janith approves a replacement. Older website prices are reference material, not the current launch decision.

Do not publish a Launch Partner Program, implementation credit or build threshold until the exact commercial terms are ratified.

## Output requirements

For proposals, plans and service copy:

- define the workflow in scope
- state inclusions and exclusions
- identify data sensitivity and approved tools
- name the accountable owner and reviewer
- include acceptance criteria and handover
- distinguish facts, assumptions and recommendations
- make the next step explicit

## LinkedIn publishing rules

The 120-day launch content system is stored in `LinkedIn Posts/`. It covers 16 August–13 December 2026 and contains 52 post drafts in 18 weekly files.

Before publishing or adapting a post:

- resolve the public-brand choice between Heutrix and the older Heutrix Labs name;
- recheck the AUD 950 plus GST diagnostic price and scope where mentioned;
- follow the status and human-check fields in the weekly file;
- reopen primary sources for source-led posts;
- keep synthetic examples visibly labelled;
- do not add client results, savings, testimonials or case studies without evidence and permission;
- do not invite sensitive information in comments, messages or forms;
- use the LinkedIn Page as the source and founder profiles for genuine, non-scripted amplification;
- update the plan from measured Page performance at Days 30, 60, 90 and 120.

The drafts use “Heutrix” as the working public brand because that is the user's requested name. This is an assumption for content production, not a ratified replacement of “Heutrix Labs”.
