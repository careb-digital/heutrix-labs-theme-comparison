# H3 — “See where Heutrix can help” call and sales-process specification

**Purpose:** A low-friction, consistent journey from the “See where Heutrix can help” CTA to a clear commercial or no-fit outcome  
**Applies to:** Home, service and sector CTAs; `/contact` or future booking page; scheduler; email/calendar messages; 20-minute calls; CRM; analytics  
**Prepared:** 1 September 2026  
**Last aligned:** 2 September 2026 against the approved three-product offer, verified facts and lead-handling standard  
**Status:** Decision-ready. Do not publish square-bracketed text or any item marked **Owner approval required**.

## Operating principles

1. Use **See where Heutrix can help** as the public CTA. Use **Book**, **Choose a time** or similar mechanism wording only when a visitor can select an available time and receive a confirmed calendar event; otherwise the form button must say **Request my 20-minute call**.
2. “See where Heutrix can help” is a 20-minute, no-obligation fit assessment—not a free workflow audit, consulting session or technical design workshop.
3. Discuss one recurring, non-clinical operational workflow. Do not request or accept patient, participant, client, clinical, Medicare, NDIS, credential or other sensitive information.
4. The call should determine fit and the smallest useful next step. It should not produce a free process map, tool architecture, integration design, implementation plan or quantified business case.
5. Give an honest no-build recommendation when the problem does not justify paid work.
6. No paid work begins until scope, responsibilities, assumptions, exclusions, acceptance criteria, timing, third-party costs, change triggers and price are agreed in writing.
7. Do not create urgency from unverified capacity or deadlines. Use the buyer's actual operational consequences and timing.

# 5. Fit, no-fit and route criteria

## Six qualification gates

Record each as **Green**, **Yellow**, **Red** or **Unknown**, with a one-sentence evidence note.

| Gate | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem fit | Recurring, non-clinical operational workflow in the launch sectors. | Adjacent sector or mixed operational/advisory need that can be separated. | Legal, clinical, regulatory, audit, registration or accountable professional decision-making is central. |
| Boundary | One workflow can be named and bounded. | Priority or boundary needs paid clarification. | Whole-platform replacement, broad transformation or undefined “automate everything” request. |
| Operational value | Repeated delay, chasing, rework, weak ownership or poor visibility creates a credible reason to act. | Effect is plausible but unmeasured. | One-off or minor inconvenience that does not justify paid work. |
| People and decision | Workflow owner, relevant staff and decision-maker are available or can be named. | One is missing but there is a credible plan to involve them. | No owner, no approval path or unwillingness to involve users. |
| Access and feasibility | Lawful access, permissions, representative non-sensitive materials and testing participation appear possible. | Vendor, security, data or integration facts need Heutrix Diagnostics or a specialist check. | Required access is unavailable or the buyer demands an unassessed integration/outcome guarantee. |
| Commercial and timing fit | Buyer can consider a four- or five-figure fixed-scope project and has a real decision window. | Valid need but later timing, budget cycle or prerequisite. | Expects a low-cost task, unpaid design, immediate emergency delivery or unsupported enterprise capability. |

## Route rules

### Heutrix Diagnostics

Recommend when the operational problem is credible but any of these remain unclear:

- which workflow should be addressed first;
- current-state boundary or ownership;
- baseline and benefit case;
- feasible options, dependencies or data path;
- acceptance criteria; or
- whether a build is justified at all.

The diagnostic must remain useful if the buyer does not proceed with Heutrix. Do not promise an automatic fee credit; reuse approved diagnostic artefacts in later scope so discovery is not duplicated.

### Heutrix Workflow Transformation

Recommend direct scoping only when all of these are sufficiently clear:

- one workflow and owner;
- users and roles;
- desired operating result;
- current systems and plausible access path;
- representative safe test inputs;
- acceptance basis;
- decision-maker and buyer participation; and
- fixed-scope commercial seriousness.

The 20-minute call may confirm this route, but detailed solution design and technical feasibility belong in paid or written scoping work. Dashboards, trackers, forms, handovers, lightweight internal tools and automation are deliverable types within this product, not separate public offers.

### Heutrix AI Guardrails

Use when the immediate problem is informal or planned workplace AI use and the organisation needs approved and prohibited uses, information boundaries, accountable human review, escalation and practical staff guidance. Route broader or unclear operational problems to Heutrix Diagnostics.

### Nurture / revisit

Use when the need fits but timing, budget cycle, internal ownership, system access or another prerequisite is not ready. Agree a specific review month or trigger. Add resources or marketing only with the relevant permission.

### No fit / no build

Use one respectful outcome:

- **DIY resource:** the issue is simple, low-risk and unlikely to justify paid work. Share only an approved resource, not custom design.
- **Referral category:** another discipline is needed, such as legal, privacy, clinical, cybersecurity, enterprise platform or change advice. Name a provider only when a real, approved referral exists; otherwise state the type of adviser to seek.
- **No-build recommendation:** the existing process or tool can be clarified without new software, the benefit case is weak, or the proposed change would add unnecessary complexity.
- **Capability no-fit:** the scope, platform, enterprise requirements, geography or support expectation is outside verified Heutrix capability.

## Hard-stop handling

If the prospect begins sharing sensitive care details, interrupt respectfully:

> I’m going to pause you there so we do not bring sensitive information into this call. A general description is enough—for example, “a document is missing” rather than who it relates to or what it contains.

If the call centres on legal, clinical, regulatory or compliance advice:

> That decision needs an appropriately qualified adviser and sits outside Heutrix's workflow-implementation role. We can assess a separate operational workflow only if it can be clearly separated from that advice.

# 6. Twenty-minute call guide

## Before the call

- Review the form and source page.
- Check for a hard no-fit or sensitive-data issue.
- Confirm caller, format and link/phone instructions.
- Open the CRM record and note the one workflow to test.
- Do not research a solution or prepare unpaid architecture.

## 0:00–2:00 — Opening and boundary

> Thanks for making the time. We have 20 minutes and there is no obligation to proceed. My aim is to understand one recurring operational workflow, check whether Heutrix is a sensible fit and agree the smallest useful next step. This is not a design workshop, so we will stay at a high level and will not need patient, participant or other sensitive information. I may pause us to keep to time or protect that boundary. Does that work for you?

Then confirm:

- caller and attendee roles;
- whether the person can approve a next step or who else is involved; and
- the one workflow to discuss.

## 2:00–6:00 — Current workflow

Ask no more than three questions:

1. What starts this workflow, and what should be true when it is complete?
2. Which roles touch it, and where is ownership or handover least clear?
3. At a high level, which tools hold the work today?

Do not map every step or troubleshoot live.

## 6:00–10:00 — Operational effect and desired result

1. What happens when the workflow does not run well—delay, chasing, missing items, rework or weak visibility?
2. How often does that happen, using their own observation rather than an industry assumption?
3. What would a useful, observable improvement look like for staff and managers?
4. Why is this being considered now?

Do not calculate a promised ROI on the call. If evidence is weak, record a baseline plan as a Heutrix Diagnostics need.

## 10:00–14:00 — Qualification

1. Who owns the workflow and who approves a change?
2. Can the relevant staff participate in a workshop, two focused reviews, testing and training?
3. Are the current systems, permissions and representative non-sensitive materials likely to be available?
4. Are there vendor, security, privacy, integration or procurement questions that must be checked?
5. If there is a sensible case, is a four- or five-figure fixed-scope engagement within the range the organisation can consider?
6. What decision window is real, and what must happen before a project could start?

## 14:00–17:00 — Summary and fit statement

> Let me check that I have this right. The workflow is [BOUNDARY]. The main operational issue is [EFFECT]. A useful result would be [OBSERVABLE RESULT]. [OWNER/DECISION-MAKER] would need to be involved, and the main unknowns are [UNKNOWNS].

Ask: **What have I missed or misunderstood?**

Then state one of:

- **Likely fit:** “This appears consistent with the kind of bounded operational workflow Heutrix assesses.”
- **Possible fit, evidence needed:** “The need may fit, but I do not have enough evidence to recommend implementation yet.”
- **No fit:** “This sits outside Heutrix's role because [PLAIN REASON].”

## 17:00–19:00 — Recommendation

Choose one route only.

**Heutrix Diagnostics**

> The smallest useful next step is Heutrix Diagnostics. The value is deciding what to fix, what not to build and what a decision-ready first scope should contain. I will send the proposed deliverables, assumptions and price in writing.

**Heutrix Workflow Transformation**

> The workflow appears clear enough to consider Heutrix Workflow Transformation, subject to written feasibility, access and acceptance checks. I will send the limited information needed to prepare a fixed-scope proposal. We will not start design or build until that scope is agreed.

**Heutrix AI Guardrails**

> The immediate need is a practical set of workplace AI boundaries. I will confirm the high-level use cases, tools, information types and accountable roles needed before proposing Heutrix AI Guardrails.

**Nurture**

> The need appears credible, but [PREREQUISITE/TIMING] is not ready. The smallest useful next step is to revisit this in [AGREED MONTH/TRIGGER].

**No fit / no build**

> I do not recommend paid Heutrix work for this at present because [REASON]. The most useful next move is [APPROVED DIY RESOURCE / TYPE OF SPECIALIST / SIMPLE NO-BUILD ACTION].

## 19:00–20:00 — Commitments and close

- Repeat the outcome and owner.
- Confirm exactly what Heutrix will send and the approved deadline.
- Confirm what, if anything, the prospect will provide—and repeat that it must be non-sensitive unless a suitable process is agreed.
- Ask whether the summary can be sent to any other decision-maker; obtain their details directly rather than guessing.
- End on time.

## Call discipline: what is out of scope

- no live workflow map;
- no vendor or integration recommendation without feasibility checks;
- no screen sharing of live records;
- no reviewing documents or screenshots containing personal or sensitive data;
- no technical architecture, implementation plan or custom checklist;
- no legal, privacy, clinical, regulatory, audit or registration advice;
- no promised savings, compliance result or delivery date; and
- no discount negotiation before scope and value are understood.

## 7.14 Proposal follow-up sequence

Use mutually agreed dates where possible. The following is an internal default for owner approval, not a public promise:

1. **Proposal sent:** record recipient, amount, offer, scope version and agreed review date.
2. **On the agreed review date:** ask whether the proposal is accurate, what remains unresolved and who owns the decision.
3. **Five business days later if silent:** send one concise check-in with the option to pause or close.
4. **Ten business days after that if still silent:** close the loop and mark `Closed Lost — No decision / no response`, unless the buyer supplied another date.

Do not send daily nudges, countdowns, invented capacity warnings or automatic discounts.

# 9. CRM and analytics specification

## 9.1 Keep three concepts separate

1. **Lead lifecycle** records the person's journey from request to outcome.
2. **Opportunity stage** begins only when a paid next step is genuinely being considered.
3. **Activity/event status** records bookings, reminders, attendance and messages without distorting pipeline stage.

## 9.2 Lead lifecycle statuses

| Status | Entry definition | Required next action |
|---|---|---|
| New Request | Server-confirmed form receipt; no time booked. | Owner review and response deadline. |
| Booking Pending | Fit appears plausible and scheduling is offered, or visitor opened calendar without confirming. | Booking link/task; do not call this booked. |
| Booked | Scheduler-confirmed event and booking ID exist. | Send confirmation/reminders. |
| Rescheduled | Existing event moved; current booking remains active. | Update CRM booking fields and reminders. |
| No Show — Rebook Pending | Caller attended but prospect did not. | Send rebook message and close task. |
| Attended — Outcome Pending | Call completed; outcome not yet recorded. | Set route before end of business day where practical. |
| Information Needed | One bounded fact set is required to determine route. | Await facts; close or reroute on due date. |
| Nurture | Valid potential need; not ready. | Set reason, consent basis and review date. |
| No Fit | No paid Heutrix path is recommended. | Set reason and close the lead. |
| Converted | A genuine paid opportunity exists. | Link opportunity and preserve source. |

## 9.3 Opportunity stages

| Stage | Definition | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified — Heutrix Diagnostics | Diagnostics is the recommended paid path. | Proposal issued or decision not to proceed. |
| Qualified — Workflow Transformation | Workflow Transformation scoping is supported. | Proposal issued, rerouted to Diagnostics or no-go. |
| Qualified — AI Guardrails | AI Guardrails scoping is supported. | Proposal issued or no-go. |
| Proposal Preparing | Heutrix is producing a written commercial scope. | Proposal sent date and version. |
| Proposal Sent | Prospect received the proposal. | Review outcome or agreed next date. |
| Decision Pending | Proposal is accurate and buyer-side decision remains. | Signed/accepted, declined or closed after the agreed process. |
| Closed Won | Written agreement and required commencement condition are met. | Record value, expected gross profit and start condition/date. |
| Closed Lost | Buyer declined or process closed without agreement. | Primary loss reason and close note. |

Do not place every attended call into an opportunity. No-fit, nurture and information-needed leads remain outside the paid pipeline until a real commercial path exists.

# 12. Owner decisions still required

These are operational facts, not copy preferences. The relevant owner should answer them before the booking experience is published.

## Launch-blocking

1. **Named caller:** Who is the primary caller for “See where Heutrix can help”? Confirm preferred public name, title/contribution area and permission to publish it.
2. **Backup and assignment:** Who covers absences, and does the scheduler assign a specific founder before confirmation?
3. **Call format:** Video, phone or visitor choice? Which approved platform and what phone-number handling is required?
4. **Scheduler:** Which platform and production booking URL will be used?
5. **Availability rules:** Confirm Australia/Sydney timezone, normal available days/hours, minimum notice, how far ahead times appear and any blackout handling.
6. **Reschedule/cancel rules:** Confirm cut-off, rebooking limits, cancellation handling and whether two no-shows close the enquiry.
7. **Two-business-day commitment:** Does it apply to request-mode review, booking questions, post-call written recommendations and general enquiries, or only specified items? Define the start time and holiday handling.
8. **Lead ownership:** Who monitors the inbox/CRM, handles failures and covers the owner when unavailable?
9. **Privacy operations:** Approve the collection notice, retention, authorised access, sensitive-data remediation and deletion process for the actual scheduler/CRM/email stack.

## Needed for a consistent no-fit and nurture experience

10. Which Heutrix DIY resources are approved to send without customisation?
11. Which referral providers, if any, are approved by category? If none, approve category-only guidance.
12. Is ongoing resource nurture offered? If yes, confirm consent wording, system, cadence and unsubscribe handling.
13. Who may approve Diagnostics, Workflow Transformation and AI Guardrails routes, proposals and exceptions?
14. What internal proposal turnaround can be honoured after the necessary information is complete? Do not publish one until confirmed.
15. What constitutes the written win condition: signed agreement, deposit/payment, or another commencement gate?

## Recommended answers to approve or amend

These are proposals, not current facts:

- Apply the two-business-day human commitment to request review and post-call written recommendation; keep booking confirmations automated and immediate.
- Default to video, with phone available on request, if both are operationally supported.
- Close a first no-show after one rebooking message and one two-business-day close-the-loop message; allow a fresh booking later.
- Send no ongoing nurture without separate permission and a specific next-review reason.
- Use category-only referrals until Heutrix has an approved, maintained referral list.

# 13. Release acceptance test

The journey is ready only when a production test lead can:

1. click a CTA whose wording matches the action;
2. submit the minimal form without sensitive information;
3. create exactly one CRM lead with source and landing page;
4. see real availability and confirm a time when **Book** is used;
5. receive a calendar event and email naming the caller, length, format, agenda, privacy boundary and reschedule link;
6. receive accurate reminders;
7. reschedule and cancel without manual confusion;
8. be marked attended or no-show;
9. receive one documented fit outcome and written next step within the approved interval; and
10. produce a complete, non-sensitive CRM record with route and reason codes for the analytics workstream.

Until all ten pass, keep the public CTA in **Request** mode and do not send meaningful traffic to the flow.
