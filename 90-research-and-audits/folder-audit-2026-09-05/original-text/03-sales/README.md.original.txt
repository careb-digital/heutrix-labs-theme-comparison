# Sales operating system

This area turns interest into an accountable decision: no fit, nurture, Diagnostics, Workflow Transformation or AI Guardrails.

The [detailed Sales journey specification](fit-call/SALES-PROCESS-SPECIFICATION.md) is maintained here as the current working process source, subject to its open approval gates. Marketing owns page copy and campaigns; it links to these operating requirements.

## Founder working guide
**Owner and backup:** [Central folder ownership register](../01-company/founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md#folder-ownership). These are deliberately unassigned. Janith coordinates task allocation and merges.

**Working scope:** Maintain qualification, follow-up, pipeline definitions and operating templates within approved offers, terms and delivery capacity.

**Current sources:** [Sales process specification](fit-call/SALES-PROCESS-SPECIFICATION.md), [fit-call status](fit-call/README.md), [lead handling](lead-capture/LEAD-HANDLING.md), [pipeline](crm-and-pipeline/PIPELINE.md), [proposal requirements](proposals/README.md). Configured systems and operating approvals remain open.

**Dependencies and review:** Coordinate lead journey changes with Marketing and the website maintainer, promises with Delivery, prices with Finance/Offers, and contract/data changes with Legal. Use the [shared dependency map](../00-control/DEPENDENCIES.md) to identify consumers before changing a rule.

**Complete when:** Qualification, stage definitions and handoff agree; templates use current rules; proposed or untested systems are not represented as live. Use synthetic leads for verification. Record evidence and affected-person sign-off before integration.

**Start and hand off:** Read the [collaboration workflow](../00-control/COLLABORATION.md), create a [change brief](../00-control/templates/CHANGE-BRIEF.md), and list it in [changes](changes/README.md). Keep current sources in the subject folders; change notes are work records, not alternative masters.
