# Heutrix LinkedIn launch content system

This folder contains the research, current strategy, activation-relative calendar, weekly post copy and publishing controls for launching the Heutrix LinkedIn Page.

## Start here

1. Read [01_Research_and_Market_Gaps.md](01_Research_and_Market_Gaps.md) for the research snapshot and September decision overlay.
2. Read [02_LinkedIn_Content_Strategy.md](02_LinkedIn_Content_Strategy.md) for the audience, message, editorial system and measurement plan.
3. Use [03_120_Day_Content_Plan.md](03_120_Day_Content_Plan.md) as the sequence, not a fixed-date schedule.
4. Complete [04_Human_Review_and_Publishing_Checklist.md](04_Human_Review_and_Publishing_Checklist.md) before scheduling.
5. Record the baseline and each result in [05_Campaign_Measurement_Log.md](05_Campaign_Measurement_Log.md).
6. Produce only the selected assets using the standalone ChatGPT Images and Gemini Nano Banana Pro prompt packs in [06_Visual_Generation_Prompts.md](06_Visual_Generation_Prompts.md).
7. Publish the first four weeks, review the signals, then adapt the content bank.

The approved version of any lead-magnet link must come from [`../../lead-magnets/`](../../lead-magnets/). Use the post-to-resource mapping in the content plan and keep download measurement separate from contact permission.

## Current campaign rules

- Do not assign dates until the outreach gates are closed and a publisher is named.
- Standard starting cadence: Monday, Wednesday and Friday; test the time in `Australia/Sydney` and review it after four weeks.
- Australian disability support providers are the primary audience. Allied health practices are secondary and receive dedicated content without equal launch weight.
- Use only the three current product names: **Heutrix Diagnostics**, **Heutrix Workflow Transformation** and **Heutrix AI Guardrails**.
- Do not publish prices, starting ranges or pricing-led calls to action. Model approval controls private quoting, not public publication.
- Use **20-minute workflow fit call**. Until the scheduler and lead path work end to end, use **See where Heutrix can help** or **Request my 20-minute call**, not **Book a call**.
- The public brand remains **Heutrix** in these drafts. The relationship to **Heutrix Labs** still requires an owner decision.
- The first 12 posts are the refined launch sprint. Weeks 5–18 are a content bank to adapt after real performance data exists.
- Link to one problem-matched lead magnet at a time. The post must still be useful without the download, and the download must not be treated as a client result or consent signal.
- P16 and P34 are prepared as anonymised Heutrix delivery posts. Do not publish either until the cited case-study claim, confidentiality and owner-approval gates are closed.
- The case-study collection contains 15 angles from one connected body of work for one Australian disability support provider. Never present those angles as 15 clients or reuse the evidence as allied-health proof.

The dated weekly filenames are retained for traceability to the original calendar. Dates inside the files are now activation-relative and control publication.

## Status meaning

- `draft_owner_review`: copy is strategically current but still needs the normal human review.
- `draft_asset_and_owner_review`: copy is current; the named asset and normal human review are still required.
- `draft_source_and_owner_review`: copy is current; re-open the primary source on publication day and complete human review.
- `owner_approval_required`: a specific claim, proof asset or commercial decision remains open.
- `conditional`: publish only if the stated condition is true and the copy is rewritten to match measured reality.

No status in this folder means that a post has been approved or published.

## Publishing discipline

- Make the first two lines earn attention with a recognisable operating tension.
- Teach one useful idea; do not turn every post into an advertisement.
- Never add invented clients, testimonials, results, savings, credentials or capacity claims.
- Label every synthetic or illustrative example in the first lines and on every related visual.
- Label an approved case-study post as anonymised Heutrix delivery, preserve the outcome's approximate wording and unit, and state the material evidence limitation.
- Never publish the source organisation's name, source screenshots, records or identifying wording. Client feedback is not a testimonial or an independent audit.
- Do not invite patient, participant, worker, client or other sensitive information in comments, messages or forms.
- Respond as a person and record useful audience language; do not run an automated sales sequence in comments.
- Review audience quality, saves, comments, Page actions and qualified conversations after Weeks 4, 8, 13 and 18 before extending the schedule.
