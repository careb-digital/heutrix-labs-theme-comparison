# 5. Image guidance

## Preferred trust asset: real founder portraits

A real image usually answers the human-proof question more strongly than an avatar. If the founders later approve portraits:

- Photograph all four founders in the same session with consistent lighting, crop and background.
- Use a natural head-and-shoulders or waist-up composition, direct eye contact and ordinary professional clothing; avoid staged boardroom clichés.
- Capture one group image plus individual 4:5 portraits and square crops.
- Export responsive WebP/AVIF versions from a high-resolution original; keep the original archived.
- Use name-and-role captions. Alt text should identify the person and relevant context, not describe appearance unnecessarily.
- Obtain written permission for website, social and proposal use.
- Do not include a client workplace, client records, screens, badges or documents unless permission and redaction are confirmed.

## Approved interim approach: founder avatars

The stated preference is avatars. Use them as honest illustrations, not as fabricated photographic proof:

- Commission four individually recognisable illustrations from founder-approved reference images.
- Keep one consistent style, background, crop and level of detail while preserving each founder's real distinguishing features.
- Label each image with the founder's name and contribution area; where helpful, use alt text such as “Illustrated portrait of Janith, Heutrix co-founder.”
- Do not add invented uniforms, security badges, clinical symbols, awards, employer logos or other authority cues.
- Do not use generic avatar-library characters or AI-generated photorealistic faces that could be mistaken for real portraits.
- Add a real candid group/workshop photograph later if all founders consent; this can offset the lower proof value of avatars.

## Optional real workspace/process photographs

Useful supporting images include:

- a founder facilitating a workflow-mapping session on a clean whiteboard;
- hands arranging a process map or acceptance checklist;
- a founder reviewing a synthetic-data dashboard with a colleague; and
- a training or handover moment using a demonstration environment.

Before shooting, replace all live information with synthetic data. Check whiteboards, browser tabs, notifications, document headers, calendars, badges and reflections for personal, client or commercially sensitive content. Do not use stock-team imagery.
