# Heutrix Labs website copy pack

This directory contains the controlled copy source for every public page on the Heutrix Labs website. Each Markdown file maps to one route and records the intended content hierarchy, visible labels, calls to action and supporting copy.

The consolidated cross-site decisions, migration rules, resource journeys and acceptance criteria are in [`WEBSITE-REFINEMENT-BRIEF-2026-09-03.md`](WEBSITE-REFINEMENT-BRIEF-2026-09-03.md).

**Aligned:** 4 September 2026  
**Latest decisions:** Remove pricing as a public website page, navigation item and CTA; prepare evidence-refreshed, anonymised Heutrix case-study placements behind a separate publication gate.  
**Publication state:** Version 1.0 resources were approved for website release on 3 September 2026 by JD as review owner and boundary reviewer. Case-study claim, confidentiality and owner approval remains open. Operational and legal gates still apply.
**Local implementation state:** The 4 September acquisition/UX refinement is integrated locally as a release candidate and has not been deployed. The evidence-refreshed case-study modules are prepared in this copy pack but are intentionally absent from `apps/website/` while publication approval remains open.

The copy pack is aligned to the current three-product architecture:

1. **Heutrix Diagnostics** — a paid, standalone decision product.
2. **Heutrix Workflow Transformation** — the primary implementation product for one bounded non-clinical workflow.
3. **Heutrix AI Guardrails** — a practical workplace AI governance product.

Australian disability support providers are the primary launch audience. Allied health practices are the secondary launch audience. GP clinics and broad “care-related provider” positioning are not part of the launch focus.

## Copy conventions

- Use Australian English.
- Lead general pages with Australian disability support providers; retain allied health as the secondary audience.
- Keep Heutrix Labs positioned as an operational workflow improvement and implementation partner.
- Make Heutrix Workflow Transformation the primary implementation product.
- Treat dashboards, trackers, forms, lightweight internal systems and automation as possible deliverables within Workflow Transformation, not separate products.
- Describe Heutrix Diagnostics as paid and independently useful, including when the recommendation is no build.
- Do not imply clinical, legal, audit, registration, privacy-advisory or regulatory services.
- Do not promise compliance, efficiency, audit or registration outcomes.
- Do not publish a pricing page, public price, starting range, pricing navigation label or pricing CTA. Commercial terms are provided only after fit and scope are understood.
- Redirect legacy `/pricing` traffic to `/services#how-engagements-are-agreed`; do not include `/pricing` in navigation, internal links, structured data or the sitemap.
- Do not name a tool, integration, founder, entity, insurer, service area or client result unless the corresponding fact is verified and approved.
- Label all hypothetical before/after examples and sample deliverables as illustrative or synthetic.
- Label approved case-study material as anonymised Heutrix delivery. State that the 15 angles come from one connected body of work for one provider, preserve every approximate unit and limitation, and never treat client feedback as a testimonial or independent audit.
- Omit an entire gated proof module while approval remains open; do not publish its implementation note or selectively remove caveats to make the section appear approved.
- Provide the complete approved lead-magnet guides and workbooks without an email gate. A download is not consent to contact the visitor.
- Keep download boundaries beside the files: use synthetic or appropriately de-identified information and never imply that a score, status or screening result is an organisational approval or client outcome.
- Keep Heutrix AI Guardrails guidance tied to approved use cases, information boundaries and accountable human review.
- Initial enquiry copy must ask users not to include personal or sensitive information.
- Name the public call **“See where Heutrix can help.”** Describe it as a 20-minute, no-obligation call to understand one problem and decide whether Heutrix is the right fit.
- Do not describe the action as a booking unless a working scheduler offers and confirms a real time. The neutral CTA may open a request flow; the form button must state **Request my 20-minute call** until direct scheduling is live.
- Do not show a submission-success message unless the lead destination confirms receipt.

## Resource integration map

| Surface | Primary resource | Intended next step |
|---|---|---|
| Home | 20-Minute Workflow Bottleneck Scorecard | Diagnostics when the priority is unclear; Workflow Transformation when one bounded workflow is already clear. |
| Disability Providers | Enquiry-to-Service-Start Visibility Starter Kit | Workflow Transformation when the template exposes recurring ownership, permission, handover or adoption gaps. |
| Allied Health | 20-Minute Workflow Bottleneck Scorecard | Compare referral, intake, reporting and document workflows before choosing a paid route. |
| AI Guardrails | AI Guardrails Staff Starter Pack | AI Guardrails when use is widespread or the starter boundaries are not enough. |
| Services | All three resources, matched to the three product decisions | Let visitors work through the problem before requesting a call. |
| Contact | Preserve resource/source context without requesting an uploaded or completed workbook | Discuss only the high-level conclusion in the fit call. |

The complete PDF guides and workbooks remain ungated. A download is not permission to contact the visitor and is not evidence of a client outcome, approved workflow or approved AI use.
