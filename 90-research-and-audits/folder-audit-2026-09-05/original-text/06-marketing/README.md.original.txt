# Marketing

**Updated:** 4 September 2026

This area holds the approved voice and design direction, current website copy, channel plans, outreach assets and honest proof.

Marketing must follow the three-product architecture and disability-primary launch position. It must not publish unresolved prices, tool capabilities, business particulars, legal assurances or case-study claims that have not passed their evidence, confidentiality and owner-approval gates.

## Current systems

- [`website-copy/`](website-copy/) controls the page-by-page public copy.
- [`lead-magnets/`](lead-magnets/) contains the three complete-draft guides and workbooks, release gate, operating standard and verification manifest.
- [`linkedin/2026-launch/`](linkedin/2026-launch/) controls the launch content sequence and resource-linked post slots.
- [`outreach/`](outreach/) controls targeted resource use in direct outreach.
- [`case-studies-and-assets/`](case-studies-and-assets/) contains the 15 evidence-refreshed, anonymised Heutrix case-study angles, their source-evidence map and publication controls.
- [`case-studies-and-assets/PROOF-ASSET-REGISTER.md`](case-studies-and-assets/PROOF-ASSET-REGISTER.md) separates Heutrix-created method assets, delivery evidence, client-feedback support and material that is not yet approved for publication.

Lead magnets are ungated method resources. They do not create contact permission and must not be presented as client outcomes, tailored advice or organisational approvals.

The case studies describe one connected body of work delivered by Heutrix for one Australian disability support provider; they are not evidence of 15 clients or engagements. The source organisation must remain unnamed, no identifying source material may be published, and client feedback must not be presented as a testimonial or independent audit. Website, LinkedIn and outreach use must preserve each claim's approximate wording, unit and documented limitation.

## Case-study alignment coverage

| Marketing area | Alignment treatment |
|---|---|
| Case studies and proof registers | Evidence-refreshed source set and claim boundaries |
| Website copy | Gated proof modules prepared for Home, Disability Providers, Services, About, FAQ and AI Guardrails; Allied Health remains illustrative |
| LinkedIn | P16 and P34 prepared as anonymised delivery posts; other synthetic posts retained where no matching delivery evidence exists |
| Outreach | Case-study selection, product linkage, confidentiality and no-testimonial rules added |
| Brand voice | Case-study attribution, unit, evidence and anonymity rules added |
| Lead magnets | No content change required; they remain method resources rather than client proof |
| Brand design | No content change required; any future case visual must follow the same confidentiality and source-material restrictions |

The 3 September 2026 website decision removes pricing as a public page and conversion path. Website journeys should instead move between problem recognition, one relevant ungated resource, the three-product explanation and the 20-minute workflow fit-call request.

## Founder working guide
**Owner and backup:** [Central folder ownership register](../01-company/founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md#folder-ownership). These are deliberately unassigned. Janith coordinates task allocation and merges.

**Working scope:** Improve copy, assets and campaign drafts within current positioning, evidence and publication permissions.

**Current sources:** [Website copy](website-copy/README.md), [voice](brand/VOICE.md), [proof register](case-studies-and-assets/PROOF-ASSET-REGISTER.md), [resource manifest](lead-magnets/RELEASE-MANIFEST.md), [LinkedIn plan](linkedin/2026-launch/README.md). Publication and measurement gates remain separate.

**Dependencies and review:** Check claims with their source owners, offer statements with Offers, lead handling with Sales/Privacy, and website source/distribution changes with the app maintainer. Use the [shared dependency map](../00-control/DEPENDENCIES.md) to identify consumers before changing a rule.

**Complete when:** Claims preserve evidence limits, product/CTA wording matches the actual journey, publication status is explicit, and rebuilt resources pass their existing release checks. Record evidence and affected-person sign-off before integration.

**Start and hand off:** Read the [collaboration workflow](../00-control/COLLABORATION.md), create a [change brief](../00-control/templates/CHANGE-BRIEF.md), and list it in [changes](changes/README.md). Keep current sources in the subject folders; change notes are work records, not alternative masters.
