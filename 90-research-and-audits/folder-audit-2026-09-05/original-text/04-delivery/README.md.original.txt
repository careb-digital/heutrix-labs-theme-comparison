# Delivery system

This area holds the repeatable method for delivering the three Heutrix products and proving acceptance before handover.

Use synthetic or de-identified information during design and testing wherever possible. A client project starts only when scope, price, terms, access, owner and data-handling arrangements are approved.

## Founder working guide
**Owner and backup:** [Central folder ownership register](../01-company/founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md#folder-ownership). These are deliberately unassigned. Janith coordinates task allocation and merges.

**Working scope:** Improve methods, checklists, reusable templates and synthetic demonstrations within the approved service scope.

**Current sources:** [Diagnostics status](diagnostics/README.md), [Workflow Transformation](workflow-transformation/README.md), [templates](templates/README.md), and [client project structure](../08-client-project-template/README.md). The legacy Diagnostics native pack is quarantined pending reissue.

**Dependencies and review:** Coordinate scope, timelines and start/acceptance rules with Sales and Offers; capacity and effort with Finance; IP, defect and data rules with Legal. Use the [shared dependency map](../00-control/DEPENDENCIES.md) to identify consumers before changing a rule.

**Complete when:** Scope and acceptance agree with approved terms, synthetic walkthroughs pass where affected, and native files and exports match. Editing a draft does not approve it. Record evidence and affected-person sign-off before integration.

**Start and hand off:** Read the [collaboration workflow](../00-control/COLLABORATION.md), create a [change brief](../00-control/templates/CHANGE-BRIEF.md), and list it in [changes](changes/README.md). Keep current sources in the subject folders; change notes are work records, not alternative masters.
