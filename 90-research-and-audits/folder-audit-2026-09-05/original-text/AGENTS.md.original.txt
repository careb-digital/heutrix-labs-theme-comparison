# Heutrix workspace instructions

These instructions apply to the entire repository unless a more specific `AGENTS.md` or `AGENTS.override.md` is added closer to a task.

## Mission

Move Heutrix from launch preparation to a state where it can responsibly reach out to prospective customers, qualify opportunities, contract and onboard suitable paid work, deliver it consistently and learn from the result.

Optimise for the next verified business outcome, not document volume.

## Read before changing material

1. Read `README.md`.
2. Read `00-control/LAUNCH-BOARD.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `VERIFIED-FACTS.md` and `RISKS-AND-BLOCKERS.md`.
3. Read the relevant workstream README and the latest dated source material.
4. Read `00-control/COLLABORATION.md`, `00-control/DEPENDENCIES.md` and the folder ownership section of `01-company/founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md`.
5. Check `git status --short` and preserve unrelated or pre-existing changes.

## Source precedence

When sources conflict, use this order:

1. a direct, dated decision from Janith;
2. the current verified public, contractual or operating asset;
3. a recent approved strategy or operating document;
4. older notes, research, audits or generated reports;
5. an AI recommendation or inference; and
6. an external result that has not been adopted.

Do not promote a recommendation into a business fact. Record unresolved conflicts in `00-control/DECISIONS.md` or `00-control/RISKS-AND-BLOCKERS.md`.

## Business facts and positioning

- Heutrix improves operational workflows using practical systems and safe AI where appropriate.
- Use a workflow-first, technology-second approach.
- The primary launch segment is Australian disability support providers.
- Allied health practices are the secondary launch segment.
- GP clinics are a future segment, not a launch segment.
- The offer architecture is Heutrix Diagnostics, Heutrix Workflow Transformation and Heutrix AI Guardrails.
- Workflow Transformation is the primary implementation product.
- Do not publish a pricing page, public price, starting range, pricing navigation item or pricing CTA. Keep fees private in the written post-fit scope; owner approval of the pricing and margin model controls quoting, not public publication.
- Use Australian English and a direct, practical, privacy-aware voice.

## Claims and safety boundaries

- Do not imply legal, privacy, clinical, registration, audit, compliance or regulatory advice or outcomes.
- Do not promise integrations, supported systems, time savings, revenue, adoption, uninterrupted services or compliance without verified evidence and a written scope.
- Clearly label synthetic and illustrative examples; never present them as client outcomes.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, credentials, entity details, insurance, addresses, team members or delivery capacity.
- Treat legal documents as drafts until the legal readiness and review registers say they are approved for use.

## Information handling

- Never add real client, participant, worker, health, credential, payment or other sensitive information to this repository.
- Use synthetic or appropriately de-identified data for builds, demonstrations and acceptance tests.
- Keep secrets in approved environment or credential systems, never in Markdown, source code, spreadsheets or committed `.env` files.
- Confirm approved storage, collaboration, access, retention and deletion arrangements before onboarding real work.
- The `08-client-project-template/` directory is a reusable structure only. Create live client work in an owner-approved secure location outside this Git repository.

## File placement

- Current launch control and cross-workstream truth: `00-control/`.
- Company identity and readiness evidence: `01-company/`.
- Market, positioning, offers and pricing policy: `02-market-and-offers/`.
- Lead handling and commercial process: `03-sales/`.
- Delivery assets and methods: `04-delivery/`.
- Legal, privacy, risk and review evidence: `05-legal-privacy-risk/`.
- Public messaging and campaign assets: `06-marketing/`.
- Financial models and assumptions: `07-finance/`.
- Application code: `apps/`.
- Historical or superseded input: `90-research-and-audits/` or `99-archive/`.

Do not create new loose files at the repository root unless they govern the whole workspace.

## Change discipline

- Update the relevant control register when a change confirms a fact, closes a risk or makes an owner decision.
- Add dates, owners, evidence links and acceptance criteria to launch-critical work.
- Keep source, draft, approved and issued artefacts visibly distinct.
- Prefer links to the authoritative source instead of duplicating copy.
- If changing public copy, check the active website and the website copy pack together.
- If changing prices, scope, timelines, warranties or data handling, review the offer, legal, sales, delivery and finance implications together.
- Preserve nested Git repositories under `apps/internal-reports/` until the owner chooses whether to keep, archive or consolidate their histories.

## Founder task boundaries

- Janith coordinates merges. Folder owners remain unassigned until recorded in the central role register; do not infer assignments from operational roles.
- Start a bounded change brief from `00-control/templates/CHANGE-BRIEF.md` in the workstream's `changes/` folder and maintain its index. Record source decisions, base commit, affected paths, checks and review evidence.
- Keep shared business context in the repository. Do not rely on private chats or parent-folder personal context as shared authority.
- Reserve native Office masters through `00-control/FILE-EDIT-REGISTER.md` before editing. A private branch reservation is not an active lock.
- Route changes to shared scope, prices, timelines, claims, terms or data handling through affected-person review and Janith's integration workflow. Routine work already allocated within approved rules should proceed without repeated permission requests.
- The current Sales process text master is `03-sales/fit-call/SALES-PROCESS-SPECIFICATION.md`; the former Marketing file is a pointer and the legacy Delivery native pack remains quarantined.
- Do not describe local templates as enforced remote permissions or assume the uncommitted folder migration exists in a founder's remote clone.

## Verification

For website changes, work in `apps/website/` and run `npm.cmd run build`. Test the lead form and key routes in a browser when changing conversion or navigation behaviour.

For document or model changes, verify the native file and rendered/exported version where applicable. Re-run the relevant audit tool stored beside the finance workstream when changing a financial model.

Before finishing:

- confirm moved or renamed paths are reflected in navigation and links;
- check `git status --short`;
- report tests performed and any test that could not be run; and
- state remaining owner decisions rather than guessing.
