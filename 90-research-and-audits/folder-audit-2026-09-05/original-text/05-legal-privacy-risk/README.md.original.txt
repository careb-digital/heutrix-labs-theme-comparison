# Legal, privacy and risk

This area separates legal masters from public website policies, operational data handling and review evidence.

Documents here are not legally approved merely because they exist or render correctly. The current client legal pack is a substantial draft and must pass the legal review register before use with paid work.

## Founder working guide
**Owner and backup:** [Central folder ownership register](../01-company/founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md#folder-ownership). These are deliberately unassigned. Janith coordinates task allocation and merges.

**Working scope:** Maintain draft controls, review evidence and documented handling rules without inventing adviser approval or verified company particulars.

**Current sources:** [Legal review register](review-register/LEGAL-REVIEW-REGISTER.md), [legal masters](legal-masters/README.md), [data handling](data-handling/DATA-HANDLING-STANDARD.md), [website policies](website-policies/README.md). Approval comes from recorded review, not file presence.

**Dependencies and review:** Route changes to every affected operator and the appropriate adviser required by existing review gates. Coordinate public notices with Marketing and the website maintainer. Use the [shared dependency map](../00-control/DEPENDENCIES.md) to identify consumers before changing a rule.

**Complete when:** Version, status and approval evidence are explicit; draft and approved artefacts remain distinct; changed native masters and exports agree. Record evidence and affected-person sign-off before integration.

**Start and hand off:** Read the [collaboration workflow](../00-control/COLLABORATION.md), create a [change brief](../00-control/templates/CHANGE-BRIEF.md), and list it in [changes](changes/README.md). Keep current sources in the subject folders; change notes are work records, not alternative masters.
