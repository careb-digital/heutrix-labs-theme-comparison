## Founder working guide
**Owner and backup:** [Central folder ownership register](../01-company/founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md#folder-ownership). These are deliberately unassigned. Janith coordinates task allocation and merges.

**Working scope:** Improve market research and offer documentation without silently changing the approved launch market, scope or pricing rules.

**Current sources:** [Offer decision](offers/offer-strategy-2026-09/01-offer-decision-document.md), [flagship specification](offers/offer-strategy-2026-09/02-flagship-offer-specification.md), [owner validation](offers/offer-strategy-2026-09/05-owner-validation-register.md), [pricing policy](pricing/PRICING-POLICY.md). Research recommendations remain proposals until adopted.

**Dependencies and review:** Review product, scope and pricing changes with Sales, Delivery, Finance and Legal; coordinate resulting public wording with Marketing. Use the [shared dependency map](../00-control/DEPENDENCIES.md) to identify consumers before changing a rule.

**Complete when:** Product names and boundaries agree across consumers, internal price assumptions retain their status, and research is distinguished from approved strategy. Record evidence and affected-person sign-off before integration.

**Start and hand off:** Read the [collaboration workflow](../00-control/COLLABORATION.md), create a [change brief](../00-control/templates/CHANGE-BRIEF.md), and list it in [changes](changes/README.md). Keep current sources in the subject folders; change notes are work records, not alternative masters.
