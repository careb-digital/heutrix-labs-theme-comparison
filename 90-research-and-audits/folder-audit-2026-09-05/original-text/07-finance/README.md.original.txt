# Finance and capacity

This area holds the internal economics needed to quote safely and plan a realistic launch: price/margin rules, forecasts and operating budget.

Financial models support decisions; they do not become commitments until their assumptions, owner and approval date are recorded.

## Current integrated planning model

- [`Heutrix-Integrated-Forecast-Budget-and-Margin-Plan.xlsx`](outputs/01a064fc-3a18-7ef1-acac-173f1f26e409/Heutrix-Integrated-Forecast-Budget-and-Margin-Plan.xlsx) is the current working model, dated 2026-09-03.
- It separates consultant effort from offshore developer effort, uses the owner-supplied $80–$100 and $30–$35 hourly ranges, and stress-tests prices at the top of both ranges.
- It contains offer unit economics, three forecast scenarios, a quarterly base case, a detailed Australian operating budget, tax/GST and working-capital planning, role-specific capacity, model checks and a source register.
- Prices, scope hours, volumes, entity/GST treatment, insurance, contractor terms and offshore data access remain internal and provisional until the relevant owner, accountant or legal approval is recorded.
- The workbook's “before quoting or publication” approval note is conservative but no longer the public rule: prices and starting ranges remain off the public website and campaign even after model approval. Approval permits private quoting under a written scope.

The older forecast and pricing artefacts remain available as historical analysis, but they are superseded for current planning because they do not use the integrated consultant/developer cost model. Do not quote from or publish their price recommendations.

## Founder working guide
**Owner and backup:** [Central folder ownership register](../01-company/founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md#folder-ownership). These are deliberately unassigned. Janith coordinates task allocation and merges.

**Working scope:** Maintain forecasts, scenarios and cost evidence; propose commercial changes without treating model outputs as approved prices or commitments.

**Current sources:** The current workbook and provisional assumptions are identified above. [Pricing policy](../02-market-and-offers/pricing/PRICING-POLICY.md) controls permitted pricing use; historical analyses are not the current model.

**Dependencies and review:** Confirm effort/capacity assumptions with Delivery, commercial rules with Offers/Sales, and entity/payment/tax assumptions with Company and relevant advisers. Use the [shared dependency map](../00-control/DEPENDENCIES.md) to identify consumers before changing a rule.

**Complete when:** Reserve the native workbook, validate changed formulas and assumptions, check native/export consistency, and use the applicable audit beside the model or report. Do not use an older forecast/report audit as proof that a different workbook passed. Record evidence and affected-person sign-off before integration.

**Start and hand off:** Read the [collaboration workflow](../00-control/COLLABORATION.md), create a [change brief](../00-control/templates/CHANGE-BRIEF.md), and list it in [changes](changes/README.md). Keep current sources in the subject folders; change notes are work records, not alternative masters.
