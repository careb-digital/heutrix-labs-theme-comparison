# Company readiness

Store verified evidence needed to represent and operate Heutrix as a business. This area should answer: who is contracting, who is accountable, what cover exists and which systems are approved.

- `identity-and-registrations/` — verified legal/trading details and registrations.
- `founders-and-roles/` — approved biographies and operational accountability.
- `insurance/` — current policies, certificates, renewal dates and approved claims.
- `approved-systems/` — systems approved for business and client work.

Do not publish or copy placeholders from this area into contracts.

## Founder working guide
**Owner and backup:** [Central folder ownership register](../01-company/founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md#folder-ownership). These are deliberately unassigned. Janith coordinates task allocation and merges.

**Working scope:** Maintain readiness evidence, role records and system descriptions within approved decisions.

**Current sources:** [Identity checklist](identity-and-registrations/IDENTITY-CHECKLIST.md), [role register](founders-and-roles/ROLE-REGISTER.md), [systems register](approved-systems/APPROVED-SYSTEMS.md), [insurance register](insurance/INSURANCE-REGISTER.md). All approval/evidence gaps remain visible in those records.

**Dependencies and review:** Coordinate entity and signing changes with Legal and Finance, public particulars with Marketing, and system/access changes with Privacy and affected users. Use the [shared dependency map](../00-control/DEPENDENCIES.md) to identify consumers before changing a rule.

**Complete when:** Evidence links resolve, placeholders are not presented as verified particulars, and affected contract/public references agree. Record evidence and affected-person sign-off before integration.

**Start and hand off:** Read the [collaboration workflow](../00-control/COLLABORATION.md), create a [change brief](../00-control/templates/CHANGE-BRIEF.md), and list it in [changes](changes/README.md). Keep current sources in the subject folders; change notes are work records, not alternative masters.
