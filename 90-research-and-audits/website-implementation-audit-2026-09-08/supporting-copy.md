We started inside
the work.

Heutrix grew from first-hand experience running a disability and allied health services organisation—and a practical question: how can the work behind care run more clearly?

In provider operations, a task rarely ends with one person. An enquiry needs a next step. A new worker needs usable access. A payment query needs the right information. Between each action is a handover that someone has to carry through.

That perspective shaped Heutrix. We focus on the everyday details that make a process work: clear ownership, information people can find and a shared understanding of what “finished” means.

EXPERIENCE BROUGHT TOGETHER

From understanding
the pressure to
improving the process.

Our founding team brings together provider administration and operations, project delivery, workflow implementation, data and analytics, and backend engineering and infrastructure.

Those experiences meet in one approach: understand the people doing the work, trace where the process becomes difficult and build an improvement the team can use.

The workflow examples explore what that could look like in practice. Worker onboarding carries through to accepted access. Reminders include a clear place to act. Follow-up actions have an owner and a review path. Each change addresses a specific point where work can stall.

Today, we help Australian disability support providers and selected allied health practices improve one workflow at a time, with the same principle that shaped Heutrix from the start: workflow first, technology second.

Where work gets stuck

An account invitation can be sent while access, guidance or handover tasks are still incomplete.

A possible improvement

Agree what ready means for each role. Track the invitation, acceptance, approved permissions and handover separately. Keep the approved worker record in its existing system.

What to test

Test a normal start, an unaccepted invitation, incorrect permissions and a delayed start. Give each exception an owner and a next action.

How to assess progress

Track incomplete starts, repeated follow-ups and time spent clarifying ownership before and after an agreed change. Agree the measures before implementation; no time saving or financial result is assumed.

### Expanded FAQ content

What does Heutrix Labs do?
expand_less

Heutrix Labs offers three practical services: Heutrix Diagnostics to identify and prioritise operational problems, Heutrix Workflow Transformation to redesign and implement one bounded workflow, and Heutrix AI Guard to establish responsible workplace AI rules. We focus on the operational systems around care and service delivery, not clinical decision-making.

Who does Heutrix Labs work with?
expand_less

We primarily work with Australian disability support providers, and with selected allied health practices. We can help where recurring intake, referral, reporting or document work crosses people or systems and needs clearer ownership or visibility. The workflow and your team’s readiness matter more than organisation size.

What is the best starting point?
expand_less

Choose Heutrix Diagnostics when the priority, workflow boundary, baseline or feasible solution is unclear. Choose Heutrix Workflow Transformation when one workflow, owner, users and desired result can be bounded and tested. Choose Heutrix AI Guard when workplace AI needs approved uses, information boundaries, accountable review, escalation and staff guidance. You do not need to select a product before the introductory conversation.

Do we need Heutrix Diagnostics first?
expand_less

Not always. You can move directly to Heutrix Workflow Transformation when the workflow, owner, users, desired result, systems and access path are clear enough to create a fixed scope and acceptance criteria. When priorities or feasibility are uncertain, Diagnostics creates a decision-ready plan that remains useful even if you do not proceed with Heutrix.

How long does a typical project take?
expand_less

Heutrix Workflow Transformation is typically scoped for 2–4 weeks after the written scope, access, decision-makers and prerequisites are ready. Client delay, third-party changes, additional requirements or unresolved information issues may change timing through written change control. Diagnostics and AI Guardrails timing is confirmed in their written scope.

Do you build software?
expand_less

Heutrix may configure or build a form, tracker, register, management view, automation or lightweight internal workflow tool where it is the simplest suitable intervention. These are deliverables within Workflow Transformation, not separate products or a promise to build a large bespoke platform.

Can you work with our existing tools?
expand_less

Heutrix first assesses approved existing systems where feasible. Every tool, integration, export, licence, permission, vendor dependency and information path must be checked before it is included. Naming a product does not guarantee capability, native integration or support for every feature.

Do you replace practice, client or case management software?
expand_less

No. Heutrix Labs does not replace your core practice, client or case management system. We help improve the operational workflows around your existing tools where appropriate.

Can you automate workflows involving sensitive information?
expand_less

Possibly, but only where the purpose, minimum necessary information, tool, permissions, access, storage, retention and review process are understood and approved by the client. De-identified or sample data should be used for design and testing where practical.

Can staff use ChatGPT or other AI tools with client, patient or participant information?
expand_less

Do not enter personal or sensitive information into publicly available AI tools. Any proposed AI use involving personal information needs an organisation-approved process covering the purpose, tool, access, storage, retention, privacy obligations and accountable human review.

Does AI replace staff review?
expand_less

No. AI may assist with approved internal admin, drafting or workflow support, but a suitable person remains responsible for checking the source information, context, accuracy, tone and final use. AI does not replace clinical judgement, legal advice, professional responsibility or organisational accountability.

Do you provide legal advice?
expand_less

No. Heutrix Labs does not provide legal advice.

Do you provide clinical advice?
expand_less

No. Heutrix Labs does not provide clinical advice.

Do you promise compliance outcomes?
expand_less

No. Heutrix Labs does not promise compliance outcomes, audit success, registration outcomes or regulatory approval. Our work can support better operational systems, but the client remains responsible for reviewing, approving and maintaining their own processes.

Do you provide NDIS registration readiness or mock audits?
expand_less

No. Heutrix Labs does not provide NDIS registration readiness, mock audits, official audit certification or regulatory approval. Heutrix Labs may support operational workflow tools for disability support providers and other regulated providers where the work is within scope.

What happens after handover?
expand_less

The agreed handover may include operating instructions, ownership and maintenance notes, known limitations and staff guidance. Optional follow-up support or further improvements can be scoped separately when needed.

What do you need from us?
expand_less

We usually need access to the people who understand the workflow, examples of current forms or trackers, a list of systems involved, and a decision-maker who can approve scope and priorities. Please do not send patient, participant, client, clinical, Medicare, NDIS, diagnostic or sensitive information unless we have agreed a secure and appropriate process.
