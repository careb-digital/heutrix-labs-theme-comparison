# Lead-magnet operating standard

**Effective status:** working draft  
**Owner:** to assign before publication  
**Last updated:** 3 September 2026

## Purpose

Use the lead magnets to help a prospective buyer make a small, useful operating decision and to learn which workflow problems deserve further attention. Optimise for qualified understanding, not raw download volume.

## Journey

1. A relevant website, LinkedIn or outreach message links to one specific resource.
2. The visitor downloads the complete PDF or workbook without submitting contact details.
3. The resource produces an immediate result and explains its limits.
4. A voluntary follow-up route invites a high-level, non-sensitive workflow fit call.
5. The call may route to Heutrix Diagnostics, Heutrix Workflow Transformation, Heutrix AI Guardrails, more evidence gathering or no project.

Do not turn the resource into free bespoke consulting. Do not ask the visitor to upload a completed workbook, screenshots, prompts, records or sensitive information through the public lead path.

## Resource positioning

| Resource | Primary problem | Immediate value | Do not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workflow Bottleneck Scorecard | Too many operational problems and no rational first priority. | A comparable score, evidence-confidence check and control flag across up to 12 workflows. | A business case, ROI forecast, automation recommendation or guaranteed priority. |
| Enquiry-to-Service-Start Visibility Starter Kit | Owner, status, next action, ageing, exception or readiness decisions are hard to see. | Minimum fields, status definitions, synthetic test records and a short review rhythm. | An approved client-data store, service-readiness assurance, compliance model or promised system design. |
| AI Guardrails Staff Starter Pack | Staff need practical boundaries for everyday administrative AI use. | A decision screen, staff checklists and use-case register. | Tool approval, legal/privacy advice, approved workplace policy or transfer of accountability to AI. |

## Channel use

### Website

- Keep the resource page in the main and footer navigation.
- Feature the scorecard on the homepage as the lowest-friction general starting point.
- Link sector-specific content to the most relevant resource rather than offering all three every time.
- Put the information boundary beside the download, not only in a footer or terms page.

### LinkedIn

- Teach the underlying idea in the post; the download should deepen it rather than replace the post.
- Use the scorecard with workflow-prioritisation posts, the visibility kit with enquiry/service-start field guides and the staff pack with practical AI-use posts.
- Never present downloads, completion rates or comments as evidence of client outcomes.

### Direct outreach

- Send a resource only when it matches a researched operating issue.
- Do not attach unsolicited files; link to the controlled website resource page once it is approved and live.
- Do not use a download as consent to add a person to a sequence or newsletter.

## Sales handoff

When a person voluntarily requests a fit call after using a resource, record only:

- lead source and resource ID;
- organisation, role and sector;
- one high-level workflow or AI-use problem;
- the reader's self-reported result in general terms;
- fit-call outcome, product route or no-fit reason; and
- next action, owner and date.

Do not ask for the completed workbook or any identifiable client, participant, patient, worker or credential information during public lead capture.

## Measurement

Do not activate tracking until the analytics, consent and privacy approach is approved and tested.

| Stage | Measure | Decision supported |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Relevant resource-page views by source and audience context | Is the right audience seeing the resource? |
| Use | PDF and workbook downloads by resource and source | Which problem earns deeper engagement? |
| Intent | Voluntary fit-call requests that reference a resource | Does the resource create a relevant next step? |
| Quality | Qualified calls, product route and no-fit reason | Which resource attracts suitable problems? |
| Learning | Repeated high-level questions, terminology and control flags | What should Heutrix improve in the resource, offer or message? |

Do not optimise on download volume alone. A smaller number of disability-provider operations leaders who use the resource and identify a bounded problem is more valuable than broad low-fit traffic.

## Review and change control

Review each resource:

- before first publication;
- after the first 25 attributable downloads or five qualified follow-ups, whichever occurs first;
- when the offer architecture, public CTA, legal boundary, privacy approach or AI guidance changes;
- after a material error, near miss or misleading interpretation; and
- at least every six months while published.

Record the version, change, owner, evidence and next review in the folder README. Replace all website-distributed copies together and test every link after release.
