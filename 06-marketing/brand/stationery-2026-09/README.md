# Heutrix email signature and business card templates

Created 16 September 2026 using the selected layered logo and current [brand palette](../DESIGN.md), with navy/teal treatment from the [social banners](../linkedin-company-v2/README.md). Existing assets are preserved.

## Start here

**Additional colour options:** [Four business card variations](colour-variations/preview.html) provides Social teal, Light & navy, Deep teal and Clean white designs, with editable details and front/back SVG downloads. These use the existing approved logo variants and brand colours. SVGs are 90 × 55 mm at trim size; printing requires personalised details, printer-added 3 mm bleed and colour proofing. Browser rendering and personalisation checked; all four pairs visually inspected.

Open [template.html](template.html) in a browser. Enter a team member's name, approved role, phone and email, then download their signature and card SVGs. Blank phone removes it from the signature. Changes are local and are not saved automatically.

- [Email signature HTML](email-signature.html): table-based, live text, Arial fallback and a 160px logo. Replace placeholders before use. Copy the rendered signature into email settings; insert `assets/heutrix-logo-colour.png` through the email editor or provide an approved public HTTPS logo URL. A local image path is for preview only. Test a received message before use; Outlook/Gmail and dark-mode rendering have not been tested.
- [Business card front](business-card-front.svg) and [back](business-card-back.svg): editable SVG masters, 90 × 55 mm. The logo remains outlined; other text is editable Arial. Longer names are reduced automatically in the editor; review legibility before printing.
- [PDF proof](business-card-proof.pdf): two pages, 96 × 61 mm including 3 mm bleed; trim box is 90 × 55 mm. Contains placeholders. It is an RGB proof, not a personalised production file or PDF/X master. Have the printer apply their colour profile, verify small text and export the final personalised artwork with bleed. The supplied high-resolution logo exceeds 300 dpi at its placed size.

The verified general email is used; no personal contact details, unverified website URL or public social handles are invented. The business name follows the owner-supplied particulars. Typography uses email-safe Arial/Helvetica for portability; the wordmark is the original artwork.
