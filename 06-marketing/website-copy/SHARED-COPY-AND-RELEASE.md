## Copy conventions

- Use Australian English.
- Lead general pages with Australian disability support providers; retain allied health as the secondary audience.
- Keep Heutrix Labs positioned as an operational workflow improvement and implementation partner.
- Make Heutrix Workflow Transformation the primary implementation product.
- Treat dashboards, trackers, forms, lightweight internal systems and automation as possible deliverables within Workflow Transformation, not separate products.
- Describe Heutrix Diagnostics as paid and independently useful, including when the recommendation is no build.
- Do not imply clinical, legal, audit, registration, privacy-advisory or regulatory services.
- Do not promise compliance, efficiency, audit or registration outcomes.
- Do not publish a pricing page, public price, starting range, pricing navigation label or pricing CTA. Commercial terms are provided only after fit and scope are understood.
- Redirect legacy `/pricing` traffic to `/services#how-engagements-are-agreed`; do not include `/pricing` in navigation, internal links, structured data or the sitemap.
- Do not name a tool, integration, founder, entity, insurer, service area or client result unless the corresponding fact is verified and approved.
- Label all hypothetical before/after examples and sample deliverables as illustrative or synthetic.
- Keep approved case-study material anonymised. State that the 15 case studies span four providers, preserve every approximate unit and limitation, and never treat client feedback as a testimonial or independent audit. A separate on-page “anonymised Heutrix delivery” label is not required for the homepage feature following the owner’s 9 September 2026 direction; its client-feedback attribution and controlled source evidence remain unchanged.
- Omit an entire gated proof module while approval remains open; do not publish its implementation note or selectively remove caveats to make the section appear approved.
- Provide the complete approved lead-magnet guides and workbooks without an email gate. A download is not consent to contact the visitor.
- Keep download boundaries beside the files: use synthetic or appropriately de-identified information and never imply that a score, status or screening result is an organisational approval or client outcome.
- Keep Heutrix AI Guardrails guidance tied to approved use cases, information boundaries and accountable human review.
- Initial enquiry copy must ask users not to include personal or sensitive information.
- Name the public call **“Request a free consultation.”** Describe it as a 20-minute, no-obligation call to understand one problem and decide whether Heutrix is the right fit.
- Do not describe the action as a booking unless a working scheduler offers and confirms a real time. The neutral CTA may open a request flow; the form button must state **Request my 20-minute call** until direct scheduling is live.
- Do not show a submission-success message unless the lead destination confirms receipt.
