# Website disclaimer

This website describes Heutrix Diagnostics, Heutrix Workflow Transformation and Heutrix AI Guardrails for Australian disability support providers and selected allied health practices.

## Service boundaries

Heutrix Labs does not provide audit certification, legal advice, clinical advice, privacy advice, regulatory approval, NDIS registration readiness or mock audits.

Operational tools may support a client's processes but do not verify compliance or remove the client's responsibility to obtain suitable advice and to review, approve, apply and maintain its own processes.

## “Request a free consultation” call

The 20-minute, no-obligation call is a general discussion to understand one operational problem and assess whether Heutrix may be the right fit. It does not provide workflow design, technical architecture, legal, clinical, privacy, regulatory or compliance advice and does not create a service engagement.

## Illustrative examples and timing

Any workflow view, before/after pattern, sample handover or synthetic demonstration is illustrative unless expressly identified as verified client evidence. It does not show a client result or promise the same fields, system, timing or outcome.

A typical 2–4 week reference applies only to a scoped Heutrix Workflow Transformation engagement after the written prerequisites are ready. It is not a guaranteed completion date. Diagnostics and AI Guardrails timing is confirmed in writing.

## Case studies and quantified examples

The 15 anonymised case studies describe work completed across four unnamed Australian disability support providers. Individual case studies must not be interpreted as separate clients or engagements.

Where an approximate outcome is attributed to client feedback, it is supporting evidence rather than an independent audit, guaranteed result or verbatim testimonial. Future results depend on the scoped workflow, baseline, users, systems, information, dependencies and client responsibilities. No source record, screenshot or identifying detail should be treated as approved for publication unless expressly stated.

## Resources, templates and synthetic examples

Downloadable resources are general operational starting points. They are not tailored advice and do not show a client outcome. A score, status or screening result is not an approved workflow, business case, service-readiness decision, approved AI use or assurance of compliance.

Use general, synthetic or appropriately de-identified information. Do not enter participant, patient, worker, clinical, credential, payment, confidential or other personal or sensitive information into these files or an unapproved system.

## Engagement terms and third-party systems

Website descriptions are not quotes or project scopes. Paid work begins only after the deliverables, responsibilities, assumptions, dependencies, exclusions, timing, third-party costs, acceptance criteria, change triggers and applicable fee are agreed in writing. Product or platform names do not guarantee Heutrix capability, native integration, availability or third-party performance.

## AI disclaimer

Heutrix AI Guardrails focuses on internal operating rules, approved use cases, information boundaries and human review for appropriate admin and workflow-support activities.

AI outputs can be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable for context. A qualified and accountable person must review any output before it is used, and AI must not replace clinical judgement or professional, legal, safety or regulatory responsibility.

## Privacy disclaimer

Personal or sensitive information should not be entered into publicly available AI tools or shared through public forms and unsecured channels.

Clients remain responsible for determining their privacy obligations and approving collection, use, disclosure, access, storage, retention and data-handling decisions.
