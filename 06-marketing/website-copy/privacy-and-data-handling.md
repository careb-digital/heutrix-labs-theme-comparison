# Privacy policy and data handling

This policy explains how Heutrix Pty Ltd (ABN 64 702 109 662; ACN 702 109 662) handles personal information from this website, business enquiries, referrals and their follow-up. Contact us at [hello@heutrix.com.au](mailto:hello@heutrix.com.au).

We ask for the least information needed to understand a workflow problem. This website is for business enquiries; project-specific arrangements must be agreed before any real client records are used.

Last updated: 12 September 2026.

## Information we ask you not to send through public forms

Do not send the following through website forms, initial email enquiries or other unsecured channels. A general description of the workflow is enough to begin a conversation.

- Patient information

- Participant information

- Client records

- Clinical notes

- Medicare details

- NDIS numbers or sensitive NDIS-related records

- Diagnostic information

- Health information

- Staff-sensitive information

- Passwords or credentials

- Confidential business records not required for initial enquiry

### Information for the introductory conversation

The initial request needs only a general description of the operational problem, roles involved, tools used at a high level and desired result. It is used to assess fit, arrange the call and provide direct follow-up.

You may provide your name, business email, organisation, role, service interest, sector, systems, workflow description, timing and preferred contact time. We also receive your permissions and any assessment summary you choose to include. Review your enquiry or referral first, then select Send Enquiry or Send Referral. Reviewing a draft does not send it. A successful submission confirms receipt of the request; a conversation time is agreed separately.

### Resource downloads

The complete public resources are available without an email address. A download is not contact permission. If you separately request an introductory conversation from the resources page, the request may record that high-level source context as part of the approved lead record.

Do not upload or send a completed workbook. Use general, synthetic or appropriately de-identified information in your working copy.

### Project controls

Any project using real client information requires an approved process for purpose, access, storage, transfer, retention, deletion, incidents and access removal. These arrangements are confirmed before real client information is accepted.

### Where information comes from

We receive information directly from you through a form, email or conversation. A referrer may provide an organisation name and a business contact’s name or email with permission. Only introduce someone or share their details if you have that permission.

You can browse and download resources without identifying yourself. To answer an enquiry or arrange a conversation, we need sufficient contact details; without them we may be unable to respond.

### Why we use the information

We use enquiry information to respond, assess whether we can help, arrange a conversation, prepare requested follow-up and manage the resulting business relationship. We also use relevant records to handle privacy requests, resolve issues and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

An enquiry or download does not subscribe you to a marketing list. The website does not sell personal information or send assessment answers to an AI service.

### Access and safeguards

Enquiry records are for authorised people handling your request and supporting the website and its communication services. We use service providers for these purposes and may disclose information where you authorise it or where the law requires it.

Website traffic uses HTTPS. No online service can guarantee complete security, so keep sensitive records, credentials and unnecessary personal details out of public enquiries.

## What happens to website information

This website is operated by Heutrix Pty Ltd, ABN 64 702 109 662. These details describe the website enquiry and resource journey.

### Sending an enquiry or referral

When you send, Formspree processes and stores your reviewed details and sends an email notification to hello@heutrix.com.au. The request includes your contact details, message, permissions and the page or resource you came from. An assessment summary is included only if you choose to share it.

Formspree processes information in the United States and other countries where it operates. It may also collect technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, to operate and protect its service. Read [Formspree’s privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/).

### Drafts and assessment answers

Unsent drafts and assessment answers stay in this page’s memory. They are not saved to browser storage or sent automatically. Reloading or closing the page clears them. Discarding a draft or successfully sending it clears that form’s browser draft; it does not delete a request already held by Formspree or email.

Only introduce an organisation or share another person’s business contact details with their permission.

### Hosting and measurement

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website and processes technical connection information, including IP addresses, as part of that service. See [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). This website does not add advertising pixels or visitor analytics scripts. Resource downloads do not subscribe you to marketing.

The website does not save form drafts or assessment answers in browser storage. Hosting and form providers may process technical information to deliver and protect their services under their own policies.

### Service providers and overseas processing

Submitted forms are held in Formspree and notifications are delivered to our business email service. Email enquiries and replies are also handled through that email service. Formspree processing includes the United States; Formspree and Cloudflare may process information in other countries where they or their service providers operate. We do not promise that website enquiry information stays in Australia.

Provider privacy policies linked on this page describe their processing. A linked external website operates under its own privacy arrangements.

### Retention and deletion

We retain enquiry records for as long as needed to respond, manage requested follow-up or an ongoing engagement, resolve issues and meet applicable legal record-keeping requirements. Our retention rule is to delete or de-identify records when they are no longer needed for these purposes. There is no automatic deletion promise when you close this website or clear a draft.

You can ask us to delete an enquiry. We will assess the request across our form and email records and explain any information that must be retained and why. Project-specific retention is agreed separately.

### Access, correction and privacy concerns

Email [hello@heutrix.com.au](mailto:hello@heutrix.com.au) with a privacy question, concern, or a request to access, correct or delete information you have sent. Include enough business context to identify the request, without sending sensitive records.

We may need to confirm your identity or authority before releasing or changing information. We will review your concern, tell you if we need more information, and explain our response and any available next steps. You may also contact the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints) about privacy rights or a complaint within its jurisdiction.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as website or information-handling arrangements change. The current version and update date will be available on this page. A new policy does not itself give permission for an unrelated use of information you have already provided.

## How we approach workflow projects

Before using live information, we clarify the purpose, minimum data required, approved tools, user access, review responsibilities and what happens to project data after handover.

- The purpose of the workflow and minimum information required

- Whether de-identified, synthetic or sample data can be used

- Who needs access and what each role can do

- Where information is stored and processed

- Which tools and vendors are approved

- Whether AI, automation or external integrations are involved

- What human review and approval steps are required

- What must be retained, archived, returned or deleted

### AI and sensitive information

Do not enter personal or sensitive information into publicly available AI tools. Any proposed AI use involving personal information needs organisation-level due diligence and an approved process.

The process should address purpose, necessity, tool suitability, access, storage, retention, permissions and accountable human review.

### Client responsibility

Each client remains responsible for determining its privacy obligations and approving collection, use, disclosure, access, storage, retention and tool-use decisions within its organisation.
