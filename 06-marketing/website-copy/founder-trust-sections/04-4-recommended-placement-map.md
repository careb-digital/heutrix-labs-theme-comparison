# 4. Recommended placement map

| Page or surface | Placement | Content | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | After the delivery method and before illustrative “before and after” examples | Full “Why Heutrix” section with four founder avatars and accountability statement | Answers “who” before the page asks visitors to rely on demonstrations or take action. |
| Home | Directly above the “See where Heutrix can help” form | Short founder-team bio plus call microcopy | Reduces uncertainty at the highest-friction conversion point. |
| About | Replace the anonymous philosophy-led opening | Founder story, four bios, accountability and delivery philosophy | Makes people and experience the organising structure of the page. |
| About | Near the final CTA | Verified company details and service area | Gives a buyer a genuine entity and contact path to assess. |
| Services overview | Between the page introduction and service list, or directly before the final CTA | Standard founder-team bio | Connects delivery capability to service choice without repeating four full bios. |
| Individual service/sector pages | Beside the first high-intent CTA | Short founder-team bio; add the actual named lead only when assignments are stable | Keeps trust next to the decision rather than buried on About. |
| Contact/booking | Above the form and in the left trust column | Founder-led “See where Heutrix can help” heading, 20-minute/no-obligation promise, agenda, outcomes, response time and assigned caller | Sets expectations before personal contact details are requested. |
| Booking confirmation | After successful scheduling or verified server receipt | Dynamic founder name, role, next step and response timing | Makes accountability concrete and avoids an anonymous handoff. |
| Footer | Legal identity line | Heutrix Pty Ltd, ABN 64 702 109 662, ACN 702 109 662 verified 8 September 2026; service area and formal notices details remain separate | Provides consistent company identity on every page. |
| Structured data/social | Organisation/person data only after the visible copy is approved | Exact legal/trading names and approved profiles | Prevents search metadata from making claims the page cannot support. |
