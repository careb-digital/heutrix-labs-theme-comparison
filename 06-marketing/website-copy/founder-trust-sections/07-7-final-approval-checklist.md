# 7. Final approval checklist

## People and permission

- [ ] All four founders approve their public name, title, avatar/portrait and biography.
- [ ] **Sach** versus **Sachin** is resolved consistently.
- [ ] Each experience statement has a dated source and named approval owner.
- [ ] Former employer/client names, sectors and locations are used only with permission.
- [ ] No resume claim has been broadened beyond the evidence.

## Delivery accountability

- [ ] “See where Heutrix can help” reviewer and caller are assigned.
- [ ] Every proposal names one accountable delivery lead and contributors.
- [ ] The lead's involvement from discovery through handover is operationally true.
- [ ] Backup/absence handling is defined.
- [ ] Scope, acceptance, ownership, training, handover and support responsibilities match contracts and delivery practice.
- [ ] The no-fit/no-build promise is understood and supported by the sales team.

## Company and contact facts

- [x] Legal entity, ABN and ACN match [official ABR evidence](../../../01-company/identity-and-registrations/REGISTRATION-DETAILS.md), checked 8 September 2026. Trading-name status and remaining business particulars stay open.
- [ ] Trading/business name use is confirmed.
- [ ] Melbourne/service-area wording is accurate.
- [ ] Email and any phone number are monitored by a named owner.
- [ ] The form, calendar, confirmation and failure path have been tested end to end.
- [ ] CTA says **Request** unless a real time can be booked and confirmed.
- [ ] Heutrix Labs/Heutrix Assurance relationship copy matches legal and proposal documents.

## Claims and evidence

- [ ] No quantified onboarding, invoicing, payroll or time-saving claim is live without calculation records and permission.
- [ ] “Average,” “per candidate” and “per pay cycle” use the correct unit and sample.
- [ ] Case evidence states its limits and does not imply guaranteed future results.
- [ ] No absolute “secure,” “privacy-safe,” “compliant” or “data integrity” guarantee is used.
- [ ] Qualifications, memberships, customers, awards, insurance and security statements are evidenced or omitted.

## Images and accessibility

- [ ] Every founder image is approved and accurately labelled.
- [ ] Avatars are clearly illustrations and do not invent authority cues.
- [ ] No stock-team image appears.
- [ ] Workspace/process images contain only synthetic or fully approved information.
- [ ] Alternative text and name/role captions are present.
- [ ] Responsive crops have been checked on mobile and desktop.

## Final copy QA

- [ ] Home, About, services, Contact/booking, confirmation and footer use the same names and responsibility language.
- [ ] All square-bracketed placeholders and publication notes are removed from public output.
- [ ] Every public factual claim maps to an approved row in the trust-facts inventory.
- [ ] Illustrative examples are labelled as demonstrations, not client results.
- [ ] Initial enquiry copy continues to prohibit personal or sensitive information.
- [ ] A founder performs a final page-by-page approval against the rendered website.
