## Start with the problem. Choose one clear next step.

Choose the operational problem that sounds most familiar. Each pathway starts with an interactive check and gives you a practical result to save or discuss with your team.

No account needed. Your answers stay in this tab's memory unless you choose to share a summary.

### I don't know what workflow to fix first.

**Primary tool:** Workflow Bottleneck Scorecard  
Assess one recurring workflow and decide whether it deserves attention. See the signals from your answers, evidence strength, feasibility and control status, with practical actions you can save without a call.

**Primary action:** Start the Scorecard  
**Optional supporting downloads:** Read the scoring guide · Compare workflows in Excel

### Our enquiry or intake process is difficult to track.

**Primary tool:** Enquiry-to-Service-Start Planner  
Assess visibility and ownership across the enquiry-to-service-start workflow. Test the normal, waiting, overdue, exception and closure paths before choosing an approved system.

**Primary action:** Open the Planner  
**Optional supporting downloads:** Read the field guide · Use the Excel tracker

### Staff are already using AI and we don't have clear rules.

**Primary tool:** AI Guardrails Check  
Assess whether sensible organisational AI guardrails are in place. Give staff a usable starting path while keeping actual tool and use-case approval with the organisation.

**Primary action:** Start the AI Check  
**Optional supporting downloads:** Read the staff guide · Use the Excel register

These tools support operational thinking. They are not tailored advice, organisational approval or an assurance of compliance. Rules, handoffs and tests are documented in `LEAD-MAGNETS.md` in the canonical `heutrix-website` repository.

## Scorecard journey — 11 September 2026

**Start the Scorecard** opens a one-workflow online assessment: 10 required questions across 3 steps (choose the workflow, understand the friction, check readiness), with an immediate result. **Download the Excel Workbook** compares up to 12 workflows. Neither requires an email address.

Rate the most recent complete cycle that reflects normal work. Staff effort is active work across roles per item or run, excluding waiting. Results show all six workload/friction signals, evidence, feasibility and control status; low ratings remain low signals. Practical actions depend on the chosen workflow and answers. Control and weak-evidence overrides remain conservative.

**Save My Action Plan** precedes the optional **Discuss My Result** conversation. Summary inclusion can be reviewed and removed on the contact form. Draft answers are retained only in memory during navigation within the active tab; refreshing or closing it clears them. Context links use only `workflow=onboarding`, `payment`, `reporting` or `action-tracking`, with an editable selection. No answers or personal information belong in links.
