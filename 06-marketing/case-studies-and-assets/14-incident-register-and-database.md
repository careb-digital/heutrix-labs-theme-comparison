# The incident register turned individual reports into a visible control

> **Anonymised Heutrix case study.** Heutrix delivered this solution for an Australian disability support provider. The organisation is not named, and no identifying source material is included.

Individual incident forms hold the detail of an event. Managers also need a controlled view of what has been reported, assessed, investigated and resolved—without opening every record to reconstruct the position.

| Starting point | Heutrix delivery | Operating effect |
|---|---|---|
| Each incident could be documented while the overall follow-up position remained hard to see | A structured register and later digital database with consistent incident and submission fields | The organisation gained a management layer across individual reports |

## The real constraint

A register can become a second, inconsistent version of the incident record if it copies too much. Capture too little, however, and it cannot show which events require attention.

The design needed a concise control layer linked to the detailed source process.

## What Heutrix delivered

The register included fields for the incident date, place, people involved, report location, witnesses, impact, reportability decision, assessment, actions, consultation, investigation outcome, communication of findings, response from the affected person, resolution and preventive measures.

The digital database used a more streamlined intake structure covering reporter details, incident date and time, location, type, severity, description, actions taken, witnesses, consent confirmation and submission metadata.

Together, the artefacts show the evolution from a wide management register toward structured digital capture.

## The operating result

The management view could support triage and follow-up without replacing the detailed incident record. Consistent fields also made it easier to filter or report on the workflow later, subject to the organisation’s access and information-handling rules.

## Approximate operating impact

- **Operational coverage:** Approximately 15 management fields spanning report details, assessment, action, investigation, communication and resolution.
- **Indicative efficiency:** Approximately 30–60 minutes less manual status collation for each management review cycle.

The efficiency range is an estimate based on the record-opening, comparison and status-reconstruction steps replaced by the delivered register view. Client feedback supports its practical use as a management control.

## Evidence and limits

The historical register and the current digital database metadata and header structure were reviewed. Client feedback supports the register and database as a practical management control, but completed incident rows were not accessed or copied. The evidence does not independently establish current policy alignment, completeness, trend accuracy or compliance.
