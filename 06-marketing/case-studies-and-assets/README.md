# Heutrix case-study collection

**Status:** Evidence-grounded editorial drafts  
**Updated:** 4 September 2026  
**Publication status:** Current 15 anonymised case studies approved by Janith on 5 September 2026.

These 15 case studies document operational solutions delivered by Heutrix across four Australian disability support providers.

Heutrix was operating as a business when this work was delivered. The fact that it had not been formally registered as a proprietary limited company does not make the work personal founder experience or change its attribution to Heutrix.

These are **not 15 separate clients or engagements**. They show distinct operational problems solved across four providers, spanning process mapping, contractor onboarding, invoicing, reconciliation reporting, incident management and shift-note administration. The source providers will remain anonymised, and no identifying source material will be published.

## Evidence reviewed

The editorial work drew on:

- the view-only support-worker onboarding and invoicing lifecycle board;
- two master Excel invoice and timesheet workbooks;
- four Power BI reconciliation report versions, including a cloud-connected version;
- 26 fortnightly timesheet snapshots and 26 payroll snapshots spanning approximately one operating year;
- a structured incident form, incident-management register and digital incident database field model;
- a structured shift-note form-response model; and
- client feedback recorded by the owner, supporting practical operation, adoption and selected approximate outcomes.

The source environments were inspected without copying real worker, client, participant, invoice, incident or shift-note records into this repository. See [Source evidence map](SOURCE-EVIDENCE-MAP.md) for the internal claim-by-claim boundary.

## Quantitative treatment

Each case study now combines two forms of quantitative detail:

- **observed operating scale**, such as the number of workflow stages, controls, report views or recurring source files in the reviewed artefacts; and
- **an approximate efficiency range** supported either by client feedback or by the manual handoffs, searches, checks or reconstruction steps addressed by the delivered solution.

Cases 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9 use approximate outcomes supported by client feedback. Other efficiency ranges are conservative structural estimates, with client feedback used where available to support practical operation. None is presented as an audited before-and-after measurement.

## Editorial standard

Each story:

- names one recognisable operational problem;
- describes the specific intervention evidenced by the source material;
- states the operating effect using observed structural counts and clearly framed approximate efficiency ranges;
- distinguishes workflow support from human judgement;
- avoids compliance, clinical, legal, payroll, tax or financial-accuracy claims; and
- links the delivered work to the relevant current Heutrix product without implying that historical scope and present offer terms are identical.

The collection uses **anonymised Heutrix delivery evidence**. Approximate ranges communicate operating impact without overstating precision or being presented as audited client outcomes.
