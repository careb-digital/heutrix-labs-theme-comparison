# Each contractor received a ready-to-use invoice workspace—not a blank spreadsheet

> **Anonymised Heutrix case study.** Heutrix delivered this solution for an Australian disability support provider. The organisation is not named, and no identifying source material is included.

A generic invoice template shifted too much setup work to the pay cycle. Every new contractor created another chance to use the wrong file, number an invoice inconsistently or leave required details incomplete.

| Starting point | Heutrix delivery | Operating effect |
|---|---|---|
| A reusable template still required interpretation at each cycle | A configured workbook and cloud folder for each contractor, prepared and checked before use | Workers started from a controlled invoice workspace with the stable details already organised |

## The real constraint

The problem was not Excel itself. It was the absence of a repeatable setup process around the workbook: assign the worker identity, allocate an invoice range, create the folder, populate stable details, name the pay-cycle tabs and verify the release copy.

When those decisions happened informally, mistakes were discovered during reconciliation rather than during setup.

## What Heutrix delivered

The solution combined a master workbook with a defined configuration workflow:

- allocate a worker identifier and invoice-number range;
- create the worker’s timesheet and invoice folder;
- copy and rename the master template using a consistent convention;
- populate approved stable details in the sample sheet;
- prepare individual pay-cycle tabs for the financial year;
- verify invoice number, invoice date, pay cycle, payee details, business details and payment information;
- check that no unintended pre-populated data remained; and
- protect the workbook before uploading the release version.

The workbook itself included an invoice overview, sample invoice sheet, pay-cycle sheets and named fields for the contractor, pay cycle, invoice totals and reimbursement components.

## The operating result

Setup became a controlled onboarding deliverable rather than a task deferred to the first invoice. Each worker received a specific file in a known location, while accounts gained a consistent structure across the contractor group.

## Approximate operating impact

- **Operational coverage:** 8 repeatable setup and release checks applied to each contractor invoice workspace.
- **Indicative efficiency:** Approximately 1–2 hours less setup and first-cycle rework per new contractor.

The efficiency range is supported by client feedback and reflects the configuration, verification and rework steps consolidated by the delivered workflow.

## Evidence and limits

The process map and two master workbook versions support the configuration steps and workbook structure described here. Client feedback supports savings of up to approximately 2 hours per contractor. The range is not an audited cycle-time study, and this case makes no claim about financial accuracy or payment outcomes.
