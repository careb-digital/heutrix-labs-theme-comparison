## Source statement

On 4 September 2026, Janith confirmed that the process documentation, invoicing solution, Power BI dashboards, incident-management forms and note-summarisation automation were solutions delivered by Heutrix for the source disability support providers. The 15 case studies span four providers. Heutrix was operating as a business at the time. Its lack of formal registration as a proprietary limited company did not make the delivery personal founder work or change its attribution to Heutrix.

This direct, dated owner decision supersedes the earlier personal-founder framing and supports attribution to Heutrix. The source providers must remain anonymised: their names, source screenshots, identifiable wording and operating records are not publication assets.

## Review method

- The Miro board was reviewed directly in view-only mode through its accessible board outline.
- The SharePoint website required authentication and did not accept the address attempted in the isolated browser session. The locally synchronised source-organisation OneDrive/SharePoint library was therefore used to review the available workbooks, Power BI packages and recurring source folders.
- Spreadsheet structure, formulas, validations, named ranges and protection features were inspected without copying live worker or invoice values.
- Power BI report packages were inspected at report-page, visual, field, entity and connection level without extracting operational records.
- Incident PDFs were text-extracted and rendered for visual verification. Completed incident records were not reviewed.
- The digital incident database and shift-note response sheet were inspected only at metadata and header level. Completed rows were not opened or copied.
- Client feedback recorded by the owner was used as supporting evidence of practical operation, adoption and selected approximate outcomes. It is treated as client-feedback support, not as an independent audit or record-level validation.
