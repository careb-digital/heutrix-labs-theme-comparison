# Pay-cycle reminders arrived with the right link and the right next action

> **Anonymised Heutrix case study.** Heutrix delivered this solution for an Australian disability support provider. The organisation is not named, and no identifying source material is included.

A generic “invoices are due” email still leaves every worker to find the correct file, remember the process and decide which cells to change. Heutrix designed the reminder as part of the workflow rather than as a separate broadcast.

| Starting point | Heutrix delivery | Operating effect |
|---|---|---|
| Submission depended on memory, inbox history and file searching | An end-of-cycle reminder linked each worker to their own controlled invoice workbook | The prompt arrived with a specific action and a known place to complete it |

## The real constraint

The recurring problem was not simply that people forgot. The path from reminder to completed invoice contained unnecessary decisions: which template, which pay-cycle tab, which fields, which submission route and what counted as done.

Automating only the email would have preserved that ambiguity.

## What Heutrix delivered

The pay-cycle design connected four components:

1. a configured invoice workbook for each worker;
2. a dedicated cloud link to that workbook;
3. an automated reminder at the end of the pay cycle; and
4. concise guidance on editable and protected fields.

Workers could open the assigned file, complete the relevant pay-cycle sheet and submit it through the defined route. The reminder supported a preconfigured process rather than asking the recipient to reconstruct it.

## The operating result

The workflow reduced the number of decisions between “invoice due” and “invoice ready for accounts”. The link, file and expected action were aligned. Administration gained a repeatable cycle trigger instead of relying entirely on individual follow-up habits.

## Approximate operating impact

- **Operational coverage:** 4 connected components: configured workbook, worker-specific link, cycle trigger and completion guidance.
- **Indicative operating result:** Approximately 60% fewer out-of-cycle payment events, reducing the associated correction and follow-up workload.

The approximate reduction is supported by client feedback and reflects the effect of connecting the reminder, worker-specific file and defined submission path.

## Evidence and limits

The process board directly documents the worker-specific links and automated end-of-cycle reminder. Client feedback supports an approximate 60% reduction in out-of-cycle payment events. The underlying automation export and delivery logs were not independently reviewed, so the percentage remains an approximate operating result rather than an audited measurement. This case does not claim guaranteed reminder delivery or complete elimination of late submissions.
