## P01 — Launch graphic

**Format:** One image.

```text
Create exactly one original 1080 × 1350 px (4:5 portrait) LinkedIn image for Heutrix, an Australian business that improves operational workflows using practical systems and safe AI where appropriate. The primary audience is decision-makers at Australian disability support providers; allied-health practices are secondary. The purpose is to introduce the workflow-first, technology-second point of view.

Use a calm corporate-modern “quiet power” design: near-white #F8FAFC background, logo navy #033862, slate #334155, border grey #E2E8F0, teal #01989C and pale teal #EFFAFA with deep teal #01647C text used only for the most important positive state. Use a bold geometric sans-serif heading similar to Plus Jakarta Sans and a clean sans-serif body similar to Inter. Use strong contrast, generous whitespace, simple line icons, 8 px card corners, 1 px borders, minimal navy-tinted shadows and 60 px safe margins. Leave a clean unlabelled area at top right for the approved logo; do not create a logo or logo-like mark.

Show five small abstract cards in the upper half: inbox, spreadsheet, shared folder, chat message and staff memory. Connect them with broken or fading slate lines. Resolve them in the lower half into one clean workflow line with three checkpoints labelled exactly “Owner”, “Status”, “Next action”. Highlight only “Next action” in pale teal with deep teal text.

Use only this exact visible copy:
“Workflow first. Technology second.”
“One workflow. Clear ownership. A practical next step.”
“Owner”
“Status”
“Next action”

Do not add other words. Do not show people, participants, patients, workers, client records, software brands, robots, AI brains, circuits, holograms, healthcare stereotypes, NDIS or government branding, testimonials, prices, statistics or outcome claims. Keep every word sharp and phone-readable.
```

**Alt text draft:** Abstract inbox, spreadsheet, folder and message cards converge into a workflow labelled Owner, Status and Next action, with the words “Workflow first. Technology second.”
