# Week 17 — Choose the next workflow

## P49 — Ten questions before choosing the next workflow project

- Publish: Week 17 Monday, 8:15 am Australia/Sydney
- Audience: disability-provider decision-makers first; allied-health decision-makers second
- Format: text
- Status: `draft_owner_review`
- Human check: adapt the opening to the current planning cycle if a timely version would be more relevant.

### Post copy

Before adding another system or improvement project to the plan, ask these questions about one workflow:

- Where does it start and end?
- Who owns the next action at every stage?
- What waits—and can anyone see it?
- What information is copied or requested twice?
- Which exception creates the most chasing?
- What changes when a key person is away?
- What requires human judgement?
- What sensitive information is involved?
- Which measure would show whether the workflow improved?
- What is the smallest useful change?

Do this with the people who perform the work, not only the people who receive the report.

The goal is not to produce a long wish list.

It is to choose one operational problem that is important, bounded and ready for a clear next decision.

Which workflow is important, bounded and ready for that attention now?

## P51 — Your AI rules should survive leave coverage

- Publish: Week 17 Friday, 8:15 am Australia/Sydney
- Audience: disability-provider decision-makers first; allied-health decision-makers second
- Format: text
- Status: `draft_owner_review`

### Post copy

An AI control that depends on one person being available is not yet an operating control.

Before holiday or leave coverage, check:

- Can staff find the current list of approved and prohibited uses?
- Is the approved tool and account clear?
- Are information boundaries written in plain language?
- Is the human reviewer named?
- Is there an authorised backup reviewer?
- Can the backup reject or escalate an output?
- Does everyone know where the approved final record belongs?
- Is there a way to pause the use case if an issue occurs?

Do not relax privacy, access or review boundaries because the usual owner is away.

If safe coverage is not possible, pause the AI-assisted step and use the approved manual process.

Resilience is part of responsible AI use.
