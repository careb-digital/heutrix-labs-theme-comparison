**Useful comment:** a comment that adds a relevant operating observation, question or example without sensitive detail. Do not count generic praise as evidence of topic fit.

**Qualified next step:** a relevant person sends a high-level workflow enquiry, makes a suitable referral or agrees a fit call through an observable, permitted path linked to the post. Count a request only when receipt can be established, and a booking only when a time is confirmed. Opening or copying an email draft, generating a resource result or clicking a CTA is not a received lead or booking.
