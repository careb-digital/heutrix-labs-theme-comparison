## Status meaning

- `draft_owner_review`: copy is strategically current but still needs the normal human review.
- `draft_asset_and_owner_review`: copy is current; the named asset and normal human review are still required.
- `draft_source_and_owner_review`: copy is current; re-open the primary source on publication day and complete human review.
- `owner_approval_required`: a specific claim, proof asset or commercial decision remains open.
- `conditional`: publish only if the stated condition is true and the copy is rewritten to match measured reality.

No status in this folder means that a post has been approved or published.

## Publishing discipline

- Make the first two lines earn attention with a recognisable operating tension.
- Teach one useful idea; do not turn every post into an advertisement.
- Never add invented clients, testimonials, results, savings, credentials or capacity claims.
- Label every synthetic or illustrative example in the first lines and on every related visual.
- Label an approved case-study post as anonymised Heutrix delivery, preserve the outcome's approximate wording and unit, and state the material evidence limitation.
- Never publish the source organisation's name, source screenshots, records or identifying wording. Client feedback is not a testimonial or an independent audit.
- Do not invite patient, participant, worker, client or other sensitive information in comments, messages or forms.
- Respond as a person and record useful audience language; do not run an automated sales sequence in comments.
- Review audience quality, saves, comments, Page actions and qualified conversations after Weeks 4, 8, 13 and 18 before extending the schedule.
