## Review each post in five passes

### 1. Truth and evidence

- [ ] Every factual claim is supported by a current source or current Heutrix decision.
- [ ] No result, saving, time reduction, client, partnership or case study has been invented.
- [ ] Any case-study post identifies anonymised Heutrix delivery, retains the 15-studies/four-provider disclosure where the collection is referenced, and preserves the approved approximate unit and limitation.
- [ ] No case-study post includes the source organisation's name, source screenshot, record, verbatim client feedback or identifying context.
- [ ] Future or retrospective wording is true on the day of publication.
- [ ] Source-led posts still match the linked source; numbers and guidance have not been superseded.

### 2. Scope and risk

- [ ] The post does not imply legal, clinical, employment, NDIS registration, audit or compliance advice.
- [ ] It does not promise productivity, savings, registration, compliance, revenue or business outcomes.
- [ ] AI remains assistive, with appropriate human review.
- [ ] The post never recommends entering identifiable or sensitive information into an unapproved public AI tool.
- [ ] The post does not invite sensitive information in comments or messages.

### 3. Commercial accuracy

- [ ] No price, starting range or pricing-led call to action appears; commercial terms are confirmed privately in writing after fit and scope are understood.
- [ ] Product name, inclusions, exclusions and CTA match the current offer.
- [ ] Capacity exists to respond to the CTA.
- [ ] The current Talk to Heutrix link works on the intended public deployment. Opening an email draft is not counted as a received enquiry or confirmed booking; the visitor must send it.
- [ ] Internal publisher notes, link instructions and conditional CTA instructions are excluded from the final caption.
- [ ] No Launch Partner Program, credit, minimum build or broader package price has been added without approval.

### 4. Voice and usefulness

- [ ] The opening sounds like a real operating problem, not a generic marketing hook.
- [ ] The post teaches one clear idea.
- [ ] Australian English is used.
- [ ] The language is calm, practical and free of AI hype.
- [ ] A founder has read the post aloud and changed anything they would not naturally say.

### 5. Production and accessibility

- [ ] The correct Page posts the content.
- [ ] Visuals use the approved logo, colour, font and layout.
- [ ] Graphics and documents have readable type and useful alt text.
- [ ] Video has captions and a clear first frame.
- [ ] Tags and @mentions are relevant, accurate and approved.
- [ ] Links include tracking parameters if measurement is configured.
- [ ] Date, time and daylight-saving setting are correct in the scheduler.

## Extra checks by status

### `draft_owner_review`

Complete the activation decisions and the normal five-pass review. No additional asset or external evidence is required.

### `draft_source_and_owner_review`

Open the linked primary source on publication day. Confirm the statistic, guidance, title, date and link. If the source has changed, revise or postpone the post.

### `draft_asset_and_owner_review`

Create the asset from the supplied brief. Do not publish an unfinished placeholder. If production is delayed, the copy may be used as a text post only when it still makes sense without references such as “swipe”.

### `conditional`

Confirm the stated condition. If it is false, replace the post; do not merely soften a false claim.

### `owner_approval_required`

Resolve the named proof, founder, commercial or operational gate. Do not schedule the post while the approval remains open.

P16 is now `draft_owner_review`; P34 is `draft_asset_and_owner_review`. Their underlying narratives already have publication approval. Check adaptation fidelity, confidentiality and product linkage in normal review; only new or stronger claims require renewed proof approval. Client feedback is not an independent audit or testimonial.
