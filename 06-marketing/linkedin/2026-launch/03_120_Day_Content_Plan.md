## Segment balance

The plan uses transferable operating principles with disability-provider language leading, then anchors dedicated sequences in each target segment:

- Disability-specific posts: P04–P06 and P22–P24, with disability-provider language leading most transferable posts.
- Allied-health-specific posts: P19–P21, P37 and P43.
- All other posts are transferable but should not default to equal-segment phrasing.

During the Week 4 and Week 8 reviews, check whether the primary audience is actually seeing, saving and responding to the material. Do not add more allied-health content merely because personal networks make it easier to reach.

## Adaptive slots

The plan is deliberately mostly evergreen. If a material, authoritative development affects disability-provider operations, privacy, workplace AI or allied-health digital operations, one post may be replaced after source and scope review. Do not publish a rapid reaction merely because a topic is trending.

Replacement test:

1. Is the source primary and current?
2. Does Heutrix have a useful operational translation?
3. Can the post stay within Heutrix's service and advice boundaries?
4. Is it more useful than the planned post?
5. Has an accountable reviewer approved it?
