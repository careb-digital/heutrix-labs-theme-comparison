# Week 16 — Make the entry offer easy to assess

## P46 — What Heutrix Diagnostics includes—and what it does not

- Publish: Week 16 Monday, 8:15 am Australia/Sydney
- Audience: disability-provider decision-makers first; allied-health decision-makers second
- Format: offer card plus text
- Status: `draft_asset_and_owner_review`
- Human check: commercial and delivery owners reconfirm scope and capacity against the [working architecture and validation gates](../../../02-market-and-offers/offers/offer-strategy-2026-09/05-owner-validation-register.md). New standard readout, format, investigation-depth and completion requirements remain proposed. Do not add a public price or starting range; model approval controls private quoting.

### Post copy

Heutrix Diagnostics is a paid, fixed-scope decision engagement.

It is for situations where the priority, workflow boundary, baseline or feasible solution is not yet clear.

**Core decision outputs, with coverage agreed in writing**

- defined problem and workflow boundary
- current-state workflow and ownership map
- agreed baseline or baseline plan
- ranked bottlenecks, risks and dependencies
- feasible options, including a no-build option
- recommended future state
- draft success measures and acceptance criteria
- next-action brief, including a first-project scope where implementation is justified
- written no-go conditions

**Not included**

- implementation or software build
- open-ended review of every process and system
- legal, privacy, clinical or medical advice
- formal audit or assurance
- NDIS registration or audit-readiness support
- guaranteed savings, productivity, compliance or business results

The purpose is to make the next decision with better evidence. The work remains useful if the organisation stops, implements internally or chooses another provider.

If one workflow, owner, users, desired result and access path are already clear enough to scope, Heutrix Workflow Transformation may be the better route. The 20-minute workflow fit call exists to assess that choice first.

Talk to Heutrix with a general, non-sensitive description of the workflow.

#HeutrixDiagnostics #FixedScope #ProcessImprovement

### Asset brief

Create a clear offer card with “Paid, fixed-scope decision engagement”, the core outputs and the line “Implementation scoped separately”. Do not add a price, Launch Partner terms or older package names.

## P47 — Who Heutrix Diagnostics fits

- Publish: Week 16 Wednesday, 8:15 am Australia/Sydney
- Audience: disability-provider decision-makers first; allied-health decision-makers second
- Format: text
- Status: `draft_owner_review`
- Human check: confirm commercial scope remains current.

### Post copy

Heutrix Diagnostics is a good fit when:

- one operational process is slow, inconsistent or hard to monitor
- the organisation needs help defining where the problem or first workflow starts and ends
- the people who know the work can join discovery
- the team wants options before committing to a build
- privacy, access and human decisions need to be considered
- leadership is willing to hear that the answer may be process change rather than new technology

It is probably not the right fit when:

- the request is for legal, clinical, audit or registration advice
- the scope is “review everything”
- a specific product has already been chosen and only implementation labour is wanted
- nobody can participate in discovery
- the expected outcome is a guaranteed saving or compliance result

A free, 20-minute workflow fit call checks these conditions at a high level. If one workflow is already clear enough to scope, direct Workflow Transformation may be the better route.

Talk to Heutrix with one general workflow problem. No sensitive records are needed for the first conversation.

## P48 — A 30-day workflow improvement experiment

- Publish: Week 16 Friday, 8:15 am Australia/Sydney
- Audience: disability-provider decision-makers first; allied-health decision-makers second
- Format: one-page template
- Status: `draft_asset_and_owner_review`

### Post copy

You can test a workflow improvement without launching a transformation program.

Try a controlled 30-day experiment.

**Week 1 — Observe**

Define the workflow boundary. Record one baseline measure. Listen to the people doing the work.

**Week 2 — Design**

Choose one change: clarify the owner, remove a step, standardise a status, create a review date or improve one handover.

**Week 3 — Test**

Use the change with a limited, appropriate group. Track exceptions and unintended effects. Keep required human decisions in place.

**Week 4 — Decide**

Compare the baseline, gather user feedback and decide whether to keep, change, expand or stop.

Do not use real sensitive information in a new tool or test environment without approved handling controls.

Small experiments work best when the scope, owner, measure and stop conditions are clear.

Save this template for the next workflow worth testing.

#ContinuousImprovement #WorkflowExperiment #OperationalDesign

### Asset brief

One accessible page with four weekly blocks and a footer containing four fields: owner, baseline, change, decision. Include a privacy and approved-data reminder.
