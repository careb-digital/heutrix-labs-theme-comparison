## P34 — Why Heutrix starts with the workflow

- Publish: Week 12 Monday, 8:15 am Australia/Sydney
- Audience: disability-provider decision-makers first; allied-health decision-makers second
- Format: single workflow-first method graphic plus text
- Status: `draft_asset_and_owner_review`

### Post copy

**Anonymised Heutrix delivery context.**

Heutrix starts with the workflow because this method has been applied in delivery—not because technology is the product.

Across four Australian disability support providers, Heutrix mapped and improved worker-onboarding, contractor-invoicing, reconciliation, incident-management and shift-note administration workflows.

The delivery used process maps, controlled workbooks, reminders, reporting, structured records and automation where appropriate. The providers remain unnamed, and the 15 case studies span four providers rather than 15 clients.

Most of this delivery maps to **Heutrix Workflow Transformation**. The shift-note summarisation work also shows why **Heutrix AI Guardrails** matters: the summarisation automation was reported to assist human review. The available evidence does not independently verify its configuration or output quality, or demonstrate that a formal AI Guardrails engagement was delivered.

The principle behind that work is practical:

Technology is useful only when it makes real work more reliable.

That means understanding the people, information, decisions, handovers and risks before recommending software, automation or AI.

We believe a good recommendation can be small.

Clarify an owner. Remove a duplicate step. Create a controlled operating view. Set a safe boundary. Automate one stable action. Build only where a tailored system is genuinely justified.

Heutrix will show delivered work only within its evidence and confidentiality boundaries. An approved case study is not a promise that a future organisation will achieve the same result.

## P35 — What happens on a 20-minute workflow fit call

- Publish: Week 12 Wednesday, 8:15 am Australia/Sydney
- Audience: disability-provider decision-makers first; allied-health decision-makers second
- Format: five-page LinkedIn explainer document
- Status: `draft_asset_and_owner_review`
- Human check: confirm call duration, lead path, CTA and follow-up owner. Add the verified public `/contact` URL to the final CTA; check the visitor-sent email path against the [current website mechanism](../../../03-sales/lead-capture/LEAD-HANDLING.md).

### Post copy

A 20-minute Heutrix workflow fit call is not a request for participant, worker, patient or confidential records.

A general description of the workflow is enough.

We will ask:

- Where does the workflow start and end?
- Who uses it?
- What makes it slow, inconsistent or hard to see?
- What systems are involved?
- Is sensitive information likely to be involved?
- What would a useful improvement look like?

Then Heutrix will assess the smallest useful next step.

That may be Heutrix Diagnostics, Heutrix Workflow Transformation, Heutrix AI Guardrails, a later review—or no Heutrix project if the problem is not a good fit.

The call is free and without obligation. It checks fit and the next decision; it does not include a workflow assessment, solution design or tailored professional advice. Any paid work has a written scope and fee before it starts.

Talk to Heutrix about one operational problem. The website opens an email draft: review and send it from your email app to request a conversation. A time is agreed separately; opening the draft does not book a call. Please keep sensitive information out of the request.

## P36 — What Heutrix does—and does not—do

- Publish: Week 12 Friday, 8:15 am Australia/Sydney
- Audience: disability-provider decision-makers first; allied-health decision-makers second
- Format: text
- Status: `draft_owner_review`
- Human check: commercial owner confirms boundaries; legal reviewer checks if available.

### Post copy

Clear boundaries make a service more useful.

**Heutrix helps with:**

- Heutrix Diagnostics when the right problem or feasible solution is not yet clear
- Heutrix Workflow Transformation for one bounded non-clinical workflow
- Heutrix AI Guardrails for practical AI-use boundaries and accountable review
- possible in-scope mechanisms such as process improvement, forms, trackers, registers, management views, appropriate automation and lightweight internal tools, subject to feasibility and written scope
- disability-provider workflows such as intake, service commencement, reporting preparation, evidence and documents, and routine handovers

**Heutrix does not provide:**

- legal, clinical or medical advice
- official audits or assurance
- NDIS registration readiness or audit-readiness services
- promises of compliance, registration or regulator approval
- clinical decision-making or diagnostic AI
- AI that removes accountable human judgement from decisions about people

AI Guardrails supports governance. It does not automatically include technical enforcement, continuous monitoring or software deployment.

We also do not recommend putting identifiable patient, participant, worker or sensitive business information into unapproved public generative AI tools.

If a problem falls outside our scope, the right answer is to say so and identify the kind of qualified support that is needed.

Fit matters more than forcing every problem into a project.
