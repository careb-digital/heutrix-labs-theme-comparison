## Deal Map

**Initiative:** turn recognisable workflow problems into qualified conversations, then a suitable written scope. Lead with a recurring operational problem, show a practical way to examine it, and offer a small next decision.

**Target outcome:** learn which workflow and buyer combination merits sustained selling effort and progress suitable opportunities towards the first properly approved paid engagement. The four-week activity targets below are operating hypotheses, not promised demand or a revenue forecast.

**Positioning:** Heutrix helps Australian disability support providers make recurring non-clinical workflows clearer and more usable. Workflow first. Technology second. Allied health is secondary; GP clinics and new sectors are outside this launch.

**Current motion:** welcomed founder introduction, permissioned relationship or inbound question → problem-specific conversation → relevant resource/proof when useful → 20-minute fit call → Diagnostics, Transformation, AI Guardrails, nurture or no fit. LinkedIn Page and founder content help buyers understand and assess the work throughout this path. Resource download and connection acceptance alone do not establish outreach permission or a qualified lead. Start public publishing with the existing Page, with founder posts in that order. Facebook remains deferred. Account access and actual composer previews remain unverified.

### Audience and problem focus

Begin with 20 organisations to research each week: 16 disability providers and up to 4 allied health practices. This 80/20 allocation is a practical test setting, not a change to approved market priority. Begin with a genuine existing professional connection or a permitted introduction where available. No named prospect or relationship has been fabricated in this pack.

Prioritise intake and enquiry-to-service-start handovers for the first two weeks. They offer a concrete opening and match the approved Visibility Starter Kit. Use reporting preparation, document follow-up or worker onboarding only where that organisation supplies a credible signal. Introduce administrative AI governance in week four or when the prospect raises it; do not force an AI pitch onto an intake problem.

A useful public signal is a provider describing multiple services or teams, a public operations vacancy, or discussion of administration and handovers. Record the public source and date. These indicate a reason to investigate, not proof of internal dysfunction, funding, budget or intent to buy. Ask the prospect whether the pattern applies.

Use roles rather than indiscriminate job-title targeting: owner/director or general manager for the decision, operations/service-delivery manager for workflow ownership, and administration/intake lead for daily use. In allied health, substitute practice owner and practice manager. Do not contact participants or patients.

### Qualification and prioritisation

Before contact, score each organisation 0–2 for sector fit, a relevant public workflow signal, likely access to an accountable role, and a genuine relationship or permitted route. Treat this as a research score, not a buyer-intent prediction. Research 6–8 scores first; put unclear entries back into research. Contact still requires a separate permission check regardless of score.

During the fit conversation, establish six things: recurring non-clinical problem; a bounded workflow; meaningful operational consequence; owner and decision-maker; plausible lawful access and staff participation; willingness and timing to consider fixed-scope paid work. Mark unknowns as unknown. A clear problem with untested feasibility is a candidate for investigation, not an integration promise. Use the [current Sales fit criteria](../../../03-sales/fit-call/process-sections/05-5-fit-no-fit-and-route-criteria.md); keep internal commercial screens out of public copy.

### The offer and conversion step

| Situation | Route | Next commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Credible problem, priority or feasible solution unclear | Heutrix Diagnostics | Privately agree a bounded paid investigation and decision output; include no build as a possible answer |
| One workflow, owner, users and outcome sufficiently clear | Heutrix Workflow Transformation | Scope the simplest useful implementation, testing and handover; this is the primary implementation offer |
| Administrative AI use lacks boundaries or review | Heutrix AI Guardrails | Establish the use, information, tool and human-review questions to scope; qualified advice where needed |
| Valid need, wrong time or missing prerequisite | Nurture | Agree one review date or trigger, with permission |
| Minor issue, professional advice or unsupported capability | Resource / no fit | Give a useful boundary and close respectfully |

These are independent entry points. A free 20-minute fit conversation is not paid Diagnostics, a technical design workshop or a guaranteed booking. Do not propose a generic free pilot. If a bounded evaluation is appropriate, agree scope, fee, staff time, safe inputs, acceptance and the decision it will enable before work begins. No duration, price, discount or delivery start is promised here.

Reduce buyer effort: ask for one workflow example described without personal details, one accountable contact and a realistic opportunity for staff review. Reuse approved existing systems where feasible. Confirm any staff testing sessions in the written scope. Use synthetic inputs until the approved project data path exists; never request source workbooks in social messages.

### Follow-up and message rules

Use the [finished message set](OUTREACH-AND-REPLIES.md). Keep direct outreach manual and problem-specific. Permission must precede a commercial electronic message. Do not send a marketing message just to ask for permission; a public email address, LinkedIn connection, resource download or third-party referral name alone is insufficient for this campaign's operating rule. Record the basis outside Git before sending. Identify the sender and Heutrix Pty Ltd, provide working contact details and a simple stop instruction. Honour stops immediately in practice. These procedures are informed by [ACMA's current guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam), checked 12 September; they do not replace Heutrix's approved outreach process.

For a permissioned first approach, make one brief follow-up after 5 business days if the original permission remains applicable. If silent, close the active approach; do not send an automated final chase or move to another channel. A person who replies can agree the next contact date. An inbound request gets a human response within the existing intended two-business-day service level once coverage is approved. Booking exists only after both parties agree a time and an actual invitation/confirmation is issued.

### Measurement and review decisions

Use Page/platform aggregate metrics and counts from the approved lead system. Do not enable website analytics, tracking pixels, remarketing, email tracking or unapproved attribution. Ask how someone found Heutrix only optionally and record their volunteered answer separately from consent.

| Measure | Four-week operating hypothesis | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Organisations researched | Up to 80 | Unique organisations with a dated source and fit note; not leads |
| Relevant first contacts | Up to 40 | Unique permissioned recipients; partners included; no minimum if permission is absent |
| Positive replies | 6 as a learning target | Explicit interest in discussing a relevant workflow or a requested resource; exclude courtesy replies |
| Fit calls held | 4 as a learning target | Attended 20-minute conversations, not form submissions or provisional times |
| Qualified next steps | 2 as a learning target | Fit criteria met sufficiently to agree a specific route and next action |
| Paid engagement | Desired outcome; no promised count | Signed approved scope and satisfied start conditions; not a verbal yes |

Compute reply rate = recipients replying / first contacts; positive reply rate = interested recipients / first contacts; held-call rate = attended calls / confirmed calls; qualified rate = qualified calls / attended calls. Use “not yet measurable” for zero denominators, count each person once in a cohort, and label small samples. Track actual staff hours so time per positive conversation and per qualified next step can be compared. Private acquisition cost and contribution require approved cost rates; do not import provisional pricing into this campaign.

Split buyer and partner cohorts before calculating these measures. The six positive replies, four held fit calls and two qualified next steps above are **end-buyer learning targets**; partner interest does not satisfy them. Buyer positive-reply rate uses buyer first contacts as its denominator. Record partner conversations → welcomed provider introductions → provider calls → qualified provider next steps separately. Keep inbound separate from outbound denominators. Count qualified opportunities by unique organisation, deduplicate referrals, and record relationship origin, delivery channel and volunteered supporting content separately in the [pipeline specification](../../../03-sales/crm-and-pipeline/PIPELINE.md#outreach-comparison-fields).

For channel comparison, use actual acquisition hours / qualified provider next steps attributed to that origin; with zero next steps report the hours and zero count, not a cost estimate. Attribute partner cultivation time to the partner route, and report shared content time separately unless an allocation rule is stated. These observational cohorts cannot establish causal channel superiority.

Review every Friday. After at least 20 properly targeted, permissioned **end-buyer** contacts and 5 business days for replies: fewer than 2 positive buyer replies means inspect relevance and message before increasing volume. Do not apply that buyer threshold to the small partner cohort. If people reply but do not attend, inspect invitation clarity and time options. If 5 held buyer fit calls yield fewer than 2 qualified next steps, inspect targeting and qualification before changing the offer. If any stage has too little evidence, extend the test and record uncertainty. These thresholds are proposed management decisions, not market benchmarks.

At 9 October, select one strongest workflow/role combination using qualified conversations and reasons. Keep disability providers primary unless the owner explicitly changes strategy. Adapt the remaining [52-post library](../../linkedin/2026-launch/README.md) to real questions. Do not pre-schedule a fabricated campaign-results post.
