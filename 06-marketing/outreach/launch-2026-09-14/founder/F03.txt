A clear workflow problem does not need to pass through a compulsory ladder of products.

At Heutrix, the starting point depends on the decision.

Unclear priority or feasibility can call for Heutrix Diagnostics, a paid, standalone decision product. A defined workflow may be ready for Heutrix Workflow Transformation, our primary implementation product. Unclear administrative AI boundaries may call for Heutrix AI Guardrails.

The free, 20-minute, no-obligation fit conversation can also lead to no project. That is a useful outcome if the problem is small, the timing is wrong or another discipline is needed. Paid work is scoped separately, and a call time is agreed after the request.
