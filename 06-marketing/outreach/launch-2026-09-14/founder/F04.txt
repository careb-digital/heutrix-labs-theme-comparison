“Can I use AI for this?” is a task-level question.

A useful answer needs more than the name of an approved product. It needs the information boundary, intended output, settings, reviewer and decision about whether that use is allowed.

At Heutrix, we treat accountable human review as part of the workflow. An unassessed idea stays in review while the open questions are resolved.

Use non-sensitive or synthetic information during assessment. Keep professional decisions with the people accountable for them.
