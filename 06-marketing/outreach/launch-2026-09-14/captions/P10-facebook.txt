Before AI supports an administrative task, make five things clear: the approved use, information boundaries, tool and settings, human reviewer, and where the final version belongs.

An approved account alone does not answer all five. Start narrow and use non-sensitive or synthetic inputs while assessing an idea.

Keep a person accountable for the final output and the decision it supports.
