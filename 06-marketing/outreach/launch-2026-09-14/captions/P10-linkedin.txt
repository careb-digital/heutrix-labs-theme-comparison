“We have an approved AI account” does not tell a staff member whether this task is safe to do.

Before AI supports a work task, answer five questions:

1. Use: what exact task is approved?
2. Information: what may—and may not—enter the tool?
3. Tool: which product, plan, settings and account are approved for that use?
4. Review: who checks the output, and what must they test?
5. Record: where does the approved final version belong?

The same tool may be suitable for drafting a generic checklist from non-sensitive instructions and unsuitable for participant, worker or other personal information.

Start narrow. Test with non-sensitive or synthetic information. Keep a person accountable for the final output and the decision it supports.

AI should assist a defined workflow—not quietly become an unreviewed step inside it.
