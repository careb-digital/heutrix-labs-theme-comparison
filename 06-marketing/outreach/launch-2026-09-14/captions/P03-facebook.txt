Does the workflow rely on someone remembering the next step?

Updates that need chasing, side spreadsheets and work that stalls when one person is away are useful signs to inspect.

Choose one recent item. Can the responsible person see its owner, status, next action and review date without asking for the backstory?

A clearer handover may be a useful first step. Keep examples general and free of personal information.
