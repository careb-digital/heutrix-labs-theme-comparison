Removing the name does not automatically remove identification risk.

In a small team or unusual situation, the remaining context may still identify a person.

The OAIC recommends avoiding personal information, particularly sensitive information, in publicly available generative AI tools as a matter of best practice.

Before an administrative AI use proceeds, check its purpose, information, approved tool and settings, access, retention and accountable reviewer.

Use non-sensitive or synthetic information while assessing an idea, and obtain qualified advice where needed.
