# Outreach and response copy

**Prepared copy, not sent messages.** Use real context only. Complete bracketed fields before use. The operator must have a valid permission basis before sending a commercial electronic message, including a message asking whether a person wants marketing. Warm acquaintance, connection acceptance, a downloaded resource or a publicly listed address alone is not this campaign's permission rule. Keep consent and suppression records in the approved system outside Git. See [ACMA guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam), checked 12 September 2026.

**15 September update:** Added M13–M19 and C09–C10 for the [recommended channel mix](CHANNEL-ANALYSIS.md). Rechecked ACMA guidance; it recognises express and inferred consent. This campaign requires a reviewed, recorded basis and does not treat a public address alone as sufficient. Keep the contact/stop route functional for at least 30 days; apply stops immediately operationally and within the statutory five-working-day limit.

**Choose one script for the actual situation:**

- Appropriate existing in-person conversation, with no electronic permission yet: M13; do not send it as a consent-request DM or email.
- Welcomed introduction or existing permission: M14 for a concise founder approach; M01/M02 for fuller context or LinkedIn.
- Confirmed operations audience and relevant public signal: M15. Owner/director: M16. Allied health: M03.
- Partner: M05 for the introduction process or M17 for a specific adviser conversation; use M05a only through an appropriate permissioned route.
- Requested resource: M06/M06a/M06b. One silent follow-up: M04. Stop or decline: M12/M11.
- Agreed telephone discussion: M18; formal fit call: use the [20-minute Sales guide](../../../03-sales/fit-call/process-sections/06-6-twenty-minute-call-guide.md). Enquiry, confirmation and recap: M07–M09.
- Partner asks about a group session: M19, conditional on capacity and actual interest.
- Objections: C01–C10. Price, scope, staff time, systems and proof responses already exist; C09 addresses savings and C10 avoids an unsolicited information bundle.

Personalise with **one verified context, one workflow and one question**. A public signal supports a question, not a diagnosis or permission. Use no more than one active opening script per recipient. Resource delivery is not permission for subsequent marketing. Business/contact placeholders are intentional and must be replaced with real facts before use.
