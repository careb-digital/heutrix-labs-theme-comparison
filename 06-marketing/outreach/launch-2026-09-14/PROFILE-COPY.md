## LinkedIn Page About

Important work becomes hard to manage when ownership, status and the next action are unclear.

Heutrix helps Australian disability support providers improve recurring non-clinical workflows. Allied health practices are a secondary focus.

We start with how work happens now: the handovers, exceptions, information boundaries and decisions that make a workflow usable. Then we assess the smallest useful improvement. That may mean a clearer process, better use of existing systems, a scoped build or no build at all.

Three independent starting points:

• Heutrix Diagnostics — clarify the problem and compare practical options.
• Heutrix Workflow Transformation — redesign, implement, test and hand over one bounded workflow.
• Heutrix AI Guardrails — establish practical administrative AI-use boundaries and accountable review.

Scope and commercial terms are agreed privately in writing. We do not provide clinical, legal, NDIS registration, audit or compliance assurance.
