## Minimum weekly measures

- suitable organisations researched;
- first contacts sent;
- replies and positive replies;
- fit calls booked, attended and qualified;
- product route recommended;
- proposals, wins and losses; and
- top objections/no-fit reasons.

Do not scrape or message people in ways that conflict with applicable laws, platform rules or the approved privacy and outreach process.

## Resource use

- Use the Workflow Bottleneck Scorecard when the prospect appears to have several competing workflow problems and no clear starting point.
- Use the Enquiry-to-Service-Start Visibility Starter Kit for intake, referral, commencement, handover, ownership or work-ageing visibility problems.
- Use the AI Guardrails Staff Starter Pack only when the prospect is considering or already using AI for administrative work.
- Link to the controlled website resource page after it is approved and live. Do not attach unsolicited files or offer a generic bundle.
- A download is not consent to continue contacting a person or to add them to a sequence or newsletter.
- Never request a completed resource or sensitive operating information through the public outreach path.

## Case-study use

- Use an anonymised case study only after its claim, confidentiality and owner-approval gates are recorded as closed.
- Choose one case that matches the prospect's workflow problem and link it to the relevant Heutrix product; do not send a generic proof bundle.
- State that the collection comes from Heutrix delivery across four unnamed Australian disability support providers. Do not imply that the 15 case studies represent 15 clients or engagements.
- Preserve approximate wording, the stated unit and the documented limitation for any outcome. Do not turn client feedback into a testimonial or a promise of the prospect's result.
- Link to an approved public case-study page or approved de-identified asset. Never attach source workbooks, screenshots, reports, records or other identifying material.
